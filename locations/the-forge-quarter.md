The East Quarter of [[High Isle]] -- "The Forges" on the city map, and the city's
industrial belt: its own island, east across the Grand Canal's southward branch
from the Merchant's Quarter. The **Foundries**, boatyards, tanneries and
metalworks crowd its yards, many of them **Dwarven-run**, their chimneys trailing
smoke over the water, and the **Forge Tower** stands over its south shore. The
quarter grinds on day and night. The **Cinder Bridge** carries its iron carts to the
Seaward Quarter wharves, and at its eastern end the **Fishmarket Bridge** crosses to
the docks of [[The Shoals]]. It
is also where the Baron's **[[The DragonBarge]]** lies at its private pier, steam
boiling off the channel where fire-magic meets the Lake's water -- the corruption
worst, and the heat most felt, in these eastern yards. (Formerly described as a
separate "Forge Isle"; it is a quarter of High Isle, and also an island of its own --
High Isle is four.)

**The lie of it.** The **Foundries** fill the island's middle, with coal and ore heaped
in open yards. The **Forge Tower** stands over the south shore, and below it is the
gated private pier where the DragonBarge lies. **Orsk & Daughter**, Hild's
goldsmithing shop, which cut the pommel mark on Santo's soul-knife, is a few streets
west of the Foundries. The **Cinder Bridge** crosses from the island's south-west
corner to the Seaward Quarter wharves. These are marked on the Forges and the Docks
sheet, `maps/forges/`.

## Taverns, amenities and the watch

- **The Anvil and Ember** -- the smiths' tavern, where the Forge Quarter decides which work it will refuse.
- **Mother Hask** -- burns-healer to the yards, the nearest help for a burned apprentice.
- **Cobb's Book** -- bookmaker taking odds on the Tourney, very short on the Baron's champion, and unhappy about it.
- **The Long Rope-Walk** -- the ropemakers' sheds, a quarter-mile of walking backwards.
- **The Sea Chest** -- sailors' lodging-house; the cheapest bed in the city with a lock on the door.
- **The Harbourmen's Office** -- the Harbor Masters' dockside office, where a berth registration is a public record.
- **The Seaward Post** (watch) -- the busiest post in the city, which has quietly stopped sending patrols anywhere near the barge pier.

Marked on the sheet in `maps/forges/`.
