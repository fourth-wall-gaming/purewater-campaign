The **seaward jaw of the Mouth**: a low, ragged island off the eastern shore at the
city's south-east corner, where the Lake's outflow finally meets the **open sea**.
The **Fishmarket Bridge** ties it to the Forges on the west; the long **Causeway
Bridge** and the lower **Tideflats Bridge** tie it to the eastern shore and the road
to [[The Vantt Estate]]. This is Purewater's rough waterfront -- fish-markets and
wharves, ship-chandlers and net-lofts, and the half-hidden **smugglers' jetties**.
The lawful traffic is governed from the **Harbour Master's Tower**, across the water
on the Seaward Quarter's longest mole, which can see the Shoals' front jetties and
not their back ones.

The Shoals face the sea and take its weather; ancestor-spirits of drowned sailors
are honoured here. The quarter draws much of its life from the neighbouring
[[The Lullwater]], across the water to the north, and the two are bound by trade both honest
and not. The [[The Dredgers]] are strong on these docks; the [[The Harbor Masters]]
officially rule them while quietly tolerating the contraband that keeps the whole
waterfront fed.

**The lie of it.** The middle of the island is open ground, **the Fishmarket**: rows of
stalls and fish-sheds, barrels, and boats hauled up on the stones. The **Fishmarket
Bridge** comes in from the Forges at the north-west corner. It is a hump of stone over
the cut, wide enough for four abreast, and at its foot is the **Fishmarket Stair**,
the busiest water-stair in the city. Marda Blackwater stands at its head when the
stairs stop, and the purge, if it comes, hangs its warning there. A second, longer
bridge crosses lower down from the Forges, and from it the **Low Canals** run out
along the south-western arm. They are plank walkways, stilt-houses and rope bridges,
and four thousand people live on them. The **smugglers' jetties** are at the far tip
of that arm. Along the south shore runs a comb of piers, with the **middle jetty**
in the centre. On the east side are the net-lofts and ice-houses: **Deller's
net-loft** by the market, and **Marn's ice-house**, where [[Crowbill]] sits in the
back with a lamp at two in the morning. Kag's weekly round, eleven calls, runs from
the ice-house to the smugglers' jetties. To the east the **Causeway Bridge** and the
**Tideflats Bridge** cross the inlet to the Eastern Causeway. Across the water to the
west, under the Forge Tower, is the private pier where the DragonBarge lies. All of
these are marked on the Shoals sheet, `maps/shoals/`.
