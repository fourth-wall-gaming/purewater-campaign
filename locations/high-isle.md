The heart of Purewater, filling the Mouth between [[Caravan Square]] on the western
shore and [[The Shoals]] off the eastern. The name is singular and the place is
not: High Isle is **four islands**, its quarters cut apart by water. The **Grand
Canal** runs west to east across the middle; a broad branch runs south from it
between the South and East Quarters; and a narrow cut divides the South Quarter
from the Seaward Quarter. **No bridge crosses the Grand Canal** -- it is crossed by
ferry -- so the city's two halves meet only by boat.

- **The North Quarter** -- the long northern island, facing the open Lake. Its
  **western half** is houses, rich toward [[The Pearl]] and middling eastward, cut by
  a small inner canal; the Lantern Bridge and the Old Span carry it out to the Pearl.
  Its **eastern half** is the official and noble quarter: the **Governor's Palazzo**
  (seat of [[Prince Emeric]]), the **Hall of Justice**, the **Barracks**, and
  **Palace Square** between them, ringed by the houses of the nobility and the
  city's wealthiest (though, in truth, few of the realm's *truly* powerful keep more
  than a token residence here). At the island's east end stands the walled
  **Tournament Ground** -- [[The Lists]] and [[The Long Butts]], the stage for the
  campaign's climax -- looking north across a narrow water to [[Temple Isle]] and
  east across open water to [[The Lullwater]].
- **The South Quarter** -- **[[The Merchant's Quarter]]**, south across the Grand
  Canal, where the Triumph Bridge brings the caravan trade ashore: counting-houses,
  the Watercrafters, the walled **Market Square** with the Guildhalls of
  Merchants, Coopers and Dyers, and the palazzi of the merchant princes who sit on
  the city council.
- **The East Quarter** -- **[[The Forge Quarter]]**, the Forges: foundries,
  tanneries, boatyards and the **Forge Tower**, trailing smoke over the seaward
  water, reaching east to the Fishmarket Bridge and [[The Shoals]].
- **The Seaward Quarter** -- the **Docks and Wharves**: a long strip on the south
  shore behind the cut, crossed to the Merchant's Quarter by the Cooper's, Dyers'
  and Tidegate Bridges and to the Forges by the Cinder Bridge. Its waterfront is a
  comb of piers and slips, with the round **Harbour Master's Tower** at the tip of
  the longest mole. (See the Forges and the Docks sheet, `maps/forges/`.)

The Baron's **[[The DragonBarge]]** lies at a private pier on the Forges' seaward
side, steam rising where its hull meets the water.
