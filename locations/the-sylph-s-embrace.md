An elegant, high-end pleasure house run by [[Constantine]], its walls thick enough to muffle the night's business. It rents rooms to women travelling alone, and [[Magda]] brings [[Gardwen]] here for beds on the night they arrive.

That night it becomes a crime scene. A nameless young patron with the di Teufel face -- [[Santo di Teufel]] -- tries to seat a lust spirit in the courtesan [[Emmeralda]] with a stolen scroll. If the working takes, he walks her down the back stair to a skiff; if it fails, he opens her the length of her body with a barbed knife and runs, leaving the knife behind the armoire. Either way he leaves the spent scroll, the chalk seat under the rug and his own coat in her room, and Magda and Gardwen see him on the stair.

## The house

A four-storey house, narrow at the street and running deep back to the water,
standing on the water's edge on Pearl Quay, between the Mother of Pearl to the south-west and the
Siren's Call at the foot of the Lantern Bridge to the north-east. Its front faces
the lamp-lit Quay street. Its back rises straight out of the basin, with no path
between the house and the water. Narrow alleys separate it from its neighbours, and
the one on the north-east side runs down to a few public steps into the basin.

- **Ground floor.** The front hall, flagstoned, where the doorkeeper sits and where
  Santo buys Emmeralda's evening (*Santo buys her evening*). Across the water side, the
  **salon** with its little dancing floor, where Emmeralda dances. **Constantine's
  office** looks onto the street. Between them are the kitchen, scullery and laundry.
  In the water-side corner, beside the salon, is the **private water-stair**, a
  covered boat-slip open to the basin with a skiff kept in it.
  This is where Santo brings his covered skiff (*Santo comes back for her*).
- **First floor.** The best rooms, and **Constantine's own chamber**, the largest,
  on the water side over the basin.
- **Second floor.** Rooms let by the night, and **Emmeralda's room**, the big room at
  the back over the basin. It has the armoire standing out from the wall in the
  corner and the big rug in the middle of the floor. The knife ends up behind the
  armoire, and the chalk seat is under the rug (*Santo's working*).
- **Attic.** The younger girls' dormitory, Ila among them; the maids' room; and the
  **top back room** over the water, cold, with a sticking shutter, where Magda and
  Gardwen lodge and the wolf sleeps by the door.

Two stairs run through the house. The grand stair goes from the hall to the second
floor. The narrow **back stair** runs from the attic to the water-stair, passing
Emmeralda's floor. It is the quiet way through the whole house, and the one Santo
runs down.
