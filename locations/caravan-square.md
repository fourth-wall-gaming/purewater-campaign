Purewater's only **land approach**. On the western shore of the landward channel,
where the Caravan Road comes in through the fields and marshes, stands **Caravan
Square**: a walled waterfront of staging-yards and warehouses, where the long
borderland road finally meets the city. Its gatehouse is the **Gate of Triumph** --
"the Land Gate" to everyone who uses it -- and from it the **Triumph Bridge** crosses
the channel onto High Isle, with a **Ferry Stage** a little to the south for the
boats. Here caravans unload, travellers and
competitors for the tourney throng in, beasts are penned and goods are staged before
everything must take to boats. It is the western shore of the Mouth, looking across
the landward channel to [[High Isle]] and the [[The Merchant's Quarter]].

It was in the crush of Caravan Square that [[Randall]] first marked the disguised
[[Baron Hanzo di Teufel]] coming into the city -- and was taken for "Lord Santo" by
the Baron's guard.

**The lie of it.** The Caravan Road comes in from the marsh and turns east into the
square between two fenced grounds. To the north is the **beast yard**: horse-lines,
pens, a well and a long trough, and bales stacked for fodder. To the south is the
**wagon park**, where caravans stand in long rows waiting to be broken down into
boats. A strip of warehouses and yards runs the length of the waterfront, with
loading doors and water-stairs onto the channel. The road ends at the **Gate of
Triumph**, the gatehouse at the head of the **Triumph Bridge**, and the bridge
crosses to the **customs house** on the Merchant's Quarter shore. The Knights stand
on that bridge in *Three of them on the gate bridge*. At the square's southern end,
a hall on a short pier is the **Ferry Stage**, for everything and everyone that
goes by water instead. These are marked on the Caravan Square sheet,
`maps/caravan/`.
