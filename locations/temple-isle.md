A small island set apart in the Lake's clean waters, **just north of the Tournament
Ground** at the North Quarter's east end, close enough that the stands look straight
across at its steps -- **deliberately unbridged** to [[High Isle]] and reached only by the
temple's own ferries, so that the profane city cannot simply walk to the holy
ground. It is given over almost entirely to the **temple of the Lake Lady** and the
**Sacred Confluence**: a great round sacred pool, fed where the purest lake-water
wells up, where divine magic runs strongest and the goddess is said to manifest at
high ceremony.

Here [[High Priestess Nerissa]] tends the waters and tracks the corruption creeping
north from the DragonBarge; here the [[The Order of the Lake Lady]] keeps its rites.

> **For the mapmaker:** unlike the dense house-by-house districts, Temple Isle should
> be rendered as **detailed, deliberate sacred architecture** -- a planned
> ground-plan of the temple precinct: the colonnaded approach, the temple itself, the
> ring of lesser shrines, and the geometry of the Confluence pool and its channels
> and basins. A formal architectural layout, not a tangle of rooftops.

**The lie of it.** The whole island is one walled precinct. The **temple of the Lake
Lady** stands at the north end. Cloister ranges run down both long sides, with small
shrines in a ring around the **Sacred Confluence**, the round pool at the centre.
The lake's channel comes in through the north wall, runs through the temple into the
pool, and leaves it through the south wall, where broad **water-steps** go down into
the lake. The Lady's blessing and the Washing are held on those steps, and they face
the Tournament Ground across a narrow water, in plain sight of the stands. These are
marked on the Lists and Temple Isle sheet, `maps/tournament/`.
