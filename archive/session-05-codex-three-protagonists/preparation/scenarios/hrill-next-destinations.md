## Greyspine foothills — nearer land expedition

reckless breach or interference. Hrill's experience can organise people and
protect a durable settlement. Possible global connection: an older water power
and a particular imperial excavation dependency; remains proposed, not canon.
Broad movement: trace empty bed, understand valley/watershed, enact a viable
water settlement. Supporting cast, mechanisms, operations and readiness remain
to be built after selection.
