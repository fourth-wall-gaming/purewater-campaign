# Thornmere shared storyline: Hrill and Nimras

Active camera: Hrill. Halla history and Quiet War operations retain their actual state. Nimras remains at his latest saved scene, not his older opening; the Midnight Exchange plan remains in its own files. Hrill road is developed in hrill-road-story.md and hrill-road-preparation.md. No cross-character knowledge or prescribed play order.

# ACT I — FIRST CUSTOMERS AND THE MIDNIGHT EXCHANGE / A ROAD PEOPLE CAN USE

**For:** Hrill: Give Hrill a practical accomplishment at a broken crossing, then a journey
through isolated communities. His calm, persuasion and judgement can get people
working together; his physical skills matter. Discover why Vaust's offer of
protection attracts support. Reach the hospice, learn the two possible routes,
and decide whether to take any continuing role. No job is accepted for him.

Parallel Nimras plan: Establish Nimras's independent business through a modest paid recovery commission. Let completed work earn contacts and later offer a separately priced, knowingly risky magical auction job. Reveal estate connections through actual discoveries, not exposition. Opening is Rennic handing him Nessa's request at the fellowship house.

**Takes:** Hrill: At risk are a stranded traveller, medicine and a usable crossing;
subsequently access and local trust. Losses require actual choices and resolution.
No compulsory rescue failure, guilt, recruitment or combat.

Parallel Nimras stakes: Initially time, agreed expenses and a prospective customer's trust. No mandatory injury, theft, ambush or accepted criminal work. Later chosen heists can put freedom, property and safety at risk.

# ACT II — THE ASHWICK PAPERS / THE PRICE OF ORDER

**For:** Hrill: Explore Wind Stair and Alder Hame, discover the difference between Vaust's
public road scheme and his coercive programme, and contest its actual dependencies.
Military knowledge can identify what the camp is preparing. Hrill can negotiate,
organise, investigate, bypass the camp or seek genuine Crown assistance. He decides
what loyalty and ending the killing require. Shared global context: imperial
restorationists need willing communities, routes and stolen spiritual heritage;
Nimras's separate venture is not a prerequisite and nobody knows his discoveries.

Parallel Nimras plan: If chosen, follow recovered evidence into the March, infiltrate surviving estate operations, recover stolen property and discover the demonological purpose and suppression of animists. Prepare live cast and operations against actual first-adventure outcomes before activating them.

**Takes:** Hrill: At risk are freedom of movement, homes, local consent and specific
people during a possible armed eviction. Threats need warnings, means and earned
information. Peaceful settlements and early antagonist defeat can end this act.

Parallel Nimras stakes: Risks come from actual informed opponents and surviving resources. No compulsory capture or loss; official help and early victory can finish the scheme.

# ACT III — THE UNBOUND DEAD / WHAT THE ROAD IS FOR

**For:** Hrill: Turn surviving discoveries and alliances into a decisive opportunity:
establish a community-controlled route, prevent a surviving eviction or intercept
the actual relic wagon. The restored route and independent communities are lasting
gains. Relic restitution is possible without undertaking a global war. A defeated
Vaust stays defeated; no mandatory working, monster, replacement boss or last fight.

Parallel Nimras plan: If still relevant and chosen, reach the Drakesfall-edge chapel, interrupt a surviving binding venture or undertake restitution and spiritual release. Nimras's spellcasting and choices are consequential alongside an independent animist ally.

**Takes:** Hrill: At risk are the particular route, people and assets still contested.
Clear victory, negotiated protection, partial recovery and withdrawal stand.
The Dragon Kings' return is an opposed project, never an inevitable consequence.

Parallel Nimras stakes: People, freedom and spiritual captivity are stakes, not prescribed losses. No reinstated Baron plot, replacement boss or compulsory ritual fight after effective prevention.
