# NPC Behaviour Model

Extend the existing character score with extras.behaviour_profile. Do not use
psychiatric diagnoses as moral labels or game mechanics. Profiles are fictional
portrayal tools, not clinical assessments. Use for allies and antagonists.

Record entitlement, empathy, remorse, manipulation, humiliation_response,
moral_limits, practical_restraints, self_control, attachment, violence_threshold,
blind_spot and evidence. Describe behaviour and conditions, not numerical
evil ratings. Kindness can coexist with exploitation; confidence does not imply
accurate perception. Record actual observations separately from GM intentions.

Before an NPC acts: what do they want, what do they actually know, which tactic
fits this profile and the counterpart, what assets allow it, and what stops it?
Link intended harm to a prepared operation, not spontaneous punishment.
Profiles neither override dice nor guarantee loyalty, redemption, violence or
escape. Earned changes and specific promises remain real. Append observed
behaviour after scenes; do not backfill invented acts as historical evidence.
