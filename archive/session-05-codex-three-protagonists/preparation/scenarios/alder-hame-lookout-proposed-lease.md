# Proposed lease: Alder Hame lookout

**Offeror:** Serevan Ashleaf, Alder Hame.
**Proposed tenant:** Sir Aldren Vaust.
**Prepared:** d12/dusk. Accepted and countersigned at d13/dusk; first rent and signed lease delivered to Serevan at d14/dusk. Boundaries confirmed. Initial term runs to d44/dusk; shelter setup is in progress.

Serevan offers the outlying rock shoulder above the dry spring shelter, beyond the low garden wall, for a small lookout post. Maelin's attached field sketch identifies the footprint and access from the ravine track. The house, garden within the wall, spring shelter and household path are excluded from the lease.

The initial term is thirty days from acceptance and payment. Rent is three gold pieces, paid before occupation. Renewal requires both parties' agreement.

At most two lookouts may occupy the post at one time. They may put up a small shelter within its marked footprint. Enlargement, permanent works or additional occupants require fresh consent.

No billeting, search, command or other authority over Serevan's household is granted. The household path remains open and free of tolls or obstruction. Access to the post is by the marked route from the ravine track, without passing through the house or enclosed garden.

Either party may end the arrangement with seven days' notice. A breach permits a request for immediate withdrawal; any contested enforcement may be put before the Crown's reeve. At the end of the lease the tenant removes his equipment and leaves the ground usable.

The returned burial standard is unconditional restitution and forms no payment, pledge or condition of this lease.

**Serevan Ashleaf:** signed as an offer.
**Sir Aldren Vaust:** countersigned d13/dusk. Cessa paid three gold to Serevan at d14/dusk and confirmed the site boundary with him and Maelin. Harl and Oda are the designated lookouts; the post has yet to be laid out.

Hrill Helmdance carries the proposal. He is not made a guarantor or signatory by carrying it.
