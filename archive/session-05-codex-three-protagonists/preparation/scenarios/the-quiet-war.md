# The Quiet War: GM Scenario

Selected 2026-10-22. Live preparation at d6/dawn; no opening scene or additional
time has elapsed. Spoilers. Use consequence-driven-design.md. The Baron chapter
is closed; no return, locket discovery or disguised Dragon Knight drives this.
Purewater geography here expands existing districts without replacing their map.

## Premise and baseline

The Marr freight concern wants exclusive carriage across the southern working
waterfront. Sella Marr bought the disused chainworks and hired Captain Oth Bren
before the Baron departed. His departure creates an opportunity; it did not
create the enterprise. Their private soldiers and tied credit threaten boat
owners and market crews. Crowbill wants to preserve his own smuggling routes
and protection income, even at the expense of independent crews.

Without effective intervention: a rival consignment is seized; independents lose
boats and credit; armed toll crews divide the canals; Crowbill's retaliation
closes escape routes during a market clearance; people can be trapped, injured
or killed; food access and livelihoods deteriorate; Marr imposes exclusive
carriage while Crowbill bargains for a smaller criminal monopoly. This is a
contingent forecast, not established history. Neri and Ward resist with real
resources. Resolve their opposition; Halla is not the only person who can act.

## Cast and resources

All new principal characters are adults. No NPC Luck. Combat statistics are
fixed preparation, never adjusted to defeat Halla. They have no unlisted rank
bonuses or supernatural powers. Use CFI and verify weapon and terrain rules at
resolution. Create any unnamed combatant's sheet before rolling for them.

Sella Marr: freight broker, concrete offers and impatient interruptions. Wants
an exclusive port concession to repay distant investors and become indispensable.
Has 120GP operating reserve, chainworks lease, two cargo lighters, one fast
launch, six civilian employees and Oth's contracted company. The chainworks
purchase and first week's retainer are already paid; future bribes, replacements
and recruitment consume the reserve. No royal authority. Knows Crowbill controls
contraband routes, Halla's public championship, not his debt or private crimes.
Will trade routes, compensate owners or surrender assets to preserve solvency.
False papers do not become valid because she can afford a clerk.

Oth Bren: veteran hired captain, terse orders and concrete soldierly judgments.
Wants a permanent armed toll command, not merely wages. Eight adult mercenaries
total besides him: four canal veterans (style65, AP2, leather2, spear/shield),
four recruits (style45, AP2, leather2, club/shield). Each person occupies one
place at a time. Opening interception uses two veterans/two recruits; four
remain at chainworks with Oth. He wears mail4 and uses sword/shield at78, AP2.
He knows the operational targets, not Crowbill's leverage. He prefers surrender
to dying for Sella's debts. Losing four soldiers or his pay gives a credible
route to defection; no automatic betrayal or free replacement company.

Neri Pell: independent boat organiser, blunt practical questions, quick laugh.
Wants her six crews to retain boats, income and freedom from both organisations.
Has two working boats, twelve adult workers (not trained assault troops), local
routes, witnesses and market trust. She will help Halla protect people but oppose
handing the waterfront to Crowbill. No automatic loyalty after one kindness.

Crowbill: preserve his dry voice, but follow purewater-tone.md: a malevolent
crime boss willing to order murders, including locals, to protect his interests.
The blanket no-killing-locals rule is superseded; selective honour does not make
him benign. Past honoured promises remain real. Wants Halla's patrol knowledge
and tolerance for his shipments. Six adult porters/boat hands and two skiffs
committed to this operation, not infinite underworld reinforcements. Will pressure
debtors, withhold favours, spread evidenced information and bargain with Marr.
He must not suddenly order a massacre to manufacture a moral dilemma.

Leverage boundary: Halla owes 4GP; paying it cancels that debt. Crowbill can
personally testify to their business, but the exact criminal parcel, witnesses
and corroboration must be audited from live events before any specific blackmail
claim. No new forged confession, retroactive murder, or link to the failed locket
theft, which Halla declined. Until verified, use favours and debt only. Disclosure
to Ward is viable; consequences depend on facts, not automatic dismissal.

Ward can deploy Jessa and four guards to a substantiated operation without
waiting for a perfect prosecution. Additional city support takes a watch to
assemble once a location and threat are identified. Private property and legal
freight are not themselves crimes. Ward acts against overt violence, protects
witnesses and can win; Halla supplies reconnaissance, leadership and choices,
not just errands. His trial status does not silently become a commission.

## Places and routes

Old Chainworks: on a southern service canal off the Forge Quarter. Public quay
and gate; rear maintenance stair reachable by skiff; roof access from neighbouring
storehouse with permission or climbing. Yard, capstan hall and freight office.
The capstan raises a local cargo boom, not all of Purewater's bridges. Counterweight
and water present visible danger; controls and alternate exits can be secured.

Lantern Float Market: a new workers' floating market on the Lullwater edge,
not Lantern Isle. Two pedestrian bridges and a boat channel join twelve trading
pontoons. Southern bank gives a third evacuation route if moorings are secured.
Neri's crews know it. Independent stalls sell ordinary necessities, not contraband.

Old Customs Cisterns: disused storage under the southern quay, with a street
stair and low-water boat entrance. Crowbill has rented access, not ownership of
every tunnel. Cargo staging area, dry gallery, isolated water chamber. Water
levels change on an openly described tidal schedule; no surprise drowning trap.

Routes: Palazzo to market one watch by ordinary boat with stops; market to
chainworks or cisterns a short canal journey within a watch. Forge workshop and
Crowbill office remain existing locations. Give the player a local sketch on
entry; use observed routes and no teleporting reinforcements.

## Operations and conditional clocks

Three focused clocks, initially 0: Marr takeover 0/4; Oth armed control 0/3;
Crowbill counter-network 0/3. Check each elapsed watch, advance only after its
named operation succeeds. The times below schedule attempts, not successes.
OOC discussion never advances the clock. Blocked operations pause or are cancelled;
replacements require time and resources. Do not count one success on two clocks.
Keep unrelated old city agendas out of this scenario unless actually contacted.

Q1 d6/day, Crowbill contact at Palazzo stair: ask for patrol timing and leniency
for a dusk boat carrying unlicensed high-value cloth through the cisterns.
Not weapons or a secretly changed cargo. Halla has not accepted. An adult
porter delivers a sealed request; this can be relayed without another named
patron. Neri independently seeks help over armed interference at the market.
First Crowbill segment only if he obtains usable patrol knowledge or protection.

Q2 d6/dusk, Marr seizure near cistern entrance: Oth's four assigned soldiers
intend to take the cloth lighter and pressure its adult crew. Sella supplied
a genuine disputed freight claim, not an order authorising force. Warning routes:
crew reports of armed inquiries, visible soldiers inspecting the landing, and
Neri's notice of the claim. Halla may guard, inspect, seize contraband lawfully,
intercept attackers or let events proceed. Success earns Marr segment1 and
captured cloth; no-intervention result depends on crew escape/resistance rolls.
Blocking the theft denies that stock and coercive example; no mandatory capture.

Q3 d7/dusk, Marr exclusivity at market: use reserve and, if acquired, captured
cloth proceeds to buy two vulnerable leases and demand exclusive carriage.
Requires solvent concern; stopping Q2 makes this dearer, not logically impossible.
Visible notices, dockworkers and sellers independently reveal the terms. Halla
can secure alternative finance, expose coercion, protect refusal or negotiate.
Success earns Marr segment2; two crews lose access if they refuse without help.
Track reserve spent, not arbitrary instant bankruptcy of every trader.

Q4 d8/day, Oth toll installation: needs at least four available soldiers and
chainworks access; occupies cargo boom and channel with toll crews. Public
construction, supply boat and Neri's routes reveal preparations. Physical
objectives: stop launch, secure capstan, escort crews through, compel withdrawal.
Success earns Oth segment1. Segment2 requires actually repelling a coordinated
challenge or obtaining compliance next watch; it never follows automatically.

Q5 d8/night, Crowbill closes the side routes: needs his boats and four porters;
plans to lock cistern access and divert independent loads until crews accept
his protection. Warning: withdrawn boat services, changed locks, named crew
reports. Halla can confront him, protect a convoy, open the routes or negotiate.
Success earns Crowbill segment2 only after segment1 or an independently paid
alternative contact network costing a watch. Refusing Halla's favour therefore
matters but does not make Crowbill helpless. Third segment requires a real
agreement giving him exclusive illicit carriage; no automatic alliance with Marr.

Q6 d10/dawn, market clearance: Oth attempts to expel remaining independent stalls
if armed toll control survived and he retains four soldiers. Sella permits
eviction but wants intact commerce. At least one full watch of visible warning
must reach affected crews: notice, assembly or runner. If nobody intervenes,
crews may resist, flee or bargain; resolve those uncertain outcomes. Injury,
loss of homes and deaths are possible, not mandatory. Crowbill's blocked routes
make evacuation harder if still blocked. Saving people, opening alternate exits,
breaking Oth's force, winning over Sella or official intervention can avert it.
Success earns Oth segment3 and Marr segment3 only for the separate completed
objectives of clearance and actual transfer of lease/control respectively.

Q7 d12/dusk, exclusive-port settlement at chainworks: requires Marr segment3,
solvency and operating transport; Sella seeks signed exclusive contracts.
No royal concession by fiat. Elders and owners can refuse if safe alternatives
exist. Advance Marr4 only on actual acceptance. Threats backed by defeated
soldiers fail. Halla may raid a surviving armed headquarters, protect a free
convoy, negotiate terms, expose offences or broker an independent agreement.
Early destruction of the scheme can preempt this meeting entirely.

## Information and opposition

Each warning has a physical source and a person-route; important news is not
locked behind a single Streetwise roll. Use the campaign's track-who-knows
mechanism if available; otherwise engine Streetwise/opposed checks with named
sources, difficulty and existing contacts, then persist knowledge edges. Secret
preparations require discovery; public intimidation is plainly observable.
Offscreen successful plans can harm people, even while Halla pursues something
else. Announce discoverable consequences without exposing secret intentions.

## Three-act structure and outcomes

I, The Favour (d6-7 baseline): put Halla's new authority against Crowbill's
expectation; save or lose people/cargo and identify the new concern. Enter II
when an intervention or public seizure exposes the wider threat, not because
Halla must accept a job. Neri's appeal provides an independent entrance.

II, The Waterfront Divides (d8-10 baseline): choose operations against transport,
money, armed control and closed routes. Useful discoveries enable attacks on
specific capacities. A captured office record helps find a launch or paid crew;
it does not replace physical action with six interviews. Enter III when one
side can attempt control or Halla has assembled a decisive counteroperation.

III, Who Holds the Water (d10-12 baseline): resolve the surviving operations,
protect people and decide control. Confrontations use the surviving cast and
resources; no forced duel, escape or resurrection. Earlier success can finish
the campaign sooner. Dates are provisional NPC plans, not act gates.

Clear victory: armed monopoly broken, independent routes restored, vulnerable
crews protected, criminal capacities removed; allies keep their gains. Halla can
earn strong references, official recognition and agreed salvage/bounty if offered
beforehand; no unpromised automatic commission or debt forgiveness.
Partial victory: market saved but one network retains illicit control, or enemy
defeated after some displacement; reconstruction and remaining dangers are named.
Negotiation: workable shared access, restitution and enforceable limits, with
actual parties agreeing and retained force tracked; no coerced moral equivalence.
Defeat/withdrawal: rival control and displaced crews persist where achieved;
no automatic loss of Halla's sister or job. A later resistance starts from real
changed assets, never a reset. Public Crown service is not plot armour.

## Stress tests

Pay Crowbill: debt ends; business conflict remains. Tell Ward: gain lawful
force, retain choices about targets and protection. Defeat Oth early: cancel
unsupported violent operations; Sella must bargain or spend/time to replace him.
Arrest Sella: seize actual criminal assets; independently motivated Oth may
withdraw, surrender or pursue his toll scheme with only his remaining people.
Refuse both patrons: Neri/visible harm permit independent involvement; other
actors still act. Negotiate successfully: honour the settlement and shorten play.
Protect every warned target: allow it if allies, timing and resources make it
possible. No compulsory sacrifice to manufacture narrative heft.

## Activation and readiness

Persist three principal NPCs, three locations, three agendas, seven conditional
operations, initial knowledge and current arc. Keep world at d6/dawn; Halla's
resources unchanged. No new favour accepted, crime committed or civilian harmed
by preparation. Enemy intentions belong to GM notes until Halla discovers them.
