## Global storyline and truth boundaries — GM only

Local human adherents are preparing people and material for a restoration of
draconic dominion. Their ideal requires human rulers and subjects to submit to
inhuman masters. The open global question is whether humanity can recognise and
resist the return of that old relationship before it is made effective again.
This adventure exposes and can defeat one finite local recruiting cell. It does
not establish a living Dragon King, a universal conspiracy, or Hanzo's command
over this cell. Other protagonists retain their independent stories and clocks.

Avel independently financed and chose Greywatch. Her sale to Dane remains an
honest purchase, not payment for an attack. She is not magically compelled and
Vane did not command or finance the attack. He did, however, recruit her into the
Unbroken Crown, promise her permanent command and encourage a veteran to establish
a first imperial foothold. Avel chose the target, timing, money and followers.
Her responsibility and Vane's exploitation are both real.

Avel's inherited mail, signet and household chronicle are authentic imperial
propaganda. “Peace through obedience” and “returning reed households” disguise
the recapture of refugees and forced levies as honourable family service. The
signet's kneeling human retains a worn collar-chain. The family chronicle's
illustration can be compared with Selith's independent account of the reed escape
route. An inner-collar formula, “the crowned breath enters the worthy captain,”
is a historical possession dedication but not an active spell. These objects
taught Avel a corrupt history which Vane deliberately used. The full causal dossier and evidence routes are in
[hrill-unbroken-crown-operations.md](hrill-unbroken-crown-operations.md).

Vane is a lay provincial organizer of the established Awake the Dragon Movement.
Cassian Bree selected him through a private Ember Society reading and gave him
only the political and logistical layer of the programme. Vane knows literal
restoration and funerary material matter; he does not know the possession working,
a caster, a spirit, an intended host or a current consignment. His Reedgate plan
would create respectable cover for future movement of anchors and a roster of
veterans for “higher service.” This connects the adventure to the demonological
wing without changing the separately prepared Midnight Exchange operations or
making Avel magically compelled. Hanzo and Vane share a compartmented movement,
not an established command relationship.

The Unbroken Crown is a four-person cell led by Ser Caldus Vane. Its internal
oath renounces human kings, promises obedience to the Dragon Kings and accepts
the subjection of other humans. Vane conceals that full oath behind veteran
fellowship and promises of order. He uses intimidation and, when needed,
abduction to keep members and witnesses silent. A public recruitment invitation,
the full oath sheet and an intercepted instruction are separate evidence routes.
The blood used to sign the oath is genuinely used in its ritual; this cell has
no working compulsion spell, caster or confirmed contact with a living king.
Magical influence remains unproved in play. Do not invent a spell result or
mind-control explanation when challenged; any later supernatural development
requires actual preparation and discoveries.

Dane sympathises with imperial restoration and knows Vane as an actual arms
customer and host of private veteran meetings. He has heard talk of the brothers
of the oath and knows the Reedgate Tollhouse meeting place and next invitation
for d43/dusk. He has not attended the full initiation, seen the internal oath,
or learned the plan against Mara. His receipt packet contains Vane's old specific
introduction of Avel and her ancestral objects; Dane treated it as a respectable
commercial referral and did not know her Greywatch plan. His legitimate salvage
can proceed independently.
He can recognise credible evidence of coercion and reconsider a sale. Do not
make either his betrayal or his redemption automatic. Asking for customers'
names becomes a substantive inquiry into sworn armed groups, rather than a
pretext to arrest a merchant or seize his discovery.

## OOC review — credible refusal, independent inquiry
The player observes that people will not simply tell Hrill who belongs to a
secret organisation. Preserve credible confidentiality and resistance. Do not
turn an Influence success into magical truth-telling or assume every customer
is a sworn member. A refusal must not force a loop of repeated polite requests,
arbitrary arrest, salvage seizure or a stalled investigation. Existing
invitation/oath/witness/activity routes are operational alternatives; reveal
them through actual questioning, access, observation and discovery. A bounded
introduction or an explanation of what an oath requires may be less sensitive
than handing over a membership list, but cooperation is earned, not guaranteed.
The first post-review exchange should reach an actionable lead or a clear
physical avenue within one substantive conversation. Do not require the player
to guess an unspoken magic phrase. This note grants no PC knowledge and holds
the clock at d41/day280.

## Spoiler design assessment — rebuilt, review remains held

The dramatic chain is now personal and operational. Avel's corrupt inherited
history led her toward Vane; Vane promised the permanent knightly command she
craved; she independently made Greywatch the first attempted foothold. The same
propaganda that calls recaptured refugees “restored households” makes the global
theme concrete: will human knights protect people or revive the old language of
human subjection? Hrill can recognise the lie through Selith's refugee history,
his loyalty to the Crown and his own weariness with violent lords.

Vane does not wait for Hrill. He watches and threatens the refusing witness,
recruits two named veterans, initiates actual volunteers, resupplies, attempts to
steal a named courier's credentials, forges a limited appointment and tries to
hold Reedgate with whatever force survives. If that succeeds, he attempts a
physical coded report to Cassian; interception can expose the wider Movement
without conjuring demonologists into the local fight. His reaction matrix says how he can
learn of interference and what he can still do. The threat is finite and dangerous;
watch protection, exposure, lost operatives, surrender or early capture can
actually end it. No confirmed living Dragon King or magical return mechanism is
invented. The local victory remains meaningful within the global open question.
