# Glass Hounds: live arc for Nimras

Selected by the player. Full GM preparation: glass-hounds-situations-v2.md, with creature rules and physical Kiln map in glass-hounds-gm.md, also stored as GM lore in TypeDB. Existing Purewater, Halla's history and the d6/night shared clock stand. Dates are conditional operation windows, never forced act transitions. The Takes field records stakes at risk, never mandatory loss.


## Resolved live position — d7/day

Nimras completed the commission and returned home outside Purewater with willing Rennic. The fellowship received its manuscript and paid the agreed 8GP. Nimras has 13GP4SP and no unspent experience rolls (Arcane Casting 71%, Stealth 57% after spending both awards). Conall and the City Watch hold the surrendered Kiln company and control instruments; Senn's sale/interference agendas are thwarted and their downstream hunt, handover, export and payment beats cancelled. No final buyer confrontation is required. Buyer arrest, convictions and the eventual disposition of the research and constructs remain unplayed. The adventure is closed; the acts below preserve the preparation history and do not schedule further compulsory play.

# ACT I - `d6 to d7` · THE HUNT

**For:** Introduce a freelance mage hunter arriving in Purewater. Contact Vey, investigate Rennic’s old lodging and contacts, navigate unfamiliar districts and discover competing hunters. A1–A4 offer searches, surveillance, dangerous encounters and a reachable teacher. Earn the glass-hound reveal through pursuit; do not start beside Rennic.

**Takes:** At risk are the teacher's freedom, the manuscript and people in the hunt's path. No injury, capture or escape is predetermined.

Move on when players gain a substantial asset, settle their commission or pursue the hunt's source. Early victory can end this act or campaign.

# ACT II - `d7 to d8` · THE PACK

**For:** Expose Senn’s coercive capture business and the buyer’s demand for an unwilling living demonstration. B1–B3 offer a hound hunt, maker recovery, workshop infiltration and evidence of the deal. Threats arise from actual enemy knowledge and resources. Allies and city powers can act on earned evidence.

**Takes:** At risk are control of the constructs, custody of useful research and the ability to prevent coercive export. No compulsory betrayal or compensating defeat.

Investigate, raid, disguise, bargain or recover. Transition follows changed control or a decisive plan, not the printed day.

# ACT III - `d8 to d10` · WHO KEEPS THE HOUNDS

**For:** C1–C2 contest loading, an actual captive if one was taken, and Varro’s conditional payment. Settle surviving transport, research, teacher and manuscript. No captive, demonstration, export or payment succeeds by date alone. Early recovery or decisive defeat stands; recalculate the finale from surviving means.

**Takes:** At risk are lasting availability of mage-hunting tools and professional relationships. Clear victory, partial victory, negotiated settlement, failure and withdrawal have distinct concrete results in the GM plan.

No mandatory final duel, sacrifice, seventh hound or returning hidden antagonist. Finish when the conflict actually resolves.
