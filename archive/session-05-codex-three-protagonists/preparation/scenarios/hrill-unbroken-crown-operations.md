# Hrill — The Unbroken Crown: causal dossier and operations

This is the authoritative GM plan at the OOC hold on Hrill d41/day280. Nothing
scheduled here has happened merely because it is written. Every operation is an
attempt requiring its stated actors, information, time and resources. Actual
losses, protection, surrender and official action remove later capacity.

## The causal story: Avel, the relics and Vane

Dame Avel Veyr inherited three genuine Dragon-Empire objects and a family story
that misdescribed what they meant.

* Her ceremonial mail has a silver collar inscription: **PEACE THROUGH
  OBEDIENCE**. A smaller dedication praises her ancestor for “returning the reed
  households to their appointed lord.” The family story calls this the ending of
  a feud. In context it commemorates the recapture of people fleeing imperial
  forced service. Beneath the inner collar, an older formula reads **THE CROWNED
  BREATH ENTERS THE WORTHY CAPTAIN**. It is a historical possession dedication,
  not an active spell or usable ritual.
* The dragon signet depicts a winged crown above a kneeling human. Its worn lower
  edge still shows a collar chain running from the kneeling figure. Wax impressions
  hide that detail unless the original seal is cleaned and examined.
* A household chronicle calls refugees “strayed dependants,” forced levies
  “restored households,” and executions “the quieting of disorder.” An illuminated
  scene shows bound people beneath the same red route through the reeds that
  Selith independently described as an escape road from a Dragon fort.

These objects are propaganda, not enchanted mind control. Elen Veyr taught the
honourable family version. Avel, after years of insecure mercenary work, read the
promise of rank and permanent command into it. The humiliations and uncertainty
of hired service made “order” emotionally persuasive; they did not remove her
responsibility.

Vane learned of the Veyr relics through Merrit Dane's trade correspondence. He
wrote to Avel as a fellow knight who understood “a house that remembered its
first duty.” He showed her a translated Unbroken Crown oath whose worst clause
was glossed as guardianship until the private initiation. He promised that the
first veteran to hold a Crown beacon in the old name would command it when the
Dragon Kings returned. Avel signed a preliminary pledge at Reedgate. Vane kept
the counterpart. Avel retained his letter and annotated a family chronicle:
“Greywatch can be the first light. Mine to hold, not another winter's hire.”

The inner collar gave Avel an apparently physical reason to believe that the old
kings could return through chosen captains. Vane claimed the words described a
future honour awaiting loyal veterans. He did not know how to perform the old
working. The modern demonologists deliberately let him substitute faith for the
missing method.

Vane encouraged a demonstrative foothold but did not order, fund or command the
Greywatch attack. Avel selected Greywatch, sold her own inherited armour, spent
her own sixty gold and recruited her own followers. Her statement that she acted
alone is true about the operation and false only if understood to mean that no
one recruited or encouraged her. She is neither possessed nor absolved.

The connection is discoverable without interviewing Avel:

1. Dane's receipt packet includes Vane's earlier introduction line asking Dane
   to send “the Veyr lady and her ancestral proofs” to Reedgate. It proves a
   specific introduction, not Dane's knowledge of the attack.
2. Close examination of the mail, signet and chronicle reveals the propaganda
   and contradicts the benign family account. Selith's refugee route supplies an
   independent historical comparison already known to Hrill.
3. Corl Hen heard Avel use Vane's phrase “first light” and saw her burn one
   Reedgate envelope before Greywatch. He knows no cell structure.
4. Vane's locked box holds the counterpart preliminary pledge, his promise of
   command and a draft disavowal to use if Avel failed.
5. Avel's supervised request for correspondence, if made and approved, names
   Vane and creates a present evidence route. It is a future attempt, not current
   Hrill knowledge.

## Connection to Awake the Dragon's demonological programme

Vane is a sworn lay organizer of the Awake the Dragon Movement. He was selected
through a private Ember Society reading by its secretary, Cassian Bree, then used
as a provincial military recruiter. Cassian's surviving introduction calls the
inner-collar words “a true remnant of the captain-making art.” That is accurate
as historical identification but withholds every practical dependency.

Vane knows that an inner circle seeks literal restoration and that old funerary
material matters. He does not know the possession procedure, the identity of a
bound spirit, a caster, an intended host or a current working. He has no spells.
His blood-signed oaths are loyalty records and recruiting theatre, not magical
links. Avel belongs to Vane's military auxiliary; she was never taught the
demonology and received no ritual power.

The inner programme wants Reedgate for three mundane reasons: a controlled road
between the March and the lake routes, respectable papers for moving future
funerary consignments, and a pool of disciplined veterans who might later be
approached for “higher service.” No particular consignment, spirit or host is
currently scheduled through Reedgate. This preserves all separately prepared
Midnight Exchange dependencies and outcomes.

Vane's locked box contains Cassian's Ember-watermarked introduction and one coded
instruction: establish a lawful face at Reedgate, keep a roster of suitable
veterans, and report when the road can receive “the old campaign dead.” Cassian's
Purewater correspondence file retains the counterpart. Either copy proves an
organizational connection; neither supplies a working ritual or proves Hanzo
commanded Vane. Hanzo and Vane serve the same wider movement through different
compartments and may know nothing of one another's operations.

## Avel's current intention in custody

Avel remains lawfully held at Hythe pending final judgement. At d42/dawn she
intends to request permission to send a supervised letter to Vane, asking him to
provide an advocate and honour his promise of wages to the Greywatch followers.
She will offer five silver from her own twenty-four gold for a lawful courier if
the request is approved. The money is not spent during preparation. The warden
can read and copy the letter. Avel will not attempt an implausible escape.

If Vane's draft disavowal or refusal to aid her followers is shown to her, she
must decide in play whether loyalty to her people outweighs her oath. That can
produce testimony or continued defiance; redemption is not automatic. If Vane
tries to recover correspondence, that is evidence of his choices, not proof that
Avel lacked agency.

## Vane's strategic objective and finite means

Vane aims to turn Reedgate into a small armed enclave that publicly claims
authority from the Dragon Kings, collects tolls, recruits veterans and prepares
a transport screen for Awake the Dragon's inner programme. His four milestones
are: obtain at least one new voluntary sworn recruit; control genuine Crown route
credentials; produce a forged order; staff an openly hostile checkpoint.
Suppressing Mara protects this plan but does not itself advance a milestone.

At d41/day280 he has Vane, Harl, Sella and Othin; 45GP including 12GP already
reserved for Harl and Sella through d43; ten person-days of food; their listed
arms; the tollhouse; ink, paper, wax and Othin's ordinary forgery tools. He has
no horses, cart, magical communication, reinforcements, quarry weapons or living
Dragon King. Planned allocations are 12GP to retain Harl and Sella through d46,
12GP maximum for two recruits, 3GP for thirty person-days of food and 2GP maximum
for information or a bounded bribe. A successful post-checkpoint report costs5SP
for a verified southbound carrier. These are reservations, not money already
spent. If all are paid,15GP5SP remains.

## Executable unopposed operations

### U0 — Watch the witness and the Crown post

Actor/aim: Othin observes Mara's livery and the Ashwick post to learn whether the
oath copy is protected. Place/timing: leave Reedgate d42/dawn180, walk sixty
minutes, observe thirty, return sixty; earliest report to Vane d42/day0. Assets:
Othin alone, ordinary clothes, no spending. Prerequisite: Othin free and Vane not
already warned by a faster actual source. Warning/discovery: livery staff or watch
can notice the same ink-stained man loitering; following him leads toward Reedgate.
Resolution: Stealth/Deceit against actual observers if challenged. Success reports
only what he sees. Failure can expose, delay or capture him. If he does not return,
Vane knows only that he missed the rendezvous after d42/day90, not why or where.

### U1 — Recover Mara and the oath sheet

Actors/aim: Othin, Harl and Sella seek Mara alive and her sheet. Prerequisites:
the trio free; Othin's actual route information not disproved by U0; target on the
ordinary run. Place/timing: west causeway, depart d42/dawn270, ninety-minute walk,
expected target d42/day60, wait no more than thirty minutes, ninety-minute return.
Assets/cost: three people, rope already held at the hall, Sella's eighteen arrows;
no new money. Warning: visible travel, hedge concealment, Othin's written recovery
instruction, U0 surveillance and sixty feet of open approach. Resolution: request
papers first, then Influence, grapple or combat under native rules. Success can
control Mara and/or the sheet; partial success may gain only the paper or identify
the cell; failure loses time or people. Absence, changed route or strong protection
ends the attempt. No one teleports to a new target. A captured or defeated pair
cancels it. Reports require a survivor's physical return.

### U2 — Recruit Tamsin Hale and Ruan Cale

Actors/aim: Vane and Othin invite two named unemployed veterans to paid tollhouse
guard duty, then seek voluntary preliminary pledges. Place/timing: separate public
meetings at Reedgate d42/dusk, about one hour each. Prerequisites: Vane and Othin
free; at least 2GP advances available; no public proof making the invitation
implausible. Assets/cost: offer each 1GP advance and 5GP after accepting the full
oath and three days' service; maximum 12GP. Tamsin and Ruan arrive with their own
listed equipment. Warning/discovery: written invitations, their departures and
the public promise of guard pay. Resolution: Vane's Influence against each
veteran's Willpower only when genuine persuasion is uncertain; no success creates
mind control. They can refuse, leave, ask questions or disclose the offer. A
voluntary signed pledge earns one agenda milestone, regardless of head count.

### U3 — Initiate actual attendees

Actors/aim: Vane administers the full oath to any actual willing attendee; Harl
and Sella guard if present and free; Othin records names. Place/timing: Reedgate
d43/dusk, two hours. Prerequisites: at least one actual attendee, Vane free, the
hall usable. A captive Mara does not count as a recruit. Assets: oath sheets,
knife for a blood signature, no spell. Warning/discovery: the d43 invitation,
Mara's copy, hall papers and activity visible through the front windows. Resolution:
attendees hear the full plain-language renunciation before deciding. Refusal does
not create a follower. Vane may try to stop a refuser carrying the sheet only if
actual guards are available; detention is an opposed social/physical action.
Success supplies only the people who truly accept. Exposure, arrest or no attendees
cancels the operation.

### U4 — Buy food and retain the core

Actor/aim: Othin buys thirty person-days of ordinary food in Ashwick and Vane
offers Harl/Sella 1GP each per day for d44–46. Place/timing: d44/day, four-hour
round trip including market work. Prerequisites: Othin free, 3GP food money and
12GP wage reserve still available. Warning/discovery: a conspicuous bulk purchase
for Reedgate and the named buyer. Resolution: ordinary purchase if stock exists;
otherwise actual price/availability governs. Loss of money, merchant refusal or
Othin's capture leaves the cell on its remaining food. Unpaid followers choose
whether to stay; loyalty is not free.

### U5 — Intercept Crown courier Edra Morn

Actor/aim: Vane, Harl and Sella ambush Edra to take her sealed route wallet and
appointment orders. Othin can identify the expected road only if he actually sees
or learns her departure/route. Edra leaves Hythe d42/dawn, walks three stages and
reaches Ashwick about d44/dusk; she leaves north d45/day0 and reaches Reedgate road
about d45/day60. Prerequisites: a credible route report reaches Vane; at least two
combat-capable cell members free; Edra actually travels that road; no effective
escort or altered route. Place: Reedgate road bend, with ditch and farm footpath.
Duration: up to thirty minutes waiting and actual encounter. Assets: only listed
weapons; Edra carries 2GP, three food-days, sealed route token and specific orders,
not a blank royal seal. Warning/discovery: missing courier, Othin's observation,
cell movement and the ambush position. Resolution: Perception, warning, pursuit,
social demand and combat by native rules. Edra can escape, surrender the wallet,
be rescued or defeat an attacker. Vane orders capture and theft; lethal force only
if armed resistance threatens the cell. Success earns the credential milestone;
failure or protection removes the forgery route. No credentials appear by deadline.

### U6 — Forge the Reedgate appointment

Actor/aim: Othin turns intact stolen material into an order naming Vane custodian
of Reedgate and authorising toll collection. Place/timing: tollhouse, earliest
d45/day after U5, three uninterrupted hours. Prerequisites: Othin free, actual
intact wallet/token/order, tools and samples. Assets/cost: ink, paper and wax
already held; three hours. Warning/discovery: practice scraps in the hearth and
comparison with Edra's real order. Resolution: Othin Forgery60 once when attempted;
failure produces detectable defects and consumes one usable sheet but does not
create authority. Success earns the forged-order milestone, not automatic belief.

### U7 — Establish the hostile Reedgate checkpoint

Actors/aim: Vane uses every free willing follower to bar the road, show the forged
order and collect tolls while declaring the Unbroken Crown's authority. Place/
timing: Reedgate, earliest d46/dawn, two hours to set trestles and barriers. Required:
Vane plus two other free combatants, food for the day, and either a forged order
or an explicit decision to operate as obvious outlaws. Assets: hall furniture,
listed weapons and remaining cash; no new palisade or troops. Warning/discovery:
roadside construction, challenged travellers, visible dragon sign and missing
courier. Resolution: travellers and Crown officers assess the document and choose
compliance, retreat or resistance; all contests use actual skills. Success means
the cell physically holds one checkpoint and earns the fourth milestone. It does
not confer lawful authority or summon a Dragon King. Arrest, surrender, inability
to staff the road, lost credentials or lack of food cancels or weakens the attempt.

### U8 — Report an open route to Cassian Bree

Actors/aim: after U7 actually succeeds, Vane dictates and Othin copies a coded
report stating that Reedgate can receive funerary consignments and naming veterans
who voluntarily accepted higher service. Place/timing: tollhouse, trigger only
after all four strategic milestones; one hour writing, then the next verified
southbound carrier. Assets/cost: one sheet, ink, Vane's personal seal and5SP from
the same remaining pool. Prerequisites: Vane/Othin free, checkpoint still held,
an actual carrier identified and paid. Warning/discovery: the draft, Ember
watermark, carrier inquiry and physical letter. Resolution: no automatic delivery;
the carrier can refuse, be warned, searched, delayed or complete the real journey.
If Cassian eventually receives it, he can forward news through existing Movement
correspondence but gains no instant troops, ritual components or knowledge of
Hrill. Interception gives proof of the wider link and leaves Cassian unaware.

## Readiness and update rule

The plan has named targets, finite money, food, people and travel time; separate
warnings; independent evidence routes; physical intervention points; and clear
failure and cancellation branches. Hrill can use honour, Crown loyalty, social
skill, strategy, tracking, archery and close combat. Official help can end the
cell early. The Awake the Dragon connection gives the local operation a real
global purpose while keeping its compartments honest: Vane prepares human
submission and a transport corridor, but possesses no magical mechanism for a
return. Capturing his correspondence exposes a route toward the demonological
programme; defeating him still ends this finite local threat.

After every elapsed watch in play, reconcile what each actor actually knows,
which people and resources remain, and which operations are now impossible.
Never fire a scheduled result merely because its date arrives.
