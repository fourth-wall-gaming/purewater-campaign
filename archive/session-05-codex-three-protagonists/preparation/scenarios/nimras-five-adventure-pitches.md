# Five adventure proposals for Nimras

These replace the rejected survey-adventure proposal. They are not yet established events. All keep Nimras's approved character, Purewater, the post-tournament period, his outside commission and his relationship with Rennic.

## A. Glass Hounds — mage pursuit and counter-magic

Rennic's trail leads into a hunt already crossing Purewater's roofs. Beasts of worked glass are tracking spellcasters, rival hunters are closing on valuable research, and the manuscript you were hired to recover matters to both sides. Your former teacher is one moving target among several people with dangerous reasons to reach him first. Expect pursuit, magical counters, street contacts and battles you can choose how to approach.

Movement: intercept a hunt → attack or bargain over its machinery → decide who leaves with the quarry and the research.

## B. The Drowned Observatory — expedition and contested discovery

Rennic has joined an expedition to a submerged observatory off Purewater. Rival scholars, salvage investors and merfolk have incompatible claims on what lies inside. Recovering your teacher may mean entering the ruin, intercepting its expedition or finding another way through the older waterfolk routes. Expect exploration, uncanny chambers, dangerous magic and competitors who can become temporary allies.

Movement: reach the observatory → explore and contest its discoveries → secure an extraction, agreement or closure.

## C. The Red Crossing — battle magic and a private war

Your pursuit reaches an outer-island crossing as rival wizard companies fight over the stronghold beyond it. Rennic is somewhere inside their competing bargains. A ferry crew needs a safe route, private soldiers have finite loyalties, and the wrong spell can turn a narrow landing into a killing ground. Expect tactical choices, counterspelling, infiltration and negotiations backed by real force.

Movement: secure a crossing → break or reshape the siege → settle the stronghold and your recovery.

## D. The Bell That Answers — supernatural hunt

An abandoned bell rings without moving, and fragments of an old magical battle appear in living streets. Rennic's missing manuscript offers a lead, while collectors and practitioners race to reach the source for different reasons. You can hunt the workings, protect people, pursue rival agents and decide what dangerous knowledge is worth preserving. Expect an active magical mystery with physical targets you can reach and disrupt.

Movement: confront a manifestation → hunt its source and competing claimants → determine the apparatus's fate.

## E. The Black Lantern Exchange — magical heist and criminal recovery

Rare spellbooks and dangerous enchanted goods are arriving for a private floating auction. Rennic and the fellowship's manuscript are connected to the sale. Bidders, hired magicians and rival claimants have plans of their own; your way in might be a disguise, an introduction, a legitimate offer or a forceful recovery. Expect social manoeuvring, stealth, counter-magic and the possibility of a very public fight.

Movement: gain access → execute your chosen recovery → secure custody amid the sale's consequences.
