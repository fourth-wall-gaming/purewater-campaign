## Adversarial spine

Player-experience brief: magic-heavy, many consequential opportunities for
spellcasting, a little fighting. First arrival and fantasy-agent fieldwork,
investigation, disguise, infiltration, counter-magic and the former-teacher
relationship are central. Do not turn every lead into a brawl. Principal possible
battles are a contested search, an earned hound interception and the surviving
climax; each remains avoidable or alterable through successful play.

Magic opportunities by situation: A1 offers examination of a real binding sketch
and any actual magical trace, with Read Magic/Detect Magic when applicable;
concealed room access can use Invisibility or Change Appearance without erasing
physical obstacles or NPC reactions. A2 permits magical concealment, disguise,
decoys and counter-surveillance; an enemy gets no automatic ability to see through
them. A3 offers hands-on examination of an unanimated sample throat Doria retained
from fabrication, with visible runes and glass structure; she cannot animate it.
It is a physical clue and test piece, not a free item granting spells or a new
hound body. A4 allows the teacher and PC to compare a physical binding diagram
and plan a real dismantling, without an NPC solving everything. B1 is direct
counter-magic against a vulnerable working construct. B2 offers magical stealth,
disguise, examination and actual dispelling of the captured bindings; physical
infiltration still works. B3 supports a disguised approach to the broker and
authentication of the actual binding research. C1/C2 allow magical extraction,
countermeasures and focused battle casting when chosen. Locate Object is useful
only for objects and circumstances its actual rule permits; no free omniscient
tracking. Prepared spells, casting checks, resource costs and opportunities to
rest follow the real CFI rules. Never demand a particular spell as the sole key.

Captain Mara Senn wants a profitable private mage-capture enterprise. She has knowingly converted salvage constructs to hunting people. Her immediate objective is Rennic's disabling manuscript and his capture; she next wants the unwilling Kest under control so the product remains reproducible. She can negotiate or surrender, but owns the coercion. Her commercial buyer, Leth Varro, wants a portable means to capture resistant practitioners for private clients. He requires a live demonstration against an unwilling active caster before paying the balance. He has no Crown or Assay authority. Both know they are arranging coercion, not a consensual exhibition. Defeating Senn ends her operation; Varro cannot conjure a replacement pack or army.

Varro's deposit10GP is INCLUDED in Senn's existing18GP chest, not additional cash. He holds the promised40GP balance in his own locked travelling money-case. He has no personal soldiers in Purewater. He arrives at the Kiln by hired ordinary boat on d8/day if invited, with prior appointment and a route which can be discovered. No offscreen crossing or payment has yet occurred. As a broker he has previously sought an introduction to Maudren Sere through an intermediary; no meeting, sponsorship, complicity or purchase by Sere is established. That avenue can expose wider arcane politics without turning a master into a secret culprit by fiat.

## Investigative spaces

Three Steps Rooms: narrow boarding-house entered from canal landing; public common room, stair and rear service landing form three routes. Rennic's upper room has one window above a rope-work awning, a lockable door and another guest's door nearby. No forced collapse. Elna holds a spare key. Receipt and binding sketch in the room can survive a failed search check; concealed details may take time. Street observers can watch the landing without being omniscient.

Sailmakers' Loft: disused upper sail store above a working chandlery, offered by Doria. Main stair, loading hatch with block and tackle, roof hatch and a canal-side rope ladder give different approaches and exits. Cloth bales provide cover and concealment; lamps are ordinary fire hazards, not scripted explosions. Rennic has a worktable and the book, not an invulnerable sanctuary. No adversary initially knows this address.

Furnace Court, Glasswalk and Drowned Kiln retain their saved maps. Glasswalk is a possible pursuit or interception route once somebody actually chooses it, not an inevitable rooftop scene.

West Lantern Landing: ferry steps, unloading platform, lamp-lit boat berths and a covered public route-board. The route-board identifies High Isle and a marked water-stair for the House of Small Windows. A hired boat or public footbridges are both ordinary possible routes; no route forces a fight or toll. The fellowship letter names a contact, not Rennic’s location. First arrival offers an intelligible destination and room to choose how to approach a new city. No expense is deducted for a voyage already covered by starting background.

## Act transitions and meaningful consequences

I: outsider pursuit becomes a contest with another organisation; finding Rennic and discovering why he is endangered are substantial achievements. II: expose and strike the business's dependencies; reveal that capture is the product and living proof is the price. III: prevent, defeat or reshape the surviving handover. Any objective can be achieved early or in another order.

Six to ten sessions is a scope estimate, not a quota. Investigation needs leads that change the board, enemies who act, and physical missions. A successful fight can grant safety, a valuable whistle or a usable prisoner. Failed operations cost finite people, time, money or access. Injuries and rest affect actual next opportunities. Institutions provide real help and opponents are not immune to evidence. Shared city factions continue their own actual agendas; none is secretly responsible for every conflict.

## Model mapping and audit

Characters carry score and doing. Fact propositions carry starting truth; learn edges specify source, certainty and time, with false interpretations recorded separately. Agendas carry goal and earned progress. Beats carry situation cards, prerequisites, cast, location, warnings and outcome branches. Planned discovery/outcome facts remain not-yet-true until actually earned; no learned edges to futures. Revealing a hideout to a handler changes Senn's operations only after an actual report. After a watch, resolve uncertain offscreen attempts once through CLI, persist outcomes, update assets and cancel impossible successors. Review uses GM visibility and consumes no world time.
