# Purewater: Campaign Tone and Antagonists

Authoritative player clarification, 2026-10-30. OOC review holds play at
Halla d8/dawn. No clock movement or new fictional event follows from this review.

## Social baseline

Purewater has pretensions of holiness over widespread dishonesty and ruthless
survival. A large majority are dishonest, ruthless, or both. Not everyone is a
murderer: survival-driven deceit, loyalty, affection, craft pride and local codes
of honour coexist with exploitation. Respectability is not evidence of virtue.
Honest people and effective allies exist without sanitising the city.

Lullwater is genuinely dangerous, populated by thieves and criminal networks.
Other districts are more respectable but still largely dishonest. Bodies in
canals are a recurring feature of the setting. Describe violence non-graphically;
severe villains do not require graphic detail or constant random attacks.

## Crowbill

A malevolent organised-crime boss, capable of ordering murders of people who
threaten his business, including locals. The earlier blanket prohibition on
killing locals is superseded. Selective loyalty and personal codes are not a
general guarantee of safety. Courtesy and generosity do not make him benevolent.
Coercion or murder can be calculated business decisions, not only angry revenge.
Practical restraints include exposure, witnesses, Crown force and retaliation.

Preserve actual play: his help, honoured promises and Halla's settled debt remain
real. No invented historical murders, new debts or automatic betrayal of an
already fulfilled promise. Halla's sister is not a compulsory retaliation target.

## Power and opposition

Power players can employ extortion, bribery, blackmail, sabotage, abduction and
deliberate killing. A polite official objection does not automatically reform
them. Public compliance can be tactical; a covert response still needs an actual
decision, information route, time and finite means. Some antagonists are genuinely
evil and need neither redemption nor a sympathetic explanation. Employees need
not share their employer's ruthlessness; Tovin's established character stands.

Before resuming rebuilt operations, specify actors, targets, motives, means,
timing, warning/discovery routes, resolution, counterplay and consequences.
Prepare reactions to protected victims, exposure, lost assets, Crown intervention
and early defeat. No omniscience, guaranteed escape or replacement resources to
cancel effective victories. No murder quota or compulsory harm.

## Reusable settings

Record separately: social corruption; district variation; antagonist cruelty;
violence thresholds; moral limits versus practical restraints; institutional
reach; narrative explicitness; preserved earned outcomes. For Purewater: high
corruption, severe principal-antagonist potential, selective honour, consequential
but uneven official protection, non-graphic depiction and honest resolution.
