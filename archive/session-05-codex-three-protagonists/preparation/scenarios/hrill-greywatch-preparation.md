## Experience and global conflict

Avel Veyr wants Greywatch's signal, defensible landing and thirty days' military
stores as a recruitment base for renewed draconic imperial rule. She believes
small independent communities will eventually accept a ruler who can enforce
order. She has committed her remaining savings and six-person company to taking
it. Corporal Dera Venn refuses to surrender royal ground or its civilians.
Avel will use lethal force against armed resistance; prisoners are leverage and
labor, not automatic murder victims. Pike wants his outstanding fee, not empire.
His pursuit of the keeper threatens the village's hospitality and her freedom.
Hrill is the trained close fighter immediately beside the threatened person.
Protecting her can lead to protecting a real Crown garrison; no oath is required.

This advances the global restoration conflict causally: a landing, stores and
signal become contested capacity. Saving the post denies that foothold. Avel is
independent of Hanzo. No secret orders, possession, endless reinforcements,
reactivated tournament scheme or cancellation of Halla's victory. Hanzo's actual
withdrawal and parked household remain unchanged. The wider question is whether
fear and promised order can rebuild the old dominion, opposed by people who
choose mutual defense. A local victory can finish this adventure completely.

## Three acts, subject to player changes

I — Shelter under arms. Protect Halwen or otherwise settle the attempted seizure.
Immediate win: safe civilian and a reduced, driven-off, captured or negotiated
pursuit party. Investigate physical boat/keeper evidence to learn the post is
threatened. If Hrill refuses, Selith can shelter Halwen and residents resist;
resolve actual contest, no mandatory death or destruction. Enemy withdrawal
changes later blockade resources. End when help/withdrawal is decided.

II — Reach and change the siege. Actual2h passage required if chosen, with
equipment/rest decisions respected. Enemy cove/boat can be disabled, bought out
or captured; infiltrate water stair, relieve garrison, recover supplies, scout
enemy positions. Discovery of imperial purpose changes an apparent local seizure
into an attempt to build real restorationist capacity. No second required fight.
If Pike is neutralized there is no blockade; if Avel already surrenders skip
later conflict. Warnings are visible sentries, ladder and shouted demands.

III — End the surviving operation. Defend against an executable breach, organize
a sortie, surround the company, secure surrender, or evacuate while denying
stores. Loss of landing/resources matters even if Avel escapes. Possible durable
endings: post retained; post abandoned with all people saved; enemy occupation;
partial rescue; lawful custody. Avel can actually win but no ending is compulsory.
An early victory stays a victory, with no revenge raid spawned to fill acts.
