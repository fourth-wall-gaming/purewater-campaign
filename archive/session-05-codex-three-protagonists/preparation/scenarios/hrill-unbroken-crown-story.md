# Hrill — The Unbroken Crown

Agreed OOC revision: investigate radical allegiance to inhuman Dragon Kings,
secret oaths and coercion; leave Dane's salvage expedition alone. Avel's capture
and independent self-funding stand. Vane recruited and encouraged her but did
not command or finance Greywatch. Hold play at d41/day280 during review.

# ACT I — WHAT THE OATH DEMANDS
**For:** Question the existing commercial contact, discover Vane's introduction of Avel, examine the inherited propaganda and possession dedication, find a recruiting invitation or witness and learn what the oath actually demands.
**Takes:** Relationships and witness safety can be exposed through actual choices; ordinary opinion is distinguished from sworn conspiracy by evidence, not a salvage dispute. Unopposed, Othin watches Mara and Vane courts two named veterans.

# ACT II — THOSE WHO REFUSE
**For:** Protect Mara and the Crown courier, interrupt or follow finite parties, investigate the tollhouse and secure the pledge, Cassian correspondence, disavowal, oath or stolen credentials through knightly action, strategy and social skill.
**Takes:** Actual coercion, capture and armed resistance can cost freedom or lives. Warning, allies, route changes and decisive action prevent dependent operations. Unopposed, Vane attempts a real initiation, resupply and courier interception.

# ACT III — BREAK THE UNBROKEN CROWN
**For:** Defeat, expose or settle the surviving cell, free any actual captive, prevent forgery, stop any still-possible armed Reedgate enclave and intercept any report that would open its road to the demonological programme. Let early official or personal victory stand.
**Takes:** Opponents can escape, evidence can be lost or captives harmed through actual contests. Every lost recruit, coin, operative or credential reduces Vane's real options. No compulsory sacrifice, replacement enemy, magical explanation or cancellation of Avel's prior defeat.
