# Glass Hounds — player/GM review and revision requirements

23 October 2026. OOC review after selection and initial setup. PLAY IS HELD AT THE OPENING while the user evaluates the design. No actions, rolls or time advance occur during this discussion. The saved rooftop opening has NOT been accepted as the revised opening. This document records authorised design direction; implementation and reconciliation with the live DB remain required before play resumes.

## Accepted direction from the player

- The immediate sighting of Rennic and a glass hound skips the desired outsider experience. Begin with arriving in a new city, navigating its communities, investigating contacts and pursuing a missing teacher. The hound should be an earned discovery, not the opening spectacle.
- Act movement should change Nimras's understanding and opportunities, rather than simply move from hunt to workshop to export.
- Rennic's danger should have personal weight through his teaching relationship with Nimras. Following familiar precautions and recognising how pressure breaks them are possible evidence, not narrated PC thoughts or mandatory conclusions.
- Real adversaries use surveillance, threats, searches, ambush and violence for particular objectives. Local dangerous people should act. Injury, defeat, capture and death are legitimate possible consequences, subject to actual action and honest resolution. No predetermined harm is required.
- The user accepted the existing motive: Rennic carries a manuscript that can disable the hounds and is helping their maker shut them down. Capture prevents his interference and secures the book.
- Build an identifiable, worthy principal antagonist with a goal, plan and adversarial organisation. Not a diffuse collection of equally sympathetic interests. Negotiability does not erase responsibility for coercion.

## Antagonist spine proposed in response

Captain Mara Senn is the principal active antagonist. She knowingly converts salvage constructs into tools for hunting and abducting practitioners. Her concrete goal is to deliver a working pack and reproducible method to a paying client, establishing a lucrative private mage-recovery business. No purchaser is yet named or live; do not invent a pre-existing master's guilt without developing that actor's motive and operation.

Her immediate obstacles are Rennic, who can disable the product, and Kest, who can stop manufacture. She intends to seize Rennic and the manuscript, secure the control notes and compel Kest's cooperation if he refuses. Forced cooperation is an intended escalation, not an event already achieved. Once Nimras's intervention is actually reported to her, she can investigate and obstruct him. She does not know his commission, skills, location or relationships by narration alone.

Use the existing six-person company as an accountable adversarial organisation, with local hired blades among its named members. Do not simply add inexhaustible cutthroats. Any changed backgrounds, additional hires, payments, orders and deployments must be prepared and written to the live save before use. Senn can be dangerous through preparation, city contacts, force and willingness to coerce without being made omnipotent or secretly commanded by a replacement boss.

Act I: Nimras's investigation crosses the company's search for Rennic. Physical evidence of intrusion, compromised contacts and surveillance can reveal that there are competing hunters. Specific operations and clues still need preparing; no automatic witness injury or inevitable ambush.

Act II: Contact with Rennic and discovery of the hunting conversion expose Senn's objective. His possession of the book and commitment to stopping her produce genuine personal and professional conflict. Senn attempts to deny the party safety, destroy or recover evidence and secure her finite assets, using information she actually acquires. Counteraction must matter.

Act III: The surviving plan moves toward an actual handover or departure, with time, location, client and prerequisites fully defined before scheduling. Nimras can defeat the company, dismantle capacity, secure official help, divide its supporters or negotiate a surrender. Early decisive victory stands. No mandatory final duel, capture, death or sequel.
