# Hrill — The Culvert Patrol
A small independent incident within the accepted week of Crown service. Prior Greywatch victory and relief request stand. Service and comradeship are chosen by the player; no hidden Dragon King connection.
# ACT I — THE ROAD BELONGS TO TRAVELLERS
**For:** Work with the patrol to protect a carrier from four armed robbers; choose ground, negotiate or fight with real allies and visible crossbows.
**Takes:** Injury, loss of cargo or capture can follow actual contested actions; warning, cover, surrender and retreat exist.
# ACT II — WHAT GETS AWAY
**For:** If any threat or stolen property remains, use troop cooperation, actual tracking and alternate approaches to the single nearby shelter. Early success can omit this act.
**Takes:** Time, separated allies and surviving enemy weapons create risk; no replenishment or mandatory second fight.
# ACT III — BRING THEM HOME
**For:** Restore surviving property, secure willing surrender or lawful custody, and return to Hythe. Preserve useful comrades and an earned safe road.
**Takes:** Custody and lives require practical care; no prescribed harm or automatic pardon, reward or wider conspiracy.
