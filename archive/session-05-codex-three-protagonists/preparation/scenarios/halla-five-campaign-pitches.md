# Halla: Five Possible Campaigns

Proposals only. Purewater remains home; each opens new parts of its islands,
waterways and surrounding lake. None is active until selected.

## 1. The Quiet War

Crime, divided loyalties, and street fighting. A new syndicate starts buying
Purewater's docks and hiring soldiers. Crowbill wants a favour from his old
employee, now conveniently wearing the Prince's colours. A struggle between
criminal powers spreads into neighbourhoods Halla has sworn to protect.

Act I: a favour becomes a serious conflict of duty. Act II: raids, dangerous
alliances and discoveries across the rival organisations. Act III: Halla gets
a chance to break the struggle for control of the waterfront.

## 2. The Drowned Crown

Expedition, treasure, and ancient danger. Falling lake levels expose a stairway
into a city absent from Purewater's histories. Rival expeditions race to enter,
and Halla has the opportunity to lead one. There are fortunes below, inhabited
places where none should exist, and routes that disappear when the water rises.

Act I: establish a foothold beneath the lake. Act II: explore the buried city
and contend with rival explorers. Act III: fight for a discovery whose fate
will change the city above.

## 3. The Siege of Lantern Isle

Military adventure, leadership, and survival. A signal station goes dark on an
outer island of Purewater. Halla's small relief party finds a working community
caught between an armed occupation and an approaching assault. Holding a street
is possible; keeping an island alive is a different sort of soldiering.

Act I: reach the island and win room to act. Act II: sorties, evacuation routes
and alliances under siege. Act III: attempt a decisive breakout, relief operation
or reversal against the besiegers.

## 4. The Night Ferry

Supernatural mystery and perilous exploration. A ferry missing for thirty years
returns to Purewater. Its passengers insist they left yesterday. When people
start vanishing from the waterfront, Halla follows a route into another shore
of the lake, where familiar streets lead somewhere they should not.

Act I: investigate the returned ferry and the disappearances. Act II: venture
between the two shores and uncover the competing purposes behind the crossings.
Act III: undertake a dangerous passage to determine who can come home.

## 5. The Ashen Hunt

Monster hunting, rivalry, and dangerous terrain. Something powerful is hunting
through Purewater's abandoned reservoirs. A substantial bounty brings competing
hunters, including a celebrated company willing to endanger whole streets to
claim it. Halla enters the hunt with a local guide and a chance to earn real
money, but tracking the quarry opens a much larger conflict.

Act I: survive the first hunt. Act II: follow the quarry through reservoirs,
reed islands and hidden dens while contending with rival hunters. Act III:
confront the source of the danger before the hunt reaches inhabited districts.
