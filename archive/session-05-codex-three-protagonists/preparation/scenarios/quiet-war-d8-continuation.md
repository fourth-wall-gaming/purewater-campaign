little remorse for deliberate harm to opponents; favours and protection as
leverage; patient response to humiliation, not automatic rage. Will order
murder when a person's resistance threatens durable control and force is

adult porters/boat hands and two skiffs only. No new trained killers.

automatically know Ward's new guard arrangement or Neri's rejected leases.

caring who bears them; minimises responsibility for hired coercion. Public

blocked boom and Neri defeated lease offers. No secret PC history.

violence against a target, but will not expend company in a hopeless assault.

wage-driven character stands; he is not a secret sadist or guaranteed defector.

use his rented cistern route on his protection terms. Target: independent
crews' route choice. Earliest d8/day, one watch including return report.
Cost: one worker-watch; no new cash or assets. Requires actual contact, not

against armed opposition, not automatic killing. Warn weapon escalation.
If Crown guards outmatch them, withdraw by available exit; surrender if trapped.

alternate routes, blockade or capture can stop operation. Failure removes any
lost boats/people and leaves witnesses. No unstoppable offscreen harm.

## Reaction matrix (all principals)

Exposed secrecy: requires eyewitness report, captured instruction or public
accusation reaching principal by messenger (one local journey, up to a watch).
Crowbill redirects uncommitted workers/abandons exposed interception; Marr may
withdraw offers and distance herself from Bren; Bren recalls detached soldiers.
No magically erased evidence. Pursuit or silencing a witness needs a separately
prepared operation and warning routes; it never occurs by this matrix alone.

Protected victims: on actual scout/porter report, Crowbill waits or redirects
to an independently known unguarded opportunity; cannot transfer an inevitable
attack onto Dagna. Marr bargains for access or withdraws, Bren avoids superior
force. The protected convoy stays protected if escort remains effective.

Lost resources/followers: subtract exact losses immediately. Crowbill C2 requires
four available workers and two boats; otherwise cancel or redesign with reduced
scope. Marr M1 cannot spend unavailable funds. Bren will bargain for surviving
company rather than summon reinforcements. Replacement takes priced recruitment
and time; no replacement planned here.

Official intervention: Marr/Bren already know Ward restriction; Q4 remains
preempted. Crowbill learns only via direct warning or report; if warned before
C2, compare actual available force and witnesses, suspend exposed seizure if
untenable. Threats may be withdrawn strategically without moral conversion.
Any later breach must be a new attempt, detectable and punishable.

Early defeat: arrest, surrender or loss of required boats/route cancels
dependent operations. No compulsory successor. Settle restitution and surviving
contracts; campaign may end early. End protection payments if coercive capacity
and dependent arrangements are actually removed.
