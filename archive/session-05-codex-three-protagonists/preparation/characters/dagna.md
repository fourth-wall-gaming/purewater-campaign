# Dagna

Adult dwarf woman; Halla's sister. Independent boatwright and repair-yard owner, Forge Quarter.

## Character

Dagna runs a small boat-repair yard in Purewater's Forge Quarter. Her expertise is fitting timber, repairing hulls and keeping practical mechanisms working. The planted-cargo trouble threatened the yard; Halla borrowed from Crowbill to protect her. She values her brother, her customers and independence. Wants steady legal work, a sound yard and eventual money to take on an apprentice; no existing apprentice invented. She is neither a hidden fighter nor merely a hostage.
