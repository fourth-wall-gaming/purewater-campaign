# Midnight Exchange opening: a first customer

Session 7 opens at d7/day in the fellowship house, with no travel, rest or contract
accepted. Glass Hounds is complete. This bounded, modest-risk commission is ready
independently of the later guarded-auction contract. The wider three-adventure
plan remains GM preparation; instantiate its opposition before activating it.

## Cast, truth and intentions

Nessa Rill is an independent appraiser, competent at assessing enchanted objects
but no spellcaster. Her blue leather tool-case contains a silver testing needle,
a folding lens, etched sample plates and her own annotated reference booklet.
She sent it home with a consignment through Tobin Marr's forwarding store; a
mislabel left it in his dry loft rather than delivering it to her workshop.
No antagonist stole it. Her forwarded request for an independent magician is
available among the fellowship's incoming business correspondence. Rennic can
recommend the opportunity without knowing its solution or inventing a history
with Nessa. Nessa offers 10GP total, 3GP of that in advance, from a reserved
10GP fee purse. Fee covers intact tools and case only. No relic recovery owed.

Tobin Marr wants to clear his store and retain customers. He has the case but
only knows it is among three crates marked as returned instruments. He admits
the delivery mistake and grants a named representative access. A damp label
prevents immediate identification; Nessa's description and two matching brass
moth clasps are sufficient to identify the real case on inspection. The loft
padlock key has snapped, both pieces kept in a dish; Tobin offers to bring a
locksmith at his own cost the following morning. Nimras can act sooner by his
own chosen means. No clock, fee escalation, surprise theft or punishment for
waiting. Tobin has no reason to lie, attack or withhold the case. He initially
knows nothing about Nimras, Rusk or demonology. He forwards auction catalogues
as ordinary business but has no secret faction membership.

Nessa knows the case description, her ownership and the delivery receipt. She
does not know where the mislabelled crate sits or why the loft cannot currently
be opened. Rennic knows only the advertised request and its terms. Nimras
learns the public offer in the opening; no private solution is revealed.

## Possible three-act development of this small job

I: Choose whether to meet the customer; inspect description/receipt and agree a
fee. II: Locate and access the case at Marr's; use ingenuity or ordinary help.
III: Return intact tools, receive the balance and decide whether to cultivate a
repeat client. No battle owed. This is a business foothold, not the entire first
adventure's heist. A later auction request is explicitly a separate job with
risk and payment disclosed before acceptance.

## Playable cards

A. Fellowship offer: at current home, Rennic passes an open work request naming
Nessa, the missing tools, fee and Merchant's Quarter workshop address. Purpose:
first customer opportunity. No roll for reading, no order to return to the city.
The player may decline, rest, ask about it or choose their own next action.
Rennic does not know the customer personally; he can vouch for nothing beyond
the letter. No automatic use of Nimras's name as advertising or business name.

B. Nessa's workshop: Merchant's Quarter; street door and rear water-stair, 20ft
square main room, workbench, stool, shelves and a charcoal brazier. Open by day.
Smell of warm beeswax, file rasp. Her duplicate lens and case drawing are on the
bench. Nessa supplies written collection permission and the described advance
if a contract is agreed; she speaks plainly about practical work, not politics.
Checking the obvious receipt succeeds routinely. Honest agreement needs no
Influence roll. Deceit is opposed by Insight55 only when an actual lie matters.
No assassination or ambush at this stage. Theft of her possessions is not assumed.

C. Marr's store: Merchant's Quarter canal frontage, foot access over the public
bridge; 30ft by20ft ground floor, front shop, rear cargo door/water-stair, safe
fixed ladder to a 15ft by20ft dry loft. Locked loft hatch; shelves and secured
crates offer cover, no unexplained lethal traps. Tobin's keys are at the desk,
broken loft key pieces in a dish. Recover case through permission and access.
Repair can target the broken key under its actual CFI rule; alternative physical
access/locksmith works. No special anti-magic or skill invented. If a risky forced
entry is chosen, establish risks/grade before rolling: damage to lock/door, not
random collapse onto civilians. Inside, matching clasps identify the case
without a mandatory roll. A Locate Object attempt follows actual familiarity
and range rules; description alone never silently counts as personal familiarity.

D. Completion at workshop: intact case/tool return earns remaining7GP if3GP was
advanced, otherwise10GP. An agreed partial recovery is negotiated from actual
losses; it does not forfeit pay for unrelated reasons. No automatic reputation
points or spells awarded. Nessa may offer introductions after the completed job.
Later consignment/relic work is separately prepared, priced and chosen.

## Operations, resources and branches

Nessa's active goal has three earned steps: hire help, regain tools, settle fee.
Contact beat requires her meeting a willing representative, not elapsed time.
Tobin's maintenance beat requires an actual visit that exposes the broken key,
an agreement to wait, and the next morning; schedule only then. The black-market
auction deadline and Rusk's hunt are not running during this low-risk foothold.
Cash is finite10GP; no money granted until accepted/earned. Tobin has one mundane
knife, no guards, no magic; Nessa one ordinary walking stick. Both surrender or
seek help if threatened, rather than escalate a parcel dispute to killing.

## Readiness and continuation

Before handing over: cast sheets and personality scores, both locations and
staging, initial NPC knowledge, Nessa's goal and contact beat persisted; letter
shown and learned in-session. Verify Nimras Luck5 for new session, MP10/15,
wealth13GP4SP, no rest, home location, worldd7/day. Actual transfer to Purewater
requires player direction and elapsed travel; tick and resolve other due events
at that time. Existing home is within a normal same-day journey, exact region
unfixed; do not relocate it to the capital. Fees and ordinary room costs can be
used as already established; no business lease imposed.

Refusal closes this offer without retaliation. Authorities or a locksmith can
solve a mundane lock. Clever magic makes an easy job easier. The later arc
provides its worthy adversary and real danger after the player chooses that
level of work. Low initial risk is the player's instruction, not a defect to fix.
