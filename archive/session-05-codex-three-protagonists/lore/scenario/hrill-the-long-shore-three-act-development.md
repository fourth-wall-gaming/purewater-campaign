# Hrill — The Long Shore

Selected by player, 26 October 2026. Additional direction: make it risky.
The completed road, restitution and bounded lookout lease remain accomplished.
No lake incident is a reversal of those victories. Other PCs retain their scenes.

# ACT I — LANDINGS PEOPLE CAN USE

**For:** Meet Edda through Maelin at Three Hearths, negotiate actual work, travel
to Hythe and join a supply boat. Explore unfamiliar shores before discovering the
great animal. Secure Bell Reed landing and deliver real supplies. Physical clues,
local accounts and a hunter's preparations reveal overlapping dangers.

**Takes:** Lives, cargo, equipment, wages and the boat can be lost through actual
hazards or enemy action. Warnings, shelter, rope, competent crew and retreat work.
No compulsory wreck, fight, accepted employment or first sight of the animal.

# ACT II — THE LIVING CHANNEL

**For:** Explore White Shingle and Narrow Water, observe the reedback, learn the
seasonal channel and make contact with Ilyen's independent shore community.
Garr's finite hunting expedition competes for these waters. Hrill can protect a
crew, investigate, negotiate, expose or disable actual hunting capacity, and
earn access to older resistance history and routes.

**Takes:** The animal, fishers' livelihood, shore access and people aboard boats
face cold water, entanglement, armed interference and a possible harpoon attempt.
No secret universal ritual, automatic hostile elves, prescribed betrayal or
claim that all earlier military service was wrong.

# ACT III — A PASSAGE WORTH KEEPING

**For:** Use earned knowledge and alliances to establish a safe northern supply
route and settle surviving conflict. Options include preventing a hunt, freeing
an actually entangled animal, protecting a consenting community's access or
arranging an enforceable practical agreement. Early solutions can end the story.

**Takes:** Confront only surviving operations with real means and intelligence.
Loss, withdrawal, partial success and lasting peaceful victory all stand. Nobody
must fight the animal or undertake a larger imperial war. No replacement villain
appears because Hrill successfully settled the conflict.
