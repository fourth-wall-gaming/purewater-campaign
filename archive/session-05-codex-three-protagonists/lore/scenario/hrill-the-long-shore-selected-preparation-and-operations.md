## Hook and bounded commission

Edda Vale, owner of the supply boat **Grey Heron**, is at Three Hearths selling
salt and needles to upland households. She knows Maelin from an earlier ordinary
quay survey; Maelin knows her profession, boat and honest working habits, not
the northern secrets. This is new selected setting, not a previously played
meeting with Hrill. Edda wants a dependable person who can judge landing safety,
organise help and deal with strangers. She heard two northern landings are unsafe;
she has not seen the creature or hired a knight specifically to fight it.

Offer:1GP/day and food/lodging for an initial ten days starting acceptance;
2GP advance against wages, remainder accrued daily, paid on demand/settlement.
Travel from hospice included. Scope: inspect/reopen three landings, assist supply
deliveries and negotiate access. No police authority, oath, compulsory fight,
unlimited service or right to command shore households. Recontract before day11;
if work ends remotely, safe return to Hythe or another agreed port included.
Refusal: Edda travels with current crew and can meet Hrill later in Hythe; no debt.
Offer and two coins must actually be accepted before purse/employment changes.
Initial meeting offers food, not an automatic signed contract or consumed meal.

## Central conflict and global connection

Storm damage has narrowed the dependable supply landings. A large migratory
reedback grazes the shallows and exposes safer seasonal passages through its
movements. Hunter Garr Hew wants the shell's valuable horn plates; a carcass may
bring60GP and a reputation. He will endanger another boat to preserve a viable
hunt, but wants to survive. Ilyen, shore warden, protects local fishing and a reed
enclave; outsiders and harpoons have made them distrustful. Both can negotiate.
Edda wants supplies delivered and crew/boat home, not command over the shore.

Global plot: independent older peoples retain a lake route and records of
resistance to Dragon Kings' rule. Listening Reeds keeps historical names of
imperial shore strongholds and seasonal refuge channels. Hrill can establish a
consenting supply/access relationship that keeps this community independent and
its knowledge available. This **creates a real logistical relationship** useful
against future imperial pressure; it does not discover a secretly revived empire
or solve a ritual by touching a relic. Garr is an independent commercial hunter,
not an automatically revealed restorationist. No Nimras knowledge imported.
Selith's historic records are evidence, not a new universal counterspell. No
active supernatural compulsion, prophecy or creature summoning occurs here.

## Creature: reedback and ecological limits

One24ft freshwater shelled herbivore, custom nonhuman sheet, no Luck or spells.
STR30 CON22 SIZ36 DEX8 INT5 POW12; AP2, Swim85 Brawn80 Endurance80,
Perception50, Evade20, bite/buffet55. Natural armour shell7, limbs/head3;
HP custom anatomical sheet in DB, bite1d8 plus2d6, body buffet1d6 plus2d6.
Do not treat it as a monster eager to fight: grazes and migrates, retreats from
serious wounds, surfaces for air, can flee instead of defending every asset.
Reed mats carry plants and nesting birds, no people/town on its back. It does
not understand Common, command water, recognise heroic worth or know a prophecy.

Approach: grazing has head low, slow wake; warning head rises and reeds shake if
a boat closes within15ft. Turning away to beyond60ft ends pressure. Continuing
to crowd permits one warning shove against hull; actual harpoon hit permits
bite/buffet at adjacent attackers or a dive. If tethered, contested Brawn80 vs
boat-handler Boating determines tow/control; crew can cut line before next pull.
No auto-sinking or auto-death. Observing at60ft+, feeding study/successful Survival
or Nera/Ilyen account reveals predictable routes; failure costs observation time
or lost position, not a random compulsory attack. Need two observations or one
observation+local corroboration to publish a reliable seasonal channel.

## Resolution and danger rules

Every uncertain roll through CLI. CFI human combat, all NPC Luck0; full AP turns,
special effects chosen before damage. GarrCombat65/AP2 inAP2, crew45-50; Hrill
can outfight one opponent, a coordinated boatful is dangerous. IlyenBow60/AP2,
scouts50; boats/shore cover/negotiation/retreat can divide conflict.

Heavy harpoon:1d10,M,Impale,30ft effective/60ft long, two actions reload, two
carried total. Recover after an actual retrieval; no endless ammunition. Lines
can be untied/cut under ordinary object rules; ropeAP6/HP3 if hacking indiscriminately,
deliberate knife cut of a slack accessible rope uses a successful appropriate
weapon/Engineering task without HP grind. Body location and armour still matter.

Boat hull sections: Grey Heron3sectionsAP4/HP20 each; Hunt2sectionsAP3/HP18.
At0 section is breached/unusable; onebreach permits crew patch/bail while making
for shelter, secondbreach requires abandonment unless repaired. No arbitrary
sinking timer: persist section damage, leaks, crew action and distance. Damages
rolled via CLI, then saved to owning actor assets. TenderAP3/HP10, no spare tender
conjured. Cuts to rigging/sails affect mobility; no character HP damage from
losing sail alone. Collision attack/avoidance: opposed Boating vs Boating or
Evade for person on quay; actual impact1d6 hull damage, +1d6 only at committed
full speed after a visible run-up. Failure can ground rather than ram.

Warnings/counterplay: squall line and shortening wind before open-water work;
Ressa can recommend shelter. Weather passage Boating Standard with sound
preparation, Hard if insisting on exposed fast approach; failure loses position
or damages a specifically exposed section1d6, offers anchor/reef/retreat before
another attempt. No rolling every calm sailing day. Wet deck AthleticsStandard
if rushing or fighting across it, a secured deliberate crossing needs no roll.
BellReed rotten outer plank visibly sags; falls6ft into cold water if tested
unsupported and failed. Safetyline/innerstonepier/repair eliminate that risk.

Cold water, mail and entanglement are real. Check current Swim/armour rules when
exposure occurs; armour penalties apply per actual CFI rule, not an invented
automatic drowning or permission requirement. Breath/asphyxiation uses loaded
system/asphyxiation: prepared Endurance seconds, unexpected passive half,
strenuous one-fifth, then Endurance each5sec round and fatigue. HrillEndurance45
thus prepared45seconds, strenuous surprise9seconds; no immediate death. Rope,
shedding gear, tender, crew rescue and shore escape all available actions.
Damage/recovery materially constrain later action. Do not increase danger merely
because previous risks were handled well.
