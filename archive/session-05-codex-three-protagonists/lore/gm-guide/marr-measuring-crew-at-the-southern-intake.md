Hedda Rook and Tovin Beck use two of Sella Marr’s six established civilian employees, leaving four other civilian slots unallocated. Both are at the southern intake working bay taking an estimate. Not mercenaries; no weapons, spells or new funds granted. Work order authorises a quotation only. This accounts for visible chalk marks without executing future lease or closure operations. They know the concern’s name and their own task, not its whole agenda. Full preparation: docs/scenarios/nimras-the-missing-teacher.md, Following Rennic.
