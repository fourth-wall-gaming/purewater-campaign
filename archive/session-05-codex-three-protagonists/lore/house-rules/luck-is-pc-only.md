Luck belongs exclusively to player characters in this campaign. All GM-run NPCs and creatures, including named villains, companions, Thuban and Cailan, have no Luck Points. Do not grant rank-based Luck bonuses, NPC Luck refreshes, rerolls, digit reversals or damage mitigation. Printed defaults or source-character PC classification do not override current role. Halla retains his normal PC Luck. Supersedes previous Thuban future-profile Luck6 claim; all other rank benefits unchanged.
