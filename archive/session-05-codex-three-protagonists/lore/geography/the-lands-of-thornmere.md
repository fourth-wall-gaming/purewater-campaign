Thornmere is a long kingdom lying between a great inland lake and the sea, and
**[[Purewater]]** sits at the one point where they meet. Everything that moves between
the Lake and the sea goes through its strait, which is why so small a city matters so
much, and why the King keeps his nephew there. The map of the realm is `maps/realm/the-realm-of-thornmere.jpg`.

## The Sacred Lake

**The Lake** is the Lady's: the water, the islands and the fish. It is a long,
narrow, winding water, nearly three hundred miles from the Mouth to its head and seldom
twenty across, running north through the whole kingdom like a crooked blade. It has bays and
arms at every bend. The **west arm** reaches toward the Ford, the long **east arm**
toward Hythe, and at its head the Lake splits into two fingers that run up into the
Wildwood. Purewater stands at its southern outflow, **the Mouth**. In the middle
reaches lies **the Lady's Isle**, uninhabited, where nobody lands except on the
Order's business. A few smaller islets are strung along the length of the Lake. Lake-boats work the whole shore,
and the lake trade (timber and furs from the north, wool from the east shore) comes
down to Purewater to meet the sea.

## The settled heart

West of the city, along the coast, lie the **farmlands**: ditched fields, market
towns such as **Southfold**, and the **King's Road** running west for nine days to
**Caldreth**, the capital, at the mouth of its own river. **King Ostmar** rules there,
among courtiers who have been the Baron's friends for thirty years. By sea it is four
days, weather allowing. The river **Cald** comes down to the capital out of the
kritch. Nothing from Caldreth arrives in Purewater quickly, and
everybody here has done that sum.

East of the city the **Eastern Causeway** runs past the Vantt estate and on into **the
Downs**, rolling sheep country with the wool town of **Wendlow**. A road runs north
across the Downs to **Hythe**, the biggest of the lake towns, at the end of the east
arm, where the east-shore boats load. South-east, at the tip of a long peninsula, is
the sea port of **Saltreach**, where ships too big for the Mouth unload. The
**Greyspine** mountains close the east, and Thornmere's border runs along their
foot.

## The March

North-west of the Lake, beyond the last farms, the kingdom stops being governed.
**The borderlands** (the March) are thirty years of raids, pogroms and reprisals in a
country of forest, scattered villages and, to the west, the **kritch**: swamp and
standing water where roads go to die. The **north road** is the only way through it
that carts can use. It runs from Caravan Square up the Lake's west shore, crosses the
river **Ash** at **the Ford** an hour out of the city, and climbs to **Ashwick**, a
wool and market town on the Ash under **the Wolds**, the low hills between the March
and the farmlands. Conall was raised there by a militiaman's family,
and the town stayed loyal to the King by keeping its head down. **Mereby** is a fishing
village on the Lake's north-west shore.

Past Ashwick the road climbs into **the Scorched Hills**, bare, burned-over hill
country, to **Emberhold**, the seat of
**[[Baron Hanzo di Teufel]]**: a keep of black stone and a single tall tower above a
burned valley. Everything north and west of it is his, whatever the King's maps say.

On the Lake's north-west shore, where the March meets the Wildwood, are the ruins of
**Drakesfall**, broken walls and a fused stone causeway running into the water. This
is where the Dragon Kings were thrown down in the uprising. The Swords of the Lake
won their oldest battle honours here, and here the age ended "at the Lake". Nobody
farms near it, and the Baron's keep is a day's ride away, which few people think
about and nobody says out loud.

## The Wildwood

North of the March and around the head of the Lake lies **the Wildwood**: old forest,
older than the kingdom, running north to the mountains and east to the edge of the
map. It is the country of the **deep elves**, who were here before the Lake had a
name and do not count themselves Thornmere's subjects. Their heart is **the Deep
Glades**, which no human has been invited into in living memory. A river comes south
out of the Wildwood into the head of the Lake.

The elves of the borderlands lived at the Wildwood's edge. They were the forest
peoples whose rites [[Gardwen]] keeps, and the Baron's Dragon Knights have been
killing them for thirty years. On the Wildwood's south-western edge, between the March
and the trees, is **the Remade Grove**, where Gardwen's family was killed and the
grove grew back around her grief. The druid **Gleinanion Othal** keeps it now.

North of everything are **the Hoarfells**, mountains nobody crosses. In the heart
of the Wildwood a small range, **the Elder Crags**, rises out of the trees beside the
Deep Glades. The Greyspine's **foothills** run down the east side of the Downs.

## Beyond Thornmere

The King's border is drawn on his maps and nowhere else. To the west, beyond the
kritch, the land is nobody's. Over the Greyspine to the east, and across the Open Sea
to the south, there are other countries; traders from both come through Purewater.
The Wildwood answers to the deep elves, not to Caldreth.
