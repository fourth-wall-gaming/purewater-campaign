Santo uses a stolen scroll of Seat the Bound to try to seat a lust spirit in Emmeralda and make her willing to leave with him. This is a poor-chance working: the recorded roll of 44 is a failure against Hard Arcane Casting 37, not a Standard success. The scroll burns out in his right hand. The binding fails; Santo panics, attacks Emmeralda with the barbed ritual knife, and flees. Gardwen and Magda reach her in time and save her life. Santo leaves the ritual knife, the spent scroll in the Baron's own hand, a chalk spirit-seat, and his crested coat. The witnesses see a young rich man escaping while cradling his burned right hand. Emmeralda survives and is herself.
