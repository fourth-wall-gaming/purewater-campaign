At the start of the evening trade, Emmeralda dances in the front hall of the Sylph's Embrace. Santo watches without drinking, then buys her evening through the house with new silver and no name. Nobody on the Pearl knows the heir's face. Magda and Gardwen are in the hall and can later identify the rich young man, his coat, and the hour. Constantine takes his money and thinks nothing of it.
