Halla comes voluntarily to Crowbill's waterside office during d0/day, carrying seven gold and intending to reduce the debt rather than risk the money in the streets. Crowbill first asks how much Halla is putting down and records only the amount Halla names.

Once the payment is settled, Crowbill has other business available. He knows the Baron wears a chain and locket and has never been seen in daylight without it. Crowbill's lamp-man on the Forge Quarter wharf has seen that the locket comes off at night. The Baron sleeps aft with it on the table beside his bed; the chain has a clasp, and Commander Vask sleeps across the door.

Crowbill does not tell Halla to steal it. He says what and where it is, and that he knows a boy who would try. Nus waits in the next room and hears the exchange. The player's response determines whether this becomes a job, a refusal, or information carried away.
