Constantine tells Marisette what a di Teufel attempted under one of her roofs and reports Santo's public threat. Marisette tries to close every Pearl house to the di Teufel retinue: no rooms, drink, music, or service. Her recorded Influence roll fails, so the coordinated district-wide exclusion does not hold; Constantine's own house refuses them, while wider agreement remains unsettled. The attempt still makes the Pearl's opposition explicit.
