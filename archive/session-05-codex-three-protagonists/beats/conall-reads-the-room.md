Demon-craft worked on an unwilling person is the King's Assay's business. Conall uses his warrant to enter the room before Santo's people can recover it. He finds the failed chalk seat, the spent Seat the Bound scroll in an unregistered hand, the barbed Asmodeus knife, Santo's crested coat, and Emmeralda alive but recovering. He secures the scroll and ritual evidence. His Arcane Knowledge roll fails, so he cannot yet certify the detailed mechanism or identify the hand, but the recovery and the evidence stand.
