Because Santo does not have Emmeralda, he returns to the Pearl at dusk with his burned hand bandaged. In front of the evening crowd he demands her, demands his coat, threatens the Sylph's Embrace, and tries to set its frontage alight. His recorded Arcane Casting roll fails, so no fire takes and nobody is injured. Blau arrives, restrains him, and removes him through a crowd that now connects the face, the burned hand, and the name di Teufel. Constantine takes the threat to Marisette.
