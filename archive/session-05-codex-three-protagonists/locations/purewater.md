Purewater is built across **the Mouth**: the narrow choke-point where the sacred
**Lake** to the north pours its holy waters south into the **open Sea**. It is the
gateway between inland lake-trade and the ocean routes -- rich, strategic, and
rotten. Locals call it **"Pisswater"** for the pollution and the corruption alike.

**The shape of the place** (north = the Lake; south = the Sea; west = the mainland).
The city map, `maps/purewater-map.png`, is canon; the full account is in
`lore/geography/the-city-and-its-islands.md`.

- On the **western** shore stands **[[Caravan Square]]**, a walled waterfront where
  the Caravan Road ends, and the **Gate of Triumph** -- "the Land Gate" to everyone
  who uses it -- the city's only land approach. The **Triumph Bridge** carries the
  road across the calm **landward channel** onto High Isle.
- Off the **eastern** shore, at the south-east corner and facing the open sea, lies
  **[[The Shoals]]** -- a low island of fish-markets, wharves and smugglers' jetties,
  bridged to the Forges and to the Eastern Causeway. This is the seaward jaw of the
  Mouth.
- Between them lies **[[High Isle]]** -- not one island but **four**, cut apart by the
  **Grand Canal** and its branches: the **North Quarter** (houses, the Governor's
  Palazzo, the Hall of Justice, the Barracks and the Tournament Ground), the **South
  Quarter** ([[The Merchant's Quarter]]), the **East Quarter** ([[The Forge Quarter]]),
  and the **Seaward Quarter** of docks and wharves along the south shore.
- To the **north-east**, a separate island reached by no bridge, lies
  **[[The Lullwater]]**, the poor-and-criminal quarter.
- North of High Isle, scattered across the Lake's calmer waters, lie **[[The Northern Isles]]** -- among them the bright pleasure-island of **[[The Pearl]]**, the
  half-drowned ruins of **[[Lost Isle]]**, quiet court-family retreats, and a few
  shunned, haunted, or magically claimed islets. Just north of the Tournament Ground,
  alone and unbridged, sits little **[[Temple Isle]]** with its temple and Sacred
  Confluence.

Gondolas and barges are the streets; bridges stitch the islands; mist and steam,
salt and canal-stink fill the air. Under it all runs the campaign's elemental
tension -- the Lake Lady's pure waters against the fire-corruption bleeding from the
Baron's **[[The DragonBarge]]**, moored at the Forges' seaward pier. Beneath
everything run **[[The Catacombs]]**.
