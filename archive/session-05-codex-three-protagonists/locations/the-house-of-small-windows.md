A narrow old High Isle house. A front workroom receives visitors; a passage leads to the private library and rear stair. The canal landing is accessible by ordinary boat. Dry book storage, an ink-stained worktable and window shutters matter more than grandeur. No automatic magical barrier, trap or teleport is established.
