An independent fellowship outside Purewater which trains and employs magicians, including mage hunters. Nimras left amicably to establish his own career. It has commissioned him to recover Rennic Vaur alive and an entrusted manuscript. Its ownership claim and concern about uncontrolled waterworks plans are genuine; it is not automatically a villain. No Crown warrant, local command, unlimited reinforcements or specified contract advance is supplied. No knowledge of Purewater’s current private affairs is presumed.
