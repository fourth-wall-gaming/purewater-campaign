## What happened

Three accounts came up short on one bridge in one week, for one reason, and the
only person in the outfit who could read a ledger went and looked. That is the
whole of it. Nothing in this run was driven by the scenario's mystery; it was
driven by arithmetic.

- **`d-3`** Collections on the Shoals. Not skimming — *asking*, because the
  Knights take what they like and nobody bills them. Called four of them thieves
  on the Fishmarket bridge in front of forty people, then discovered there is no
  quartermaster and nine weeks of nobody presenting a bill.
- **`d-2`** Went east under a water butt and counted the camp: **twenty-five
  tents, eleven of them cold, forty-one horses**. The occupation was a bluff and
  she was the only person alive who could prove it. Cleared an innocent Sword of
  the Lake out of Marisette's cellar by pointing out that a Pearl boy running at
  four in the morning does not go *east*.
- **`d-1`** Took a charcoal rubbing of the soul-knife's pommel rather than lift
  the knife, and traced the mark to Orsk & Daughter and **the fourth of June** —
  four months before the column reached the city. Refused, to Crowbill's face,
  the arithmetic that said settle with the Baron.
- **`d0`** Ran a snatch on the Lily Garden's water-stair and took the Baron's son
  alive.
- **`d1`** Killed him at first light, because the alternative was a reprisal on
  four thousand people; called Nus off the locket at the Lady's blessing, which is
  why the purge of the canals never happened; built a case and handed it to a
  King's officer for nothing; and beat a man with no breaking point by sending him
  an itemised invoice with his employer priced at four shillings a head.

Ends with a cut ring in her hand on a wet pier, a dead Baron on the ice, a boy
with an open seat in him and four mornings to close it, and *this is Crowbill's
town* said out loud in front of twenty-six people.

## Kag is in the seed as an NPC

She is canon now. The clean seed at the repository root ships her as a fully
written NPC — sheet, agenda, day-by-day timeline — so a future party can meet her.
See `setting/kag-as-an-npc.md` there. The one rule for running her: **how you
talk to her decides what she is.** Address the muscle and she is exactly as much
use as you paid for; ask her what a thing *costs* and you have the best analyst in
Purewater for nothing.
