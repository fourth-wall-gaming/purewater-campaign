The guild of boat-builders, canal engineers, water-diviners, and makers of water-enhanced goods -- the artisans whose enchantments keep the canal-city functioning. They wear indigo sashes, rank marked in silver, blue, or mother-of-pearl.

**Ranks:** Apprentices, Journeymen, Master Crafters, Tide Masters, and a governing council of seven.

**Leadership.** [[Guildmaster Torval Bluehand]] -- middle-aged, practical, forward-thinking, his hands stained permanently blue by decades of watercraft enchantment. He prizes innovation over tradition, which is exactly the trait the Baron has been able to use.

**Stated aim:** advance the craft and protect guild members. **Real aim:** monopolize water technology and magic in Purewater; they secretly research how to harness the power released where lake and sea waters mix.

## Where they stand, Tourney week

**They are fascinated by the wrong thing.** The guild has been studying [[The DragonBarge]] since it moored -- a hull that steams continuously where it meets the water, fire magic held in sustained contact with a sacred channel, which as an engineering problem is the most interesting object any of them has ever seen. Torval Bluehand wants to understand it. He does not want it towed away, and he has said so in guild council.

That appetite is why the fouling assessment went the way it did. The Order's alarm was referred to the guild; **[[Ivo Calder]], Tide Master and the city's appointed assessor, took the reading himself, found it entirely real, and signed it false.** He was asked to, once, politely, by a man he respects at a private dinner of [[The Ember Society]] -- and the reason he has been able to live with it for six weeks is that the guild's own institutional wish was pulling the same way.

**The guild does not know it has been used.** Torval Bluehand believes his assessor's signature. If Calder recants, the guild is publicly humiliated and Bluehand becomes an extremely motivated enemy of the man who arranged it.

## The door

**Any Journeyman on a canal job**, for ordinary business -- repairs, a hired diviner, a water-enchanted tool.

**[[Ivo Calder]]** for the plot: fifty, distinguished, unbribable, and carrying something. He kept his original working notes, which show the fouling as real and bearing north-north-east from the Baron's pier, and he has moved them twice. He will defend the assessment vigorously and fold completely the moment somebody puts an independent second reading in front of him.

**Torval Bluehand** if the party has something technical worth his time -- and nothing gets his attention faster than an offer of access to that hull.

## What they can do for you, and the price

| | |
|---|---|
| **A second reading** | an independent water-divination of the channel: the one piece of evidence that breaks Calder |
| **Water-enchanted gear** | breathing, buoyancy, sealed cases, tools that work wet |
| **Canal engineering** | sluices, levels, and where the water goes -- including under buildings |
| **Boats** | built, hired, or quietly modified |
| **Expertise on the hull** | what is actually happening where that ship touches the water, from the only people studying it |

**The price is craft and monopoly.** They trade knowledge for knowledge, guard their methods jealously, and will charge a guild rate for everything else.

## Friction

Allied with the [[The Order of the Lake Lady]] -- an alliance now sitting on a fault line, since a Watercrafter's signature is what gagged the Order's alarm. Rivals of the dwarven crafters of [[The Forge Quarter]]. Two hundred years of quarrel with [[The King's Assay]], who decide whether guild work may be sold and stamp it accordingly. Professionally contemptuous of the [[The Harbor Masters]], who regulate water they do not understand.

## Hooks

- The party asks for a second reading and does not realise they have just aimed a weapon at the Baron.
- Torval Bluehand offers the party money and gear to get him aboard that ship, or to bring him back measurements.
- Calder is followed, or warned, or found in the water, depending on how loudly the party asks.
- A guild apprentice has noticed something about the barge's steam nobody senior wants written down.

**Agendas on the clock:** none of their own -- but the guild is the hinge of *Keep the Lady's water clean* ([[High Priestess Nerissa]]), because their signature is what closed it.
