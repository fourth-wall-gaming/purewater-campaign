**The set piece of Act III.**

Two hundred paces of marked grass, a crosswind everybody is complaining about,
the Governor's box at the near end, half the city on the slope behind. The one
open event: no colours, no fee, anyone may shoot. Which is why an elf in plain
harness is standing in it.

**Temerach wins.** Let her win -- three days of being stopped on the road and
spoken to in front of her own escort, and then two hundred paces of absolute
silence and she puts them where nobody else in the field can.

**The dice decide what comes after**, standing in front of the box with four
thousand people watching and a prize she has to be handed:

- **She spends it.** Names, out loud, whose men stopped a King's officer on the
  Anminster road and what has been done in this city this week. Thirty years of
  standing converted into one public accusation the Governor cannot pretend not
  to have heard. It buys a hearing. It makes her a marked woman four days early.
- **She holds it.** Takes the prize, says the correct words, walks. Still a
  King's officer with a clean record and a door she can open once. Colder, and
  far more useful later.
- **She loses.** Rarer and better than it sounds. The household's archer takes
  the wreath, and the scene becomes the party watching the one person with
  standing lose her chance to use it.

**This is the scene the standoff answers.**
