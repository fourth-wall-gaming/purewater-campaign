She has been waiting for a moment when he is already asking the right
question. His mother was an elven mage the Baron seduced and broke; she fled to
this city and died here; Temerach has known since before she recruited him.

**If Conall is not a PC in this campaign,** this still fires -- and the party
learns it secondhand, from a Conall who is suddenly a different man.

Bring this forward with `revise-beat` if the players work it out on their own;
there is nothing worse than an NPC withholding a fact the table already has.
