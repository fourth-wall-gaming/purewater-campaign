He collects a PC off a ledge at first light, as he has done to people in this
city for thirty years, and puts them on a cookshop roof to talk.

He already knows about whatever they did last night -- the hour, the tide, how
many. **He does not ask what they found**, which is more unsettling than a
threat and establishes exactly how much of this city he sees.

He does not help people. He trades, and he wants something they will not enjoy
giving.

What he can do that nobody else can: **the Baron wears a chain**, and on it a
locket, and he has never been seen without it. There is exactly one hour in the
week when that man is reachable in a crowd.
