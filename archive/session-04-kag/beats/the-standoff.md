**However the room ends**, Hanzo comes out of it into the open, in front of
the entire city. And on the far side of the arena is an elf with a bow.

It is a standoff and not an assassination **because both of them know.** She
does not shoot from cover. She is a King's officer and she will make it look
like a lawful act even when it is not: she **names him**, across eighty yards,
in front of four thousand people, and gives him the chance to answer -- which is
the one thing he has spent the whole week never having to do.

Then she shoots.

- **She kills him.** The house is broken in public and the war is not over,
  because the credit line was never his.
- **She wounds him.** Arguably the best outcome. Alive, humiliated, and carried
  out through a crowd that watched him refuse to answer.
- **She misses.** Also playable. He answers her, in that warm reasonable voice,
  in front of everyone, and for about four seconds he wins -- and then somebody
  in the crowd throws something, and then everybody does.

**She has to be alive, free and present.** If the archery went badly, or she is
behind a shut door, this beat does not fire and Act V ends without its best
image -- which is the cost of the middle game, paid here.
