The old burial galleries under the Hill quarter: dry, cold, chalk-white,
three centuries of the respectable dead stacked in them. The houses use them
because nobody official goes down there and sound does not carry up.

Two men tied to chairs in the dark with the same face, who have never met.

This is where the twins find out what they are and somebody says the word
*brother* out loud. Marisette's Insight decides whether she believes them fast
or slow; slow means hours in the dark first, and she is right in the end either
way. A witness from the house shortcuts it entirely -- she believes a person who
was there over a face, every time.
