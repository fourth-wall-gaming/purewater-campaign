In the noise, or in the water, or in the wreck of that room, somebody gets
Cailan's body into their own hands, **alive**.

**And he is still not her brother.**

The locket only ever tied Thuban to *Hanzo*. What holds him in the world is a
box of bones in a tower on the border estate, four days' hard riding, behind his
own men. The rite cannot be said. The words that were never said over a knight
of the Sixth Wing still cannot be said.

**The last beat of the adventure is Thuban telling her where they are** -- with
perfect thousand-year-old courtesy, in a seventeen-year-old's voice, because
under pressure his manners get better rather than worse.

This is the hook for the next adventure, and the ache is deliberate. Do not
resolve it. Do not let anyone say the words tonight.
