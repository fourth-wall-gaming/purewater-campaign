**The fuse for `nus-lifts-the-binding-locket`, which the catalog calls the hinge
of the whole adventure -- and it is being handed to Kag to light or not.**

Nus is young, quick, dressed better than he can account for, and has been
circling her for two days. He does not ask permission. He asks a **technical
question**, because that is the flattery he has worked out will land on her and
he is not entirely wrong.

> "Registration. The crush at the tables. If a man was wearing something round
> his neck under his shirt -- could it be had? I'm not saying I would. I'm
> saying could it."

**He has not said whose neck.** He will, if she asks. He will also stop and go
away if she tells him to, because he is bold rather than stupid and she is Kag.

**What she knows that he does not:** what happens to the canals afterwards. Or
rather -- she does not know, but she is the only person in this conversation
capable of working it out, because she has spent three days costing what an
armed company does to a neighbourhood when nobody stops it.

**If she says yes, or says nothing, or says *not the Baron* in a way a
nineteen-year-old hears as a dare** -- `nus-lifts-the-binding-locket` fires at
registration, and `the-purge-of-the-canals` comes after it, and four thousand
people on plank walkways pay for a locket.

**If she says no and means it,** the hinge has to turn some other way and that
is a legitimate campaign. Revise the beat rather than working around her.

**Do not warn her.** No foreshadowing, no significant pause, no NPC saying *are
you sure*. She is the person in this city best equipped to see what it costs.
Let her see it or not.
