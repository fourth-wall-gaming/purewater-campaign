A day short of the city. Four knights, bored, who stop a King's officer
riding in plain harness with no house on her shield -- **because she is an elf,
and because they can.**

Not a fight. The slow, public, entirely deniable humiliation of somebody who
outranks them and cannot use it. They go through her saddlebags. They ask her to
say something in elvish. It goes on far longer than is comfortable to play, and
that is the point.

**She takes it.** Completely still, no answer, eyes front.

If Conall is with her the roll is his Willpower not to intervene; she has told
him not to. **Do not let her win this scene.** Her not winning it here is what
buys the archery and the shot.
