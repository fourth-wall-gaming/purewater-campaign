Two hundred paces of flat grass marked out between the water and the stands,
with the Governor's box at the near end and half the city lying on the slope
behind. Late morning, flat light, and a crosswind off the lake that everybody
complains about and nobody shoots well against.

The archery is **the one open event** -- no house colours required, no fee,
anyone may enter. That is the whole reason it matters: it is the only place in
Purewater during that week where a person with no standing can stand in front of
the Governor and be handed something, in public, and be owed a hearing for as
long as the crowd is watching.
