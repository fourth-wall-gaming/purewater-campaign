## What happened

Magda arrived at the ford a stranger and by the end of it had sworn herself to
Gardwen, killed the Baron's heir with a sling stone and fed him to pigs, allied
with the ruler of the Pearl, taken the DragonBarge in the Mouth, killed Blau,
laid a thousand-year-old murdered knight to rest, and torn the binding locket
off a Baron's throat on a service stair.

**The dice diverged from the written adventure at the first hinge.** Santo's
binding *succeeded* — 19 against a Formidable 25 — so Emmeralda lived, bored and
possessed and chatty, which is the only reason anyone in Purewater ever worked
out what a shade was. The entire back half of the campaign came out of a script
that failed.

Still open at the end: Bettan and the third filled girl; Emmeralda still
carrying her demon; the Awake the Dragon Movement, who kept Thuban's border
tower and lost their candidate; and two bastard di Teufel twins who had just
become extremely interesting to some very bad people.
