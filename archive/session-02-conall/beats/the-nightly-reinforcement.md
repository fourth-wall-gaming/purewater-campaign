Every night, the ritual. Anyone close enough to hear it learns the two
most important facts in the campaign: that the champion is a prisoner, and that
the thing inside him does not want to be there.

Thuban Eta surfaces in these moments. A betrayed knight, enslaved through the
torment of that betrayal -- and a possible ally, if the party can reach him
rather than the boy.

Getting inside for this is the natural use of Crowbill's canal routes.
