STALE PREMISE REVISED. The original beat had Santo returning for a soul-knife the party had taken off him. In this telling he never lost it: the binding failed (36 vs Formidable 25) but his escape succeeded (17/50) and he walked out of the Sylph's Embrace with every instrument he came in with, unseen and unnamed. Nobody is hunting him because nobody knows he exists.

So he is not under pressure and he is not recovering anything. He is a young man who botched the most important working of his life two days before the Tourney, with his father in the city being toasted, and four twists of hair still in his coat.

He goes again, sooner, and he will pick somewhere quieter than a house full of people -- and he has already shown he will travel for a subject, because the twist he spent came off a head that is not on the Pearl.
