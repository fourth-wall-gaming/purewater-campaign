**DORMANT until the Baron -- or anyone who reports to him -- gets a
clear look at Randall's or Conall's face.** Then it activates immediately, at
priority 5, and it does not go dormant again.

**What he wants.** Hanzo seduced and broke an elven mage long before his open
descent; she fled to Purewater and died there. He believes that line ended. It
did not: it forked into identical twins. A living bastard of his blood is two
things at once -- a tool, and a claim. He wants the tool and he wants the claim
extinguished, in that order.

**How he pursues it.** Quietly first: a name, a description, a purse for whoever
supplies both. Then Blau, then the Dragon Knights. He will not announce a bastard
to the city; he will take one off a street at night.

**What changes his mind.** Nothing. Once he knows, he never stops. This is the
single most dangerous thing the party can trigger, and it is triggered by
*being seen* -- which makes every public scene a real decision.

**GM note.** If Santo dies while this is dormant, activate it as soon as anyone
connects the twins' face to the dead heir: with Santo gone the twins are the only
di Teufel blood walking, and that is a succession dagger at Hanzo's name.
