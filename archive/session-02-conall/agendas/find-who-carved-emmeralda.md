**What she wants.** Marisette rules the Pearl. A girl of hers has been
opened up with a ritual knife under a roof she is responsible for. She wants a
name, and then she wants a reckoning that every house in the city hears about.

**How she pursues it.** She has Hesper, an arcane enforcer who does not ask
permission, and she has the only intelligence network in Purewater that hears
what men say when they are not being careful. She grabs first and asks after --
which is exactly how strangers with the wrong face end up bound to chairs in the
catacombs.

**What changes her mind.** She is fair, in her way. Shown the truth she acts on
it, including against a Baron: her most effective weapon is not violence but
withdrawal -- she can close every pleasure house in Purewater to the di Teufel
retinue and make the city notice.

**Hesper's own agenda runs underneath this one.** He serves Marisette and
reports, secretly, to the Quiet Hand.
