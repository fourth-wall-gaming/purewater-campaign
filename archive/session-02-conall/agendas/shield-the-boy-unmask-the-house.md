**What she wants.** Temerach Nebulo of the Swords of the Lake has known
for years whose son Conall is. She brought him to Purewater deliberately. She
wants the di Teufels exposed by lawful means -- and she wants the boy to survive
learning what he is.

**How she pursues it.** Patiently, and with more discretion than the party will
like. She withholds. She is the Lakelady's Javelin and can lend that authority,
but spends it slowly.

**What changes her mind.** Danger to Conall overrides everything, including her
own caution and her Order's protocol.

**She is a resource the players must earn.** Treated as an ally she is
formidable; treated as a superior officer to be managed she closes up.
