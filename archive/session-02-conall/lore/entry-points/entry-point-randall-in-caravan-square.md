**You are Randall.** Born in the Pearl, raised by courtesans who were
kinder than the streets required, and grown into a thief good enough to still
have all his fingers. Tourney week is the best earning of the year: the city
fills with strangers who carry their purses where you can reach them.

Caravan Square, mid-morning, and the crowd is thick enough to work. You have had
a decent hour.

Then the crowd turns, the way a crowd does, and the Dragon Knights come through
the gate -- twenty-odd riders in scale, a covered litter in the middle of them,
and at their head a lord who does not look at anybody.

The guard walking at that lord's stirrup looks straight at you, and goes white as
a sheet.

**What you know.** This city, completely. Every madame, every fence, every canal
that doesn't appear on a map. Asphodel and Mariarta at the Promise of Heaven will
tell you anything they hear. Nus will do anything you ask, which you should
probably think about more than you do.

**What you do not know.** Why a Dragon Knight just looked at your face like he
had seen a ghost. Or a nobleman.

**Opening move:** the guard has seen you and he is still staring. Run, brazen it
out, or vanish into the crowd and find out who these people are.

*Starting location: Caravan Square, Purewater. Game clock: d-3/day.*
