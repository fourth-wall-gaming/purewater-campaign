**You are Magda.** You keep pigs. You are old, you are not remotely
frail, and for eleven nights the death-goddess has put the same dream behind your
eyes: a young woman with a wolf, a burning house, and a knight in dragon-scale
who is already dead but does not know it. Hel does not explain. Hel points.

So you walked. Three weeks of road with a quarterstaff and a staff sling, and
this morning you are at the ford outside Purewater with a crowd of tourney
traffic queueing to cross -- carters, pilgrims, a troupe of tumblers, all of them
funnelling toward a city that smells of salt and standing water.

And there, in the queue, is the woman from the dream. Wolf and all.

**What you know.** Purewater a little, from years ago -- enough to know which
madames rent rooms to women travelling alone and which ones ask questions. The
Sylph's Embrace on the Pearl is Constantine's house; he is decent, and he has
beds.

**What you do not know.** Why Hel wants you here. The dream said "lay the dragon
warrior to rest," which is not the same as kill, and you have been turning that
over for three weeks.

**Opening move:** the woman is right there, and the queue is slow. Speak to her,
or watch her, or let her pass and follow. Your call.

*Starting location: the ford outside Purewater. Game clock: d-3/dawn.*
