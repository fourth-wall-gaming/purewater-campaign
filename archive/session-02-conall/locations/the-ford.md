Where the north road meets the water and everyone entering Purewater from inland has to queue. In tourney week the queue is carters, pilgrims, tumblers and pickpockets, backed up behind whatever armed column has decided it goes first. The city is visible from here as a smear of towers and standing water. Magda and Gardwen meet in this queue.
