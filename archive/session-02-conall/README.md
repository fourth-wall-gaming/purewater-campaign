## What happened

Conall rode in from Anminster three days before the Tourney and spent two days
doing the only thing he does: asking. He read Temerach Nebulo on the verge as the
di Teufel column went past and saw that she was counting faces and did not find
the one she wanted. He walked straight at the man tailing him through the Moist
Oyster and took him off the board with a bought drink — Kerrin, one of Crowbill's,
who had been on him since midday. He bought old Thorne's silence about his own
face, which is how he learned there was a second one wearing it.

Hesper lifted him off a ledge at dawn and put him in front of Crowbill in the
Catacombs, where his Incognito failed by two in plain sight of a sorcerer and made
every true thing about him visible at once. Crowbill declined to say what he
wanted. Conall declined to promise anything he could not keep, and offered instead
to come and say no to his face if it ever came to that. It was accepted.

Then Temerach told him whose son he is.

He searched the room at the Sylph's Embrace and found nothing, because Santo had
walked out with every instrument he came in with. He went to Lost Isle on bad
information, brought Sana out of a tower-stub, took Bettan alive rather than kill
her, lost his rapier to a bare-handed grip and broke contact on a critical Evade
with a hole in his thigh.

He finished the run by taking **Santo di Teufel alive** at the Siren's Call, four
minutes before the knife, and opening him up on an Insight critical: noon on the
day of the Tourney; a hundred years and four generations of the house; a man
called only *the quiet one*; and nine folded sheets in a hand that is not his
father's, copied out of a locked case behind the border rolls. The critical also
gave Conall the hole Santo could not see — that "noon on the day" is the end of a
plan he was never included in. He worked it out in front of the room.

## Why it was reset

The run was archived rather than continued because the scenario underneath it was
rewritten. Two threads had grown in play that the author did not want: **Lost Isle
as part of the Baron's scheme** (a drowned threshold, a thin channel at noon,
anchor-harvesting, hollow men), and **Santo as a serial demonologist** working
through a series of women. Both were GM inventions layered onto the authored
spine, and both are gone in the rewrite: Santo wants one girl and wants her for
himself, and the Baron's plan runs through the Tourney and the Prince, not through
a drowned island.

Everything in this directory is the state as it stood before that change.
The raw session log is `session-logs/session-02-conall-5619508f.jsonl`
(cumulative — it contains the Magda run as well).
