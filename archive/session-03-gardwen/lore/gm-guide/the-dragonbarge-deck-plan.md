**120 feet long, 30 in the beam, six feet of draft.** Shallow enough for the canals
and the Mouth, heavy enough for open sea. Two lateen masts, sweeps for calms, a
carved dragon prow, and the whole hull **steaming faintly where it meets the
water** in a haze that never quite lifts.

**Twenty aboard.** Every one of them knows every other face, which is the single
most important fact about this ship.

---

## Deck 1 — Weather deck (open)

Bow to stern:

1. **Dragon prow and beakhead** -- carved, gilded, and where the steam is thickest.
2. **Forecastle** -- capstan, bell, anchor gear. Crew territory.
3. **Forward gangway (port side)** -- the delivery point. Bread, water, fodder,
   lamp oil, laundry. **Taken at the rail by the cook, who does not let carriers
   past it.** This is the one place a stranger is expected.
4. **Forward hatch** -- down to the cabin deck, forward section.
5. **Deckhouse amidships** -- galley with its stovepipe, and the laundry copper
   beside it. Always warm, always somebody in it.
6. **The awning** -- an open pavilion of dragon-red canvas where the Baron
   receives petitioners in daylight. Deliberately visible from the pier.
7. **THE COMPANIONWAY** -- the single stair down to the after cabins, and the
   whole security model of this ship. **One of the Dragon Circle stands on it at
   every hour of the day and night.** There is no second way aft that does not
   involve the water or the hold.
8. **After deck** -- cleared before first light so **Cailan can be exercised in
   harness with nobody watching**. From the pier you see a masked figure and two
   men, forty minutes, every dawn.
9. **Stern gallery** -- windows, below the taffrail, giving onto the Baron's day
   cabin. *The one approach that bypasses the companionway entirely, and it is
   over water in full view of the after-deck watch.*

## What the plan is *for*

- **The companionway is the adventure.** Aft is thirteen paces from the awning and
  there is a man on the stair. Every approach the party invents has to answer that
  one stair: pass it, distract it, come in through the stern gallery over water,
  or come up from the hold and hope.
- **The hold connects fore to aft and nobody thinks about it.** Deck 3 is the only
  level with no controlled threshold, because who would be down there. Coming up
  from the hold puts you aft of the companionway without ever using it -- and puts
  you next to Cailan's door on the way.
- **Cailan is the deepest point in the ship.** Getting him out means the longest
  possible route with the least possible air, carrying a seventeen-year-old who
  has been opened every morning for ten years.
- **The dawn drill is a forty-minute window** in which the after deck is cleared,
  the Baron is on deck watching, and the after cabins are as empty as they ever
  get.
- **Delivery day is the other window.** The cook is at the forward rail, the galley
  is unattended, and the forward hatch is standing open for the stores.
