**You are Gardwen.** Ten years ago Dragon Knights came through your
family's steading at night and left nothing standing. You survived because you
were small and the woods took you in. Your brother's body was never among the
dead -- which has been the whole shape of your life since.

Three weeks ago you picked up a column of them on the north road, twenty or
thirty riding under di Teufel colours toward a tournament in a canal city. You
have followed at a distance the old powers let you keep. Ruhi walks at your heel
and the ground does not tell anyone you are coming.

Now the column has crossed the ford ahead of you and you are stuck in the tourney
crowd behind it, watching the last of the dragon-scale disappear toward the city
gates -- and there is a covered litter in the middle of them that the outriders
will not let anyone near.

**What you know.** Woodcraft, healing, and the shape of the wound you carry.
Nothing at all about cities.

**What you do not know.** That an old woman leaning on a staff three places back
in the queue has been dreaming about your face for eleven nights.

**Opening move:** you can push on after the column, or you can deal with the fact
that someone in this queue is staring at you.

*Starting location: the ford outside Purewater. Game clock: d-3/dawn.*
