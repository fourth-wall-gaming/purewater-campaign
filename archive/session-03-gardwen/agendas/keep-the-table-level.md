**What he wants.** Crowbill is a wiry, forgettable man with a crow
tattoo who owns the canals nobody maps. He does not want the city cleaned up; he
wants it *predictable*. Tournament week is a profitable week.

**How he pursues it.** Information first. He knows what is happening before the
Watch does, and he corrects people who lie to him -- calmly, once, to establish
that it is not worth trying twice.

**What changes his mind.** He has a line, and demon-craft worked on a helpless
girl beside the Lady's water crosses it: that is not crime, it is a monster
kicking the whole table over. Bring him proof rather than a grievance and he will
open canals, safe houses, men, and the street's rumour-machine.

**His price is always the same:** something he can use. Intelligence on the
Baron's plan, a route into a fortified estate, or control of a story the street
is already telling.
