**What he wants.** Tat Atarer is a mercenary captain, not a fanatic. The
Dragon Knights are a company with a contract. He wants the tournament fee, his
men alive, and no part of whatever is going on behind the Baron's inner doors.

**How he pursues it.** Professional soldiering, and a careful blindness.

**What changes his mind.** Arithmetic. Show him the contract is going to cost him
men or reputation -- or show him he is guarding something that will get the
company hanged -- and he becomes negotiable in a way no other Dragon Knight is.

**He is the crack in the Baron's wall,** and the players are unlikely to notice
him unless the GM lets his reluctance show.
