Ila is thirteen. She found Emmeralda. In four days not one adult in that house has asked her a single question about what she saw, and she has spent those four days sitting on the front step working out that nobody is going to.

She is not waiting any more. She went out of the front door of the Sylph's Embrace during the commotion in the back lane on the night before the Tourney, pulled it to behind her, and left her cloak on the hook.

She is two segments in. She has been thinking about this for four days and she knows the island better than anyone looking for her.
