**What he wants.** Commander Vask -- Blau to the men -- is the albino
enforcer whose actual job is keeping the heir's appetites from costing the
family anything. He wants a quiet tournament week and a Baron who never has to
hear about it.

**How he pursues it.** He is genuinely good at this: he pays people off, he
hauls Santo home by the collar, he notices lies. He is not fooled by disguise
easily and he does not panic. When containment fails, he escalates fast and
brings knights.

**What changes his mind.** Evidence that the mess is bigger than Santo. If the
party can show him his young master has been dealing with Hell in a way that
endangers the Baron's own plans, Blau's loyalty tilts toward the father and away
from the son -- he is a servant of the house, not of the boy.

**If Santo dies,** this agenda converts to vengeance and Blau becomes the party's
most persistent pursuer.
