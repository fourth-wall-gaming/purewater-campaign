**What she wants.** The Lake Lady's Tourney is the year's holy business
and the High Priestess is responsible for it. Infernal work anywhere near the
water is desecration and she will treat it as such.

**How she pursues it.** Ritual, authority and public standing. She can deny the
Baron entry, bless or refuse a champion, and command the Swords of the Lake --
but she needs cause she can defend in front of the city and Prince Emeric.

**What changes her mind.** Evidence, in the open. She cannot act on a rumour
carried by strangers; she can act decisively on a demon-marked blade laid on her
altar in front of witnesses.

**She is the party's best lever against the tournament itself** -- and the
hardest to reach, because reaching her means being credible.
