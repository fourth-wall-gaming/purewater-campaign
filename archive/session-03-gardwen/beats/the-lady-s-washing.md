Nine of the Order inside the rope, all named by Nerissa. Gardwen is one of them, in blue-green. Sana keeps the jar at the left shoulder.

A count of FORTY per man, eleven men. Gardwen's stated plan is to LOOK ONLY -- Spirit Sight on Cailan, which is Concentration and needs no touch, and to see whether anything live is being carried within sight of the steps. She has ruled out speaking to the passenger because the Baron will be standing there.

IN RESERVE, not used unless asked for: Sana's fouled-draw. A washing drawn bad must be drawn again from the beginning -- it is in the book, Warden Pol makes novices learn it aloud, and the water in this city has been fouled for two years. Nobody can argue with a bad draw and nobody can prove one. Worth eighty seconds, or a hundred and twenty if she is clumsy about it.

WHO IS WATCHING: Hanzo, Blau (who counted the knot of blue-green at the rail the previous afternoon), the Baron's retinue, whoever the Crown sends. Nine women inside the rope and not one of them can fight.
