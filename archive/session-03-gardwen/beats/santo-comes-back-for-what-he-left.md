STALE PREMISE REVISED. The original beat had Santo returning to carry Emmeralda off to the barge. She died before dawn (his binding failed on a spent scroll; her Endurance failed at Hard), so there is nobody to take. What is left in that room is a hanging matter in his father's own handwriting.

He is not grieving. He is nineteen and terrified, and he has worked out -- correctly -- that the knife, the burnt scroll and his own crested coat are the only things in Purewater that connect a dead courtesan to the name di Teufel.

So he comes back, or sends someone, between the noon rest and the evening trade. If the house has already been searched properly he finds bare boards, and that is worse: it means somebody has them.

He does not yet know that Marisette's people are already hunting the man with that face, and that the Pearl closed to the entire di Teufel retinue before breakfast.
