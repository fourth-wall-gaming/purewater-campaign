He cannot go himself. Blau put him on a boat in front of thirty people this afternoon and there is a man on his door tonight.

So he sends, or he pays somebody to send. ROLL IT -- whoever he gets is as good as the money and the hour allow, against Constantine's house, which has been robbed once this week already and has men in it now.

He is looking for a burnt sheet of vellum and a knife. The sheet is NOT in that house any more -- Gardwen is carrying it. Success therefore establishes only that somebody turned the upstairs room over a second time, and failure establishes somebody caught in Constantine's house at night, which is a person who can be asked questions.

Either outcome advances 'Get it back before my father finds out' toward the thing it is really counting down to: Santo having to tell him.
