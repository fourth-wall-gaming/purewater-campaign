The hinge of the whole adventure. In the crowd at tournament
registration, Nus takes the binding locket off Baron Hanzo di Teufel.

**Only fires if someone asks him to,** or if the party has left the idea lying
around where he could pick it up. Nus is bold, not omniscient -- if nobody points
him at the Baron, he robs someone safer and this beat should be revised or
cancelled.

Once the locket is out of Hanzo's hands, the daily reinforcement gets harder:
advance *Wake a Dragon King* nothing, but stall *Field the champion* -- and start
the purge clock.
