A quiet boat, a sealed packet, and the reminder that Hanzo is not the
top of this pyramid. The courier carries instructions about the champion and a
line of credit that explains where the tournament money came from.

Intercepting this is the party's only chance to learn the shape of what is behind
the Baron -- and to understand that killing him does not end it.
