# Chapter 14 — What Is Still Open

Four days.

An old woman came down out of the north on eleven nights of the same dream and
walked into a city she half remembered. She swore herself to a stranger at a
ford. She killed a Baron's heir with a stone and fed him to hogs. She took a
ship in the Mouth, killed the man who ran it, and tore a hundred-year-old latch
off a nobleman's throat on a service stair while six thousand people booed a
champion who was never going to come.

And she stood in black water and gave a murdered knight back to the goddess who
takes the ones nobody sings about, because it turned out that all it had ever
needed was one person to turn up.

That is what closed.

Here is what did not.

---

**Bettan is still in a room on the Northern Isles.** Fourteen, cheerful, and
carrying something that came off a threshold under a drowned island. Two of the
three girls came out and are sleeping. Hesper has not said anything about the
third, and Magda destroyed the only living map to how any of it was done,
because a man was pleasing himself and she had an axe.

Nerissa said what that was and she was right, and she said it in front of twelve
Swords, and she will not take it back. She will also not be welcoming Magda on
Temple Isle again. Both of those things are true and neither cancels the other.

**Emmeralda is still in the green room.** Comfortable, charming, asking after
people by name and requesting her shawl. What is seated in her likes the house
and likes being fussed over and has spent thirty hours learning how Hesper's
mind works. Nobody has any idea how to get it out, and the man who might have
known is in a sack.

**The Awake the Dragon Movement lost a candidate, not a purpose.** They funded
Hanzo. They kept the texts and the money and the patience, and they still hold
his border tower — a hundred years of that family's work sitting empty tonight
with nobody in it. A courier came down the sea road for that ship two nights
ago. Somebody will read what came back.

**And there are two young men standing in a tavern looking at each other.**

Bastards do not inherit. But Santo di Teufel is dead and Hanzo will be
attainted, and when a Crown disposes of a border march it looks for blood
because it is tidier than a garrison. There are men in three counties who will
hear *the di Teufel bastard* and start doing arithmetic.

Neither of them has any protection at all except an elven knight who lied to one
of them for three years, and an Order that owes them nothing.

---

**And there is a stair.**

It is uncovered now, and swept, and the weed has been cut back off the treads,
and everyone who put it there is dead or roped or gone.

It goes down into water that does not hold lamplight. It is a door for the
drowned, older than the Lake Lady's presence in that channel, in no doctrine the
High Priestess has read in forty years.

The Mermaid's Court has been keeping the small confused things near its
threshold since before the causeway, and three men with a lantern came down and
took two of them and nobody could stop it.

Somebody has now opened that door twice in one night and it worked both times.

Thorne is still owed the true version. All of it, in order, told once and never
written down.

He is going to want it soon.
