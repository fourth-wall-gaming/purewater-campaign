Purewater is built on water and admits it.

There is no ground. There are buildings, and there is water, and between them
there are ledges a yard wide with no rail. The traffic that matters goes
underneath, poled and rowed and shouting. The city drinks from a channel that
runs down out of the lake and through the temple on Temple Isle, and the
priestesses of the Lake Lady say the water is the goddess and mean it plainly.

Once there were Dragon Kings. They are gone and every child knows it. What is
left of them is armour on the border lords, a name on a barony, and men who
would like the old power back badly enough to pay for it.

The Lake Lady's Tourney comes once a year. Archery, single combat, and a
scramble through the canals. It is older than the Governor's office. For three
days the city fills with strangers and nobody looks closely at anybody.

This is what happened in those three days.
