Monochrome ink wash and brush drawing on damp rag paper. Black ink and its dilutions ONLY -- no colour anywhere. Built from tonal washes, not line: shapes read dark-against-light or light-against-dark rather than being outlined. Visible brush loading, strokes that begin wet and heavy and end dry and split; wet-in-wet blooms and back-runs where washes meet; dry-brush for wet stone and rope and weathered timber; soft gradient for fog, breath, steam and lamp-haze. Full tonal range from bare untouched paper to ONE area of near-solid black. Highlights are RESERVED PAPER, never white paint. Detail concentrates in one small area and everything else is suggested and left alone. Generous unworked margins, subject placed off-centre, three clear depths: a dark near-silhouette framing one edge, a mid-tone where the subject is, pale paper behind. Register is Rembrandt brown-ink sketch and sumi-e, a travelling artist observing from a little way off. Setting: a pre-industrial canal city with no roads and no horses -- buildings rise straight out of the water, doors open onto steps going under the surface, flat-bottomed poled boats, wet stone, oil lamps and lanterns as the only light. Working dress: leather, wool, aprons, rolled sleeves, bare feet. NO colour. NO pen crosshatching. NO engraving, woodcut or scraperboard look. NO comic outlines. NO photoreal or 3D render, no digital glow or lens flare. NO white gouache highlights. NO text, captions, speech bubbles, signatures or logos. NO horses, carts, wheels, roads, firearms or modern objects.

SCENE: A frosty open meadow camp at midday, seen from inside the camp. Rows of pointed military tents receding, a horse line, a farrier's banked fire making a soft smudge of smoke, a wagon with a wheel off. Walking down the slope INTO the camp, an enormous half-orc woman carries a big wooden water butt on one shoulder with a hand on the rim, head down, at the trudge of somebody who would rather not be. Nearer the viewer a soldier at a cook-line has already gone back to his bowl and a boy with an armful of tack is stepping aside WITHOUT LOOKING AT HER FACE. Nobody looks at her face -- that is the subject. Near-solid dark wash in the foreground figures and tent shadows; the woman and the barrel in clean mid-tone against pale reserved paper for the cold sky. Frost suggested by bare paper in the grass.
