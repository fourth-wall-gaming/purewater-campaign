# Chapter 19 — The Reversal

HESPER HAD NOT LEFT THAT GREEN ROOM IN THIRTY HOURS AND WHEN HE FINALLY
came out of it he had the expression of a man who has just told a demon that he
will be back shortly and been told *of course you will, dear.*

He had bought it a syllable at a time and he laid it out in a passage on the
Pearl with the door shut behind him.

"You cannot simply pull a shade out and let it go. It has to have somewhere to
be, even for a moment, or it goes into the nearest living thing standing over it
— which will be one of you.

"So a removal needs three parts. The door. The *thing it comes out of*. And the
**anchor** — something that already holds a piece of him, that he'll consent to
sit in while he's between."

He looked at her.

"The Baron carries a locket. It's the small anchor, the working one, and he's
had it on his person since before they left the border. That's what keeps the
daily ritual holding.

"But that's not the primary. And the thing in that room told me what is, this
morning, while complaining about how much *nicer* it would be to have a proper
one."

He took a breath.

"They've got his bones. Thuban's. In the Baron's border tower, where they've
been for a hundred years, because you cannot hold a man like that with anything
less."

Magda laughed. It was not a good laugh.

"Well. How the fuck are we going to do that?"

---

Hesper did not laugh back. He looked at her for a moment and then something
happened behind his eyes.

"Wait. Say the bit about the quiet one again. The timing."

"Gone since before dawn yesterday. Ellick reckoned four hours out to the border
tower and four back with a fair wind."

"And it's been thirty-six."

His hands came up.

"So he is not *at* the tower. He has been there, and he is on his way *back*,
and he is late because he is carrying something heavy and he will not risk it in
bad water."

Gardwen got there half a beat before Magda did.

"He's bringing the bones *here*."

"He has to." Hesper was talking fast now. "They cannot do a removal without the
primary anchor, and the primary anchor has sat in a border tower for a century
because nobody dared move it. But they've run out of boy. So for the first time
in a hundred years, Thuban Eta's bones are out of that tower and on the water,
and they are coming to Purewater, and they are going to the one place the work
can be done.

"Which is a stair under Lost Isle that you are already sitting on with a dozen
Swords of the Lake."

Nobody said anything for a second.

Then Hesper added it, quietly, and it landed like a stone:

"And Magda — you were never going to be able to lay that man to rest without
them. You know that. You've buried enough people."

"You don't put a man down without his bones."

"No. You don't."

He almost smiled, and it was an awful smile, because he is a man who has just
watched a problem turn inside out.

"They have spent a hundred years keeping the one thing you need locked in a
tower you could never reach.

"And tonight they are rowing it to you."
