The campaign opens by drawing four strangers together in the days before the tourney -- and one elf who is not one of them, and is quietly moving at least two of them:

- **[[Gardwen]]**, sole survivor of a Dragon Knight massacre a decade past, follows the Baron's column to Purewater seeking the brother who vanished that night. Her wolf [[Ruhi]] walks beside her; the old powers keep her footfalls silent.
- **[[Magda]]**, an old pig-farmer and warrior of **Hel**, is sent by the death-goddess's nightmares to protect the others and "lay the dragon warrior to rest." She finds Gardwen at the city's ford and becomes her curmudgeonly bodyguard.
- **[[Randall]]**, a Purewater street-thief raised in the Pearl, is mistaken for the noble's son **Santo** at the city gate -- and then framed for the carving of [[Emmeralda]].
- **[[Conall]]**, an arcane investigator warranted by [[The King's Assay]] and riding in with the knight [[Temerach Nebulo]], is Randall's identical twin -- and, unknown to him at first, the Baron's son.

The twins meet bound side by side in [[The Catacombs]], taken by Hesper for the face of the man who went upstairs with Emmeralda, until Magda and Gardwen -- who saw the man on the stair, his burnt hand and his coat -- clear them. Out of that cellar four strangers tied by fate forge an alliance: *"We could. We should." -- "Justice."* The twins' shared parentage, and Conall's, unspool from there.
