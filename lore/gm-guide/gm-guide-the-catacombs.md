Nobody in Purewater could draw this. Everyone knows a piece of it: the houses know
the galleries, the smugglers know their runs, and the Order knows its deep stair
and keeps quiet. **This is the whole of it, for the GM.** It is drawn over the city
map in `maps/catacombs/catacombs-overlay.jpg`, the plan is in
`maps/catacombs/plan.yaml`, and the two drawn sheets are
`maps/catacombs/the-ossuary.jpg` and `maps/catacombs/the-oldwater.jpg`. Never letter any of it on a player map.

## The Ossuary -- the dry galleries under the Hill

The North Quarter rises at its west end: a hump of chalk the city calls **the
Hill**, which is why the island is "High" Isle and why the rich live there. Under
it are the **old burial galleries**, dry, cold and chalk-white, cut three
centuries ago and filled with the respectable dead. A spine gallery follows the
ridge from the Pearl end toward the Processional, with side galleries off it, and
one wide chamber near the middle that the houses call **the Chair Room**. It is
the only room down there with a door that bars from outside.

**The houses use it** because nobody official goes down there and sound does not
carry up. Their way in is a passage from the **Mother of Pearl's cellar**, cut
under the narrow water from the Pearl to the Hill. That is how Hesper brought the
twins in without carrying them over a bridge (*Two men with one face*, *Hesper
takes the look-alike*). The other ways in are the **ossuary chapel** in the North
Quarter, sealed by the council a generation ago and opened by anyone with a crowbar,
and the **undercroft of the Old Span**, a boatman's way in at water level.

## The Old Water -- older than the city

Before there was a city, the Lake's outflow ran in one channel from the spring
under Temple Isle to the sea. The city was built over it, and the channel did not
stop. It runs, flooded and roofed, from **the Deep Pool** beneath the Sacred
Confluence, south under the narrow water, under the **Tournament Ground** (where
it feeds **the Lists Cistern**), under the east end of the Grand Canal, under the
Forges (**the Warm Pool**), and out to the sea at the piles of the private pier
where **the DragonBarge** lies. A branch runs north-east from the Deep Pool to the
drowned ruins of Lost Isle.

**This is the corruption's road.** The barge sits on the mouth of the Old Water,
and the fire it sheds goes up the channel, not round it. That is why [[Ivo Calder]]'s
original notes show the fouling **bearing north-north-east from the Baron's pier**:
the line runs straight to the Confluence. The Warm Pool under the Forges is why the
yards' water steams in the morning when it did not used to. A party that finds
the Old Water can prove the fouling's path without a diviner, simply by walking
it and feeling the heat rise toward the barge.

The untouched pools along it are the ones with "strange magical properties". The
**Mermaid's Court** knows this water better than anyone alive and comes and goes
by the Lost Isle branch. The Order keeps one way down, **the temple's deep stair**,
and lets nobody use it (the encounter table's "somebody praying at the deep pool
who should not have access" means this stair). The **Lists' water-gate sluice**
opens onto the cistern, and the Harbor Run's course passes over it.

## The smugglers' runs -- Crowbill's

No bridge reaches the Lullwater, and yet contraband flows "from one straight into
the other". It does so underground. From the **Moist Oyster's back room**, which
already "sits right on the water", a run goes south under the strait to **Marn's
ice-house** on the Shoals, where Crowbill sits at two in the morning, and on to the
**smugglers' jetties**. A second run goes west from the Lullwater under the water
to the Forges and breaks into the Old Water near the Warm Pool. Crowbill's people
know that last stretch is getting hot, and do not know why.

These are the hidden boat-paths and "safe houses ... including the Catacombs" in
Crowbill's faction entry. They are low, wet and tide-dependent. At the spring tides
they flood, and anyone who does not know the hours drowns.

## Rumoured, never found

- **The Governor's Stair.** A passage said to run from the Ossuary east under the
  North Quarter to the Palazzo's undercroft and on to the Old Water. If it exists,
  the Governor's house has a way to the Lists that nobody watches.
- **Under the Grand Canal.** Every generation someone swears there is a tunnel from
  the Hill to Market Square. Nobody has found it, and the canal is very deep.
