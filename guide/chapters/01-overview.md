# Overview

Baron Hanzo di Teufel brings his Dragon Knights to Purewater for the Lake Lady's Tourney, with a champion who rides in a laced-shut litter and is never seen. The champion is a stolen seventeen-year-old named Gabriel, called Cailan, and inside him the Baron has held the spirit of a thousand-year-old dragon-knight, Thuban Eta, for ten years. If the champion wins the Single Combat, he is presented alone and unarmed to Prince Emeric, the King's nephew, behind a closed door, by a rite older than the city's government. In that room the Baron means to draw Thuban out of the boy and seat him in the Prince.

The characters arrive the same day as the Baron. That night the Baron's spoiled heir, Santo, tries to seat a spirit in a courtesan named Emmeralda with a scroll stolen from his father's case. Whether he succeeds or fails, he leaves a trail that two of the characters witness and the other two are arrested for, because they wear his face. Over five days the characters learn who did it, discover that knowing is worth nothing in a city whose watch has been ordered to stand aside, and find their way to the locket the Baron wears on his chain: the leash that binds Thuban to him. What they do about that locket decides how the week ends. The Baron may take the Prince quietly, take him in the smoke of a burning quarter, or lose his champion to the thing inside it.

At the end of the scenario the characters know what the Baron is, who funds him, and where Thuban's bones lie. Whatever happens in Purewater, the only way to lay the dragon-knight to rest lies four days away, in a tower on the Baron's border estate.

## Non-Player Characters

- **Baron Hanzo di Teufel:** A charming, patient borderland lord and the last complete practitioner of a forbidden school of binding. He wants to be a Dragon King, and Prince Emeric's body is the next step.
- **Cailan (Gabriel):** The Baron's champion, a boy taken from a burning steading ten years ago. Gardwen's brother. He is rarely present in his own body.
- **Thuban Eta:** A knight of the Sixth Wing of the old dragon armies, drowned by his own company for refusing an order. Bound into Cailan by his own bones and the Baron's locket; courteous, grieving, and unwilling.
- **Santo di Teufel:** The Baron's legitimate heir, nineteen, who has never been told no by anybody who mattered. He wants one girl, Emmeralda, and he has one stolen scroll.
- **Blau (Commander Vask):** The Baron's albino enforcer. Patient, unfoolable, and good at keeping the heir's messes from costing the house anything.
- **Tat Atarer:** Captain of the Dragon Knights, a mercenary who fights for pay and does the arithmetic on what the Baron's private business is costing his company.
- **Temerach Nebulo:** An elf knight of the Swords of the Lake and the order's spymaster in Purewater. She has known for years whose son Conall is, and she runs people; she is not recruited by them.
- **Marisette:** The madame who rules the Pearl. A woman was wronged under one of her roofs and she wants a name.
- **Hesper:** Marisette's mage-enforcer, who takes first and asks afterwards, and who secretly serves the Quiet Hand.
- **Emmeralda:** The finest dancer on the Pearl, and the object of Santo's working.
- **Crowbill:** The crime lord of the Lullwater, who wants the city predictable and draws a line at demon-craft worked on the helpless.
- **Nus:** A young pickpocket who wants one big score and a name. Randall's friend.
- **Sinnit:** Randall's old enemy, a Lullwater canal-rat who has nursed a seven-year grudge and sells whatever he sees.
- **Orrin Sculle:** The champion's keeper aboard the DragonBarge, who has watched the boy for ten years and told nobody what he knows.
- **Prince Emeric:** The King's nephew and Governor of Purewater: young, decent, badly advised, and the Baron's true target.
- **Marda Blackwater:** Leader of the Dredgers, three hundred dock labourers with a grievance and no lawful way to settle it.

## Key Points/Timeline

1. Three days before the Tourney, the Baron's column enters Purewater. A guard at his stirrup mistakes Randall for Santo, and Sinnit corners Conall in the Lullwater and mistakes him for Randall.
2. That night Santo works his stolen scroll on Emmeralda at the Sylph's Embrace. It either takes, and he walks her out of the house, or it fails, and he cuts her open and runs. Magda and Gardwen, lodging in the house, see the man on the stair and his burnt hand.
3. Before dawn, Hesper takes both men in the city who wear the attacker's face. In the catacombs, Magda and Gardwen clear them, and the four characters meet.
4. Conall investigates the room with the King's Assay's warrant and secures the evidence before Santo's men return for it.
5. Thwarted, Santo loses his temper with fire on the Pearl street, and the city learns his name. The Pearl closes its doors to the Dragon Knights.
6. At the presentation of the entrants, the champion's helm comes off and Gardwen recognises her brother.
7. On the first day of the Tourney, Temerach wins the Archery. That night Gardwen goes aboard the DragonBarge and learns what is inside her brother and what holds it there.
8. On the second day the champion wins the Single Combat. That night Nus and Randall steal the locket from beside the sleeping Baron.
9. On the last day, the theft decides the ending: the Baron takes the Prince quietly; or he burns the Lullwater to recover the locket and takes the Prince in the smoke; or Thuban, unleashed, breaks free and escapes the city with the characters.
10. Every ending points to the same place: Thuban's bones, in the Baron's border tower.

## Areas to be Covered

- **Purewater:** A canal city of islands across the mouth of a sacred lake. The scenario does not leave it.
- **Caravan Square and the Gate Bridge:** The city's only land approach, where the Baron rides in.
- **The Pearl:** The pleasure island, under Marisette's rule. The Sylph's Embrace, where Santo works his scroll, and the Mother of Pearl, Marisette's house, stand on its quay.
- **The Lullwater:** The bridgeless thieves' quarter, home to Crowbill, Sinnit, Nus and the Dredgers' Hall. On the last day it may burn.
- **The Catacombs:** Burial galleries under the Hill quarter, where the houses take people they want to question.
- **The Shoals:** The docks and fish-markets, and the water-stairs the Dredgers can stop.
- **The Merchant's Quarter:** Counting-houses and the palazzi of the councillors who signed the standing order.
- **The Forges and the DragonBarge:** The foundry quarter, and the Baron's steaming ship at its private pier. The champion lives below the waterline, and the Baron sleeps aft.
- **The Lists and Temple Isle:** The Tournament Ground, the Long Butts where the Archery is shot, and the holy island across the water.
- **The Vantt Estate:** A councillor's water-meadow two miles east of the walls, where the Dragon Knights are camped.
