# Conclusion

Purewater ends at dusk on the last day of the Tourney, or in the dark just after it, on one of three roads. None of them is a victory over the Baron. The best of them takes his champion from him and sets the characters on the road to the bones, and the worst leaves the Crown's nephew carrying a dragon-knight while nobody in the kingdom would believe the people who know it.

## If the Locket Is Never Taken

The Baron walks his champion into the presentation room at the closing, and the Prince walks out. Nothing burns and nobody dies in public. The Tourney closes with prizes, the Dragon Knights march home the next morning, and the standing order lapses with nobody quite remembering who signed it.

**What the characters hold:** whatever evidence they gathered (the scroll in the Baron's hand, Conall's finding, Ivo Calder's notes) against a man who now has a prince to vouch for him. Cailan was carried out of the room emptied. If the characters can reach him, he is a seventeen-year-old who cannot stand, coming apart without the seat that held him together, and Gabriel may surface for longer than a second, because there is nobody else in there now.

**What follows:** a pursuit and a conspiracy. The Prince returns to the King's court at Caldreth wearing Thuban, and the Baron's friends at court have been his friends for thirty years. The bones are still in the border tower, and they still hold Thuban in the world, now in a far more dangerous body.

## If the Locket Is Taken and Recovered

The Lullwater burns. Nus is dead at Blau's hand, unless the characters changed who paid. The Prince summons the Baron to answer for the smoke and is taken in the room he summoned him to. Temerach's shot wounds the Baron in front of four thousand people, and she leaves the city an outlaw, with the characters.

**What the characters hold:** a wounded enemy who was seen, by the whole city, to refuse to answer; a King's officer who threw away thirty years of standing on one shot; and the knowledge of what is now wearing the Prince. The Lullwater will not forget who burned it, and Marda Blackwater's three hundred, Crowbill's people and the Pearl have all now seen what the Baron is. In Purewater, at least, nobody will doubt the characters again.

**What follows:** the road out, and the tower. Temerach knows the border country, and she has nothing left to lose.

## If the Locket Is Taken and Kept

The characters keep Nus alive and keep the locket from Blau through a day of fire, and the Baron goes into the evening without his leash. Thuban breaks free of a decade of bondage and cuts his way out through the Dragon Knights, with Gabriel's body and Gabriel's voice, and he leaves Purewater beside the woman who came aboard and spoke to him in her brother's name. The Prince is untouched. The Baron has lost his champion, his proof and his room.

**What the characters hold:** the locket, which they cannot use and cannot easily destroy, and which the Baron will want back more than anything in the world; Thuban, a courteous, grieving and terrifying ally who wants only to die properly; and Gabriel, still underneath him.

**What follows:** the hardest ride of the campaign, to a tower on the Baron's own border estate, with the Baron and everything he has left behind them.

## What Comes Next: the Bones

All three roads lead to the same place. Thuban's bones lie in a box in a tower on the Baron's border estate, four days' hard riding from Purewater, behind the Baron's own men. Until the bones are found and the rite that was never said over a knight of the Sixth Wing is finally said, Thuban stays in the world and Gabriel stays under him.

Magda's dream told her to lay the dragon warrior to rest, which is not the same as killing him. Gardwen came to Purewater to bring her brother home. Laying Thuban to rest in that tower is the only way to do both, and it is the next adventure.

**Loose threads to carry forward:**

- **The Movement** is untouched. It holds the credit line and the texts, and a failure at Purewater costs it a season, not a life's work. It is already looking for its next candidate.
- **The twins** are the Baron's only other blood, and if he knows of them he wants them: the tool first, then the claim extinguished.
- **Santo,** if he lives, is a frightened young man with a burnt hand and a father who has stopped forgiving him.
- **Emmeralda,** if the working took, still has a small hungry spirit seated in her, and only a binding school or a powerful Dispel Magic will draw it out.
- **Blau** has spent a week watching the characters, and he does not forget faces.
- **Tat Atarer** has done the arithmetic. A company that has seen what was behind the inner doors may not ride for the Baron again.

## Experience

Award three Experience Rolls for completing the scenario, and one more for each of the following: Nus alive at the end; the Lullwater not burned; Marda's grievance answered without bloodshed; the Pearl's rule upheld; Thuban freed of the Baron. Review every character's Passions. Purewater is a scenario about what people love and fear, and most characters should leave it with at least one Passion changed by ten percentage points or more, and some with a new one.
