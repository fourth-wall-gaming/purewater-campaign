# The City Closes

Two days and one day before the Tourney: the face is arrested twice, the four become a party, and the Pearl draws a line.

## Hesper takes the look-alikes

<!-- beat: hesper-takes-the-look-alike -->

Marisette's arcane enforcer takes both men in the city wearing the face of the man who went upstairs with Emmeralda, and puts them in chairs in the catacombs.

## Two men with one face

<!-- beat: the-catacombs-two-men-one-face -->

Randall and Conall, tied side by side, are questioned together by Marisette; Magda and Gardwen clear them with what they saw -- and the four meet for the first time.

## Conall reads the room

<!-- beat: conall-reads-the-room -->

Demon-craft worked on a woman under a Purewater roof is the King's Assay's business, and Conall carries its warrant: he goes to the Sylph's Embrace to establish, record and refer -- and gets there before Santo's men do.

## A boat taken off the water-stair

<!-- beat: a-boat-taken-off-the-water-stair -->

Four Knights commandeer a working skiff with its boatman still in it -- the third this week, and the last two boatmen have not come back.

## Santo comes back for her

<!-- beat: santo-comes-back-for-her -->

The spirit took but he was stopped on the stair; a boat, two hired men, and a second try at getting a willing girl off the Pearl.

## Sinnit tells the Pearl it was Randall

<!-- beat: sinnit-tells-the-pearl-it-was-randall -->

The morning Hesper takes the twins, Sinnit tells every stair and wine-shop that it was Randall who carved the girl -- and on Randall's own ground, a lie told loudly enough outlives the truth.

## Santo's tantrum on the Pearl street

<!-- beat: santo-s-tantrum-on-the-pearl-street -->

Thwarted, Santo screams threats at the Sylph's Embrace in the open street and puts fire into its front before Blau hauls him away -- and the whole Pearl learns his name.

## The Pearl closes to the Dragon Knights

<!-- beat: the-houses-close-to-the-di-teufels -->

On Constantine's complaint, Marisette shuts every house on the Pearl to the di Teufel retinue -- and only to them.

## Temerach tells Conall whose son he is

<!-- beat: temerach-tells-conall-whose-son-he-is -->

In a stable, out of earshot, the knight finally hands over the truth she has held for years.

## The stairs stop

<!-- beat: the-stairs-stop -->

Marda Blackwater pulls three hundred dock labourers off the water-stairs on the eve of the Tourney, and the whole city's provisioning stops with them.

## Maro Quist reads it to the end

<!-- beat: quist-reads-it-to-the-end -->

The magistrate reads everything put in front of him, at his own pace, while you wait -- and then explains accurately why it is not his to act on.

## The presentation of the entrants

<!-- beat: the-presentation-of-the-entrants -->

The day before the Tourney, every entrant is presented to Prince Emeric. The champion appears -- and the helm comes off.

## Alderic Vantt jokes about his cellar

<!-- beat: alderic-vantt-jokes-about-his-cellar -->

The warmest man in the city makes two self-deprecating jokes in a row, which is where the seam is.

## Sinnit sells the face

<!-- beat: sinnit-sells-the-face -->

Sinnit takes what he knows to the Vantt camp: there are two men in Purewater with the young lord's face, and one of them is a thief. Blau pays him, and the Baron learns he has more sons than he thought.
