# Introduction

*And Then the Dragons Came* is a scenario for *Classic Fantasy Imperative*, for four characters, set in Purewater: a canal city where the sacred waters of the Lake Lady meet the sea. Baron Hanzo di Teufel arrives with his Dragon Knights for the Lake Lady's Tourney, and his champion rides in a litter nobody is allowed to approach. Four strangers arrive the same week. Five days later the city has either been saved from him or handed to him.

This book is for the Games Master. Players should read nothing in it except the handouts.

## How This Book Works

Purewater is built as a living world. Every Non-Player Character and faction wants something, and what they want moves forward on a clock whether or not the characters are watching. The Events chapters describe what happens if nobody interferes, day by day and watch by watch, and what changes when somebody does.

- **Overview** sets out the scenario in one page: who is involved, the key points of the week, and the places it visits.
- **Background and Introduction** gives the Games Master everything that is true before play starts, and how the four characters come together.
- **Purewater** is the city, district by district, with a map and key for each.
- **The Column**, **The City Closes**, **The Archery**, **The Sand and the Theft** and **The Last Day** are the events of the week, in order.
- **Conclusion** covers the three ways the week can end, and what follows.
- **Running the Living World** explains the agendas and clocks, and how to keep them on paper.
- **Non-Player Character Statistics** holds a full stat block for everybody in the book.
- **The Characters** and **Handouts** hold the four pregenerated characters and the material meant for players.

## The Clock

The week runs from three days before the Tourney to its last day. Each day has four watches: dawn, day, dusk and night. Scene cards give the latest point at which an event happens, written as the day and the watch: *d-3/night* is the night three days before the Tourney, and *d0/day* is the first day of the Tourney itself.

| Day | What Happens |
|---|---|
| d-3 | The Baron's column enters the city |
| d-2 | The city closes |
| d-1 | The presentation of the entrants |
| d0 | The Tourney, first day: the Archery |
| d1 | The Tourney, second day: the Single Combat |
| d2 | The Tourney, third day: the Run, then the closing |
