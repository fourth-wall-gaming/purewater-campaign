# Purewater

Purewater is built on islands across the Mouth, where the Lake Lady's water drains into the sea. There is no ground worth the name and there are no horses: everything and everybody moves by boat, over a bridge or up a water-stair. Every local can handle a boat, and no outsider can crew one. A character who needs to cross water hires a boatman, finds a ferry or swims, and the Games Master should make the choice matter.

The city map below is canon. Each district sheet that follows enlarges a part of it, with a numbered key. Crosses mark places, discs mark taverns and amenities, and squares mark posts of the city watch. Nothing secret is lettered on any district sheet, so they can be shown to the players.

<!-- map: purewater-map.png -->

**Getting about.** Three facts shape every journey. No bridge crosses the Grand Canal: the North Quarter and the Merchant's Quarter meet only by the Palazzo Ferry and the Guild Ferry. No bridge reaches the Lullwater or Temple Isle: the first is reached by boat from Shilling Stairs, at a shilling a crossing, and the second only by the temple's own ferries. And the Triumph Bridge from Caravan Square is the only way into the city by land.

**The Dragon Knights in the streets.** Until somebody changes it, the occupation is the weather of every outdoor scene. Knights walk in threes and fours, take the middle of a bridge and let the city go round them, and pay for nothing. Use them as a presence, not an encounter: they are mostly not fighting and always making the city smaller.

## The Pearl

The pleasure island, off the North Quarter, strung with lamps along Pearl Quay. Its houses are the Sylph's Embrace, the grand Siren's Call at the foot of the Lantern Bridge, Marisette's Mother of Pearl in the middle of the Quay, the Lily Garden on the western lobe, and the poorer Promise of Heaven, where Randall was raised, by the Old Span. Marisette rules every house on it, and one rule older than any of them holds: a night is for sale, a person is not.

The Pearl is where the scenario opens. Santo buys Emmeralda's evening at the Sylph's Embrace on the first night, comes back for her by boat on the second day if the working took, and loses his temper on the Quay street that evening. After that, every door on the island is shut to the Dragon Knights.

## The Lullwater

The thieves' quarter, a separate island to the north-east reached by no bridge: the narrowest canals in the city and tenements leaning over them until they almost touch. One straight canal, the Gutter, runs down its spine. The Still, a round pool of dead water, sits at its heart with the Moist Oyster tavern on its edge. Boats from High Isle put in at Shilling Stairs. Crowbill sits on the roof over the cookshop ovens beside the Gutter, the Dredgers keep their hall on the south shore, and Sinnit and Nus both live here.

Conall is cornered here by Sinnit on his first evening. If the locket is stolen, the Baron hunts the thief into the Lullwater at dawn on the last day, the quarter fights back, and he burns it.

## The Shoals

The docks and fish-markets at the city's south-east corner, facing the open sea: the Fishmarket, the Fishmarket Stair (the busiest water-stair in the city), net-lofts and ice-houses, and the smugglers' jetties at the tip of the south-western arm. The Low Canals, four thousand people on plank walkways and rope bridges, run out along that arm. The Dredgers are strongest here.

If Marda Blackwater's grievance is not answered, it is from the head of the Fishmarket Stair that she stops the water-stairs on the eve of the Tourney.

## The Lists and Temple Isle

The walled Tournament Ground at the east end of the North Quarter: a six-sided list of raked sand ringed by timber stands for four thousand people, the Governor's box in the western stand, and behind it the small stone room where the champion is presented. The Long Butts, where the Archery is shot, run along the north side. The water-gate in the east wall is where the Run goes out.

Temple Isle lies just north across a narrow water, unbridged, a single walled precinct around the Sacred Confluence. Its water-steps, where the Lady's blessing and the Washing are held, face the stands in plain sight.

The presentation, the Archery, the Single Combat and the closing all happen here.

<!-- map: districts/tournament -->

## Caravan Square

The city's only land approach, on the western shore: a walled waterfront of the beast yard, the wagon park and warehouses, ending at the Gate of Triumph and the Triumph Bridge to the customs house. The Ferry Stage serves everything that goes by water. The Baron's column comes through here on the first day, and three Knights hold the middle of the bridge.

<!-- map: districts/caravan -->

## The Forges and the Docks

The Forges are High Isle's industrial island, all foundries, boatyards and smoke, with the Forge Tower over the south shore. Below the tower, at a gated private pier, lies the DragonBarge, steaming where its hull meets the water. Orsk & Daughter, the goldsmith who cut the mark on Santo's knife, keeps a shop a few streets west of the Foundries. The Seaward Quarter's docks run along the south shore, with the Harbour Master's Tower at the tip of the longest mole.

<!-- map: districts/forges -->

## The North Quarter

The long northern island facing the Lake. Its western half is houses, rich toward the Pearl; its eastern half is the official quarter of the Governor's Palazzo, the Hall of Justice and the Barracks around Palace Square. The Processional sweeps through it from the Pearl's bridges to the Grand Canal. The Palazzo Ferry and the Guild Ferry cross the canal.

<!-- map: districts/grandcanal -->

## The Merchant's Quarter

The South Quarter, where the Triumph Bridge brings trade ashore: the walled Market Square and its three guildhalls, the counting-houses, the Watercrafters' Guild, and the palazzi of the merchant princes along the Grand Canal. Palazzo Vantt, Palazzo Pyle and Palazzo Quist belong to the three councillors who carried the standing order.

## Buildings

### The Sylph's Embrace

Constantine's house on Pearl Quay: four storeys, narrow at the street and deep to the water. Emmeralda dances in the salon on the ground floor, and her room is the big room at the back of the second floor, with the armoire in the corner and the rug in the middle. The back stair runs from the attic, where Magda and Gardwen lodge, past Emmeralda's floor to the private water-stair, where a skiff is kept. It is the quiet way through the whole house, and the way Santo leaves.

<!-- map: buildings/sylphs-embrace -->

### The Mother of Pearl

Marisette's house and the seat of her authority: the oldest house on the Quay, with a plain water-stair, no sign, and a doorman who already knows who you are. Marisette receives in a small audience room on the first floor, where she sits and everybody else stands. The cellar holds a lock-up with a ring in the wall, and an old bricked arch with a heavy door that leads down into the catacombs.

<!-- map: buildings/mother-of-pearl -->

### The Moist Oyster

A rough Lullwater tavern of four buildings round a yard on the edge of the Still. The storyteller Thorne holds court by the hearth most evenings. The back room is built out over the water on piles, with a water-door onto a plank landing, and things go in and out of it by boat that never cross the bar.

<!-- map: buildings/moist-oyster -->

### The Lists

The Tournament Ground in plan: the sand, the stands, the Governor's box and the presentation room behind it, the competitors' enclosure, the Long Butts and the water-gate.

### Temple Isle

The temple precinct in plan: the temple at the north end, the cloisters, the ring of shrines around the Sacred Confluence, and the water-steps facing the Lists.

## The DragonBarge

The Baron's ship: twenty people aboard, and that is the whole of it. A stranger on its deck is noticed inside a minute. The Baron's quarters and his working cabin are aft; Santo has the good cabins forward; the champion and his keeper, Orrin Sculle, live below the waterline aft, by a lamp that is never put out. Six of the Dragon Circle stand watch-and-watch. Provisioning comes out daily and is taken at the forward gangway by the cook.

Getting aboard is an invitation problem more than a stealth problem: a tailor, a petitioner or a woman brought out for an evening comes aboard escorted and never goes aft. A night entry comes from the water, under the pier or over the stern. Gardwen goes aboard on the first night of the Tourney, and Nus and Randall on the second.

<!-- map: encounters/dragonbarge-map.png -->

## The Vantt Estate

Councillor Alderic Vantt's country house and water-meadow, two miles east of the walls by the Causeway road, where the Dragon Knights are camped: twenty-five men, their horses, the pavilions and the tourney gear. Tat Atarer runs the camp. It is a rich man's farm with tents on it, not a fortress: no walls, four gates, a river frontage, local labour coming and going all day, and bored men who talk. It is a far softer target than the barge. There is no sheet for it; describe it from this paragraph.

## Beneath the City: the Catacombs

Old burial galleries and older water-pools under the Hill quarter and beyond: dry, cold, chalk-white, with three centuries of the respectable dead stacked in them. The houses use them because nobody official goes down there and sound does not carry up. To be taken to the catacombs is to be in serious trouble. These sheets are for the Games Master only, and nothing on them is known to the city above.

## The Realm of Thornmere

Purewater is one city of a larger kingdom. King Ostmar rules from Caldreth, far away and content. The Baron's border estate, where Thuban's bones lie, is four days' hard riding from the city.
