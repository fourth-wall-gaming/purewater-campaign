# Purewater

Purewater is built on islands in the mouth of the lake, where the Lake Lady's water meets the sea. There is no ground worth the name and there are no horses: everything and everybody moves by boat, over bridges, or along the water-stairs. Outsiders cannot crew a boat here, and every local can.

The city map below is canon. Each district sheet that follows enlarges a part of it, with a numbered key. Crosses mark places, discs mark taverns and amenities, and squares mark posts of the city watch.
