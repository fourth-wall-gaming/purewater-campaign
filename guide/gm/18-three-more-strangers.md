# Three More Strangers

The four strangers are not the only people in Purewater this week with a story of their own. These three each carry one, and it runs whether or not the characters are near it. Each has a long thread, the thing they want over the whole campaign, and adventure-sized steps under it. Each step asks one question and ends when the question is answered. None of the three is a guide or a rescuer. They are people the characters can meet, hire, help, cross or be crossed by.

When a character's adventure runs into one of these threads, whatever the stranger has already done is settled history, and whatever they are doing now is live, with its own dice. Do not invent something they did earlier to make a scene convenient.

## Halla

Halla is a dwarf who owes everything to Crowbill and wants to owe nothing to anyone. He has entered the Single Combat to fight his way out of that debt, and he is good enough to reach the final. There he meets the Baron's champion, and he loses, unless somebody at the table changes the week.

**His steps:** first the Tourney, and the final on the sand. After the Tourney comes *The Drowned Trade*: people are being taken out of the low water, and Halla goes looking for them on his own account.

**Where he meets the Baron's people:**

- Tide Lord Aurelie Stane, one of the Society's Four, who is selling the taken;
- Severin Kade, and the relic of Aun he works with;
- Lady Marr's ember mark on the receipts;
- and Crowbill, who holds his debt, and who wants Stane dead once he learns what she has been doing.

## Hrill

Hrill is a traveller who cannot walk past a wrong, and he finds that this one goes all the way up to the Crown. He is only passing through Purewater, but he keeps standing between the Dragon Knights and the people they push. Each time he does, he ends up further on the wrong side of the Knights and of the law that has been told to leave them alone.

**His steps:** scrapes with the Knights in Purewater, each worse than the last, with no fixed end. Then the road north, to the elven teaching house at Wind Stair, and after that to the sworn companies Caldus Vane is raising in the provinces.

**Where he meets the Baron's people:**

- Caldus Vane and the oath companies;
- Sir Aldren Vaust and Serevan Ashleaf at Wind Stair;
- Sergeant Lysa Bell at Ashwick, who is on the road to Emberhold in the first act of the Bones;
- and Captain Ward.

## Nimras

Nimras is a mage for hire who is learning which clients cost more than they pay. His business is already running. He does lawful work for a fee, for Hester, for merchants and for a growing list of the nobility on the Grand Canal. **The characters can hire him.** He will do what they pay for, and he will ask what they want it for.

**His steps:** jobs for whoever pays, each a small adventure of its own. Then *The Midnight Exchange*, where somebody is buying what no lawful mage should sell.

**Where he meets the Baron's people:**

- Severin Rusk's binding venture, which buys elven grave goods and the elven dead as anchors;
- Talarin Reedwake, who is trying to stop it;
- and the binding buyers of the Exchange.

## Running Them

Their operations run on the world's clock, like everybody else's. Halla investigates the drownings himself, Hrill is on the road where Vane is organising, and Nimras takes whatever work comes through his door. The dice decide how they fare. A character may arrive in the middle of any of these threads and find the stranger already winning, already beaten or already gone.
