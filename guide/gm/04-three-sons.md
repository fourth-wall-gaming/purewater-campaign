# Three Sons

Baron Hanzo di Teufel has three sons, and he knows about one of them.

## Santo

The one he knows about is his heir. **Santo di Teufel** is nineteen, dressed beautifully and always slightly too warm for it, with rings on his fingers and a good sword he cannot really use. He is loud, generous and enormous fun for about an hour. He spends money on people to watch their faces. He has never had to wait for anything, and so he experiences an ordinary refusal the way other people experience being robbed.

His father is the only person alive who can make him feel small, and does it without raising his voice. Everything Santo does too loudly, he does at that distance. The Baron has nothing but contempt for a son who is not enough, and Santo knows it. He has taught himself a little demonology by tracing his father's work out of a locked case: he can open a channel to the spirits and he can see them, and that is the whole of it. He cannot seat a binding. He has no idea his father is planning anything bigger than a tournament.

This week he wants one thing, Emmeralda, and he has one stolen scroll to get her with. When it goes wrong, Santo does not run. He takes hold of whoever is nearest, because that has always worked for him, and nobody has ever made it stop working.

## The Twins

The two the Baron does not know about were born in Purewater a little over twenty years ago, to a young elven mage he seduced and broke long before his open descent. She fled to the city to have them and died there when they were small. Nobody claimed them as a pair.

**Randall** was raised on the Pearl by the women of the Promise of Heaven, who are still, collectively, his family. He grew up a thief of the practical sort: locks, pockets, other people's routes and a very good read of a room. He knows every roof, ledge and water-stair on that island, and he has never in his life been more than four minutes from somebody who would hide him. He does not discuss his mother. People have been telling him all his life that he has somebody's face, and he has never once asked whose.

**Conall** went to Ashwick, to a militiaman's family, and was pulled out of a ditch at nineteen by an elf knight of the Swords of the Lake, who taught him to read and then taught him a good deal more. An old Master Assayer of the King's Assay recognised what he was as a boy, healed him, trained him and handed him on to her. Now he carries the Assay's warrant, its silver thumb-ring and a steel punch with his own mark on it, and he investigates things other people would rather call bad luck.

**They have never met.** They are identical, and they both wear the di Teufel face, which is Santo's face. In a city where nobody knows the heir by sight, that turns into three men with one face the moment somebody needs to find one of them.

## Who Knows

**Temerach Nebulo** knows. She has known whose son Conall is since before she recruited him, and she brought him to Purewater on purpose. She has not told him, because the truth has been more useful to her kept than told. Before this week is out, that changes.

**The Baron does not know**, and he must not find out lightly. A living bastard of his blood is two things at once: a tool, and a claim on his name. He wants the tool, and he wants the claim extinguished, in that order. The moment anybody in his service sees two of that face together, or is *sold* the fact, he begins to hunt them. Quietly at first, with a name and a purse. Then Blau. Then the Knights. He will not announce a bastard to the city; he will take one off a street at night. And once he knows, he never stops.

**This is the most dangerous thing the characters can set off**, and it is set off by being seen. That makes every public scene the twins share a real decision. A guard who sees Randall in the wrong place sees Santo slipping his leash, and thinks nothing more of it. A guard who sees Randall and Conall side by side sees the end of the Baron's line, and runs to tell somebody. In the natural course of the week, the person who sells the secret is a canal-rat with a grudge called Sinnit, and he sells it on the eve of the Tourney unless somebody has made peace with him first.

**If Santo dies**, everything above happens at once. With the heir gone, the twins are the only di Teufel blood walking, and that is a dagger at the Baron's name.
