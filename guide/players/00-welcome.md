# Welcome to Purewater

You are about to spend a week in a city built on water, three days before its holiest festival, in a season when everybody has agreed not to notice something.

This is the Players' Guide to *And Then the Dragons Came*, a scenario for *Classic Fantasy Imperative*. Everything in it is safe to read. It tells you what anybody in Purewater would know: what the city looks like, how you get about it, who its gods and powers are, what the Tourney is, and what kind of week this has been so far. It introduces the four characters you can play, with their sheets. It explains how to build your own character instead, and, if you have never played *Mythras*, how the game works. It does not tell you what is really going on. Finding that out is the game.

## What Kind of Game This Is

In Purewater everybody wants something this week, and the city keeps moving whether you are watching or not. If you spend an evening drinking in the wrong quarter, things still happen in the right one, and you hear about them in the morning. You cannot be everywhere, so where you go, whom you trust and what you say out loud all matter.

You will spend more of this week talking than fighting. You will meet people who could help you, if you can work out what they want, and powerful people who cannot be touched, unless you can find somebody who is allowed to touch them. Expect to talk your way into rooms, read a city's mood, follow money, and sometimes climb up the side of something in the dark. When there is a fight, *Mythras* fights are fast, frightening and decided in a few blows, so choose them well.

The people of Purewater remember what you do, and so do the dice. Your character has **Passions**, the things they love, hate and fear, and the week will test them. The best moments of the game come from those tests.

## Before You Begin

Read *The City on the Water* and *This Week* before the first session, or have your Games Master tell you the short version, which takes five minutes; stop them when you start asking questions. Then read all four characters in *Choosing Your Character* before you pick. If you are new to *Mythras*, read *Playing Mythras* last. It is short, and the Games Master will teach the rest at the table.

Then stop reading. The rest of this story is yours to find.
