# Purewater — what else is happening

A city where every event is about the plot is not a city, it is a corridor with
scenery. These are for the spaces between nodes: crossing town, waiting for
dawn, walking somewhere to ask somebody something.

**How to use them.** Roll **1d20** when the party moves between districts, or
once per watch if they are lingering. On **1–8, nothing happens** — most walks
across a city are just a walk, and rolling them is how the ones that *do* happen
land. Nothing here is a hook unless the players make it one.

**The rule that keeps them honest:** an encounter must not know the plot. None of
these people have heard of the Baron's champion. Their problems are their own and
they are, to them, the most important thing in Purewater today.
