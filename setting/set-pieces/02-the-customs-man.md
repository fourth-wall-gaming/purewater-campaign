# The customs man at the water-gate
*Magda's run · CH.3 · the water-gate, d-3/dawn · Act I*

**What happened.** Magda took two people and a wolf through the boom without
paying, purely on presence — no bribe, no story, just refusing to be a problem
he wanted. And while losing, he let something slip: they were **the third lot
through that day carrying something they would not show him.**

**Why it worked.** The model gate scene. It is small, it is over a coin, it is
entirely public, and it costs nothing if you lose — and it carries a fact that
nothing else in the act was going to deliver. A functionary who is being
overborne complains, and complaints leak.

**The dice.** Willpower 43/63 — no skill, no charm, no lie. Just standing there.

**Carrying it over.** Fully portable, and the better use of it is as the template
rather than the scene: **any transit scene the party must pass through should be
carrying one fact they cannot get elsewhere.** That is the difference between a
gate and shoe leather.
