# Incognito fails by two
*Conall's run · CH.3 · a ledge at dawn, then the catacombs · Act II*

**What happened.** Hesper took Conall off a ledge at dawn on a witnessed
identification. Conall's attempt to pass as nobody **failed by two points**, in
plain view of a sorcerer — which meant that from that moment **every true thing
about him looked like evidence.** A King's warrant. A capital accent. A twin's
face. All of it now reading as a cover story.

He rode the compulsion rather than spend a Luck Point on it, lost, and was
hooded, boated, and put in a chair opposite Randall.

**Why it worked.** The margin is what makes it hurt — two points — and the
consequence is not damage, it is that *the truth stops being usable as a
defence*. That is a far better punishment than a wound.

**The dice.** Incognito 75/73, failed by two. Willpower 69/74 against Arcane
Casting 84/100, riding the compulsion by choice rather than spending Luck.

**Carrying it over.** Very portable. Whenever a disguise or a cover fails
narrowly, **do not simply reveal them — make their honesty look like a lie.**
And note the player's choice to take the hit rather than spend the resource;
that is a PC finding out what kind of person he is, and it should be honoured.
