# Santo sees his own face and is delighted
*Gardwen's run · CH.10 · the DragonBarge, d0/dusk · Act III*

**What happened.** Randall sold a delivery aboard the Baron's ship — a forged
Asmodeus knife, wrapped Quarter-fashion — and the cook took it and sent for the
young master.

**Santo came out and saw his own face standing on his father's deck.**

And he was *delighted*. Because a second face is not a threat to a man like
Santo. It is an alibi.

> *"How much to be me for an afternoon?"*

**Why it worked.** The reversal. Everybody at the table had spent two sessions
treating Randall's face as a liability and a danger, and the villain's reaction
turned it into an **offer**. It also characterises Santo completely in one line:
he is not frightened, he is not suspicious, he is shopping.

**The dice.** Deceit 25/113 at Easy to sell the delivery — a walkover, because
the disguise problem had already been solved by preparation rather than by
rolling.

**Carrying it over.** Extremely portable and worth building toward. **When the
PCs bring the villain a problem, have him see an opportunity instead.** Any
scenario with a double should reach this scene. Note that it also hands the
party a genuine, tempting, terrible option.
