# The room that had almost nothing in it
*Conall's run · CH.4 · the Sylph's Embrace · Act II*

**What happened.** The murder room yielded **nothing**, because Santo had taken
every instrument out with him — no knife, no cloth, nothing of his at all.

One thing survived, in the joint between two floorboards: a scorched, spent twist
of **oiled silk**, with three or four long **dark red hairs** still caught in the
fold, bound with fine copper wire.

**Why it worked.** An empty crime scene is more frightening than a full one,
because it means somebody competent has been there — and then the one object that
did survive is small, specific, physical, and raises a question nobody had asked
yet. Whose hair, and why is it wound with wire?

**The dice.** Perception 61/73. A single success, and the entire yield of a scene
that everyone expected to be the big one.

**Carrying it over.** Extremely portable, and a good correction to the instinct
to reward a search roll with a full explanation. Let a searched room give up
**one** object. Make it a thing rather than a fact. Let the players carry it
around for two days not knowing what it is.
