# It is not a demon, it is a dead man
*Magda's run · CH.6 and CH.9 · Act II*

**What happened.** Hesper, who had spent an afternoon with the thing in
Emmeralda, named what was aboard the ship: not a demon but a **shade** — a bound
dead man in a living host, **maintained by the daily bleeding**. Ellick Sarn,
who sold himself for warmth, filled it in: the binding aboard is *furnished*, it
**fights**, and the morning cut is what keeps it tired.

Then Thorne found him on the old muster rolls. **Thuban Eta** — drowned by his
own company for refusing to fire a valley, held in the world by the betrayal
itself, and nobody ever came back for him.

**Why it worked.** Three separate people each holding one piece, none of whom
knew what the others had. And the reveal reframes the whole campaign: the thing
in the boy is not the enemy. It is another prisoner, and it has been waiting a
thousand years for somebody to say the words over its bones.

**The dice.** None that mattered. This is the payoff of legwork already done.

**Carrying it over.** Essential and portable. The specific route — Hesper, Sarn,
Thorne and the muster rolls — is one of several; what must survive is that the
party assembles it from three sources and that the last piece is *why he stayed*.
The morning cut keeping it tired is a gift to any rescue plan and should be kept.
