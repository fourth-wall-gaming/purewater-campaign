# Refused by one point
*Gardwen's run · CH.4 · the Sylph's Embrace, overnight · Act I*

**What happened.** Santo offered to buy Emmeralda's contract and Constantine
refused him — **by one point.** She nearly wavered.

Then he tried the binding from the stolen scroll and **both rolls failed**:
nothing seated, the scroll burnt out, and he finished it with the knife.

**Why it worked.** The whole tragedy runs on two narrow numbers. Constantine is
not incorruptible, she is a businesswoman who came within a point of taking the
money — which makes her refusal a *decision* rather than a character trait, and
makes her guilt afterwards real.

And Santo's failure is the engine of everything: a competent villain binds a
demon and walks away. An incompetent one leaves a corpse, a burnt scroll and a
knife, and a father who now has to cover for him.

**The dice.** Influence 49/62 against Willpower 50/62 — one point. Then Arcane
Casting 49/37 at Hard, against her Willpower 74/58. Both failed.

**Carrying it over.** Keep the margins and say them out loud to the player later.
"She nearly took it" is worth more than "she refused." And **Santo must fail his
working** — the campaign does not exist if he succeeds cleanly.
