# The knights who were not from the column
*Gardwen's run · CH.2 · the gate arch, d-3/dawn · Act I*

**What happened.** Gardwen held her face walking past dragon-scale — which for
her is the hardest thing in the act — and then noticed something while she was
doing it.

The two Knights at the arch **were not from the column.** They were dry. No
road-mud. Harness settled the way harness sits after a week of daily wear rather
than after four hundred miles. And one of them was eating from a stall he
clearly had strong opinions about.

They live here. They have been here a while. This is not a visit.

**Why it worked.** The dread arrives through kit and breakfast. A garrison that
knows which stall does the good pies is far more frightening than an army,
because it means occupation rather than passage.

**The dice.** Willpower 18/81 over a Hatred of 88/70 just to walk past without
doing something. Perception 67/79 for the rest. Note the order: the hard roll was
emotional, and the observation was the reward for making it.

**Carrying it over.** Fully portable, and one of the best cheap tools in the
game. **Let PCs read strangers off their equipment and their lunch.** Dry harness
and a favourite pie stall establish an occupation in two sentences.
