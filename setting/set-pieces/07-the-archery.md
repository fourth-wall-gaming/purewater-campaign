# The archery, and the prize that went astray
*Magda's run · CH.12 · the long butts, d2 · Act III*

**What happened.** Temerach won the Archery on **a critical on the moving-boat
pass** — the hardest shot in the competition, taken in front of the whole city.

And then the machinery moved: the chamberlain **Vosk** displaced Nerissa from
presenting the prize, so the warning Nerissa was carrying never reached the
person it was meant for. The box arrived on time. The message did not.

**Why it worked.** The tournament is a *procedure*, and procedures can be
interfered with by people who are not in the fight at all. Nobody threatened
anybody. A chamberlain changed a running order, and an entire thread failed.

**The dice.** The critical is what makes her win undeniable — she does not
squeak it, she takes it in front of four thousand people who came to watch
somebody else. Nerissa's roll to get the warning through was 94/80: a failure,
and the reason is a piece of protocol.

**Carrying it over.** Both halves. The critical moving-boat pass is the image.
Vosk and the running order is the better mechanism — **the Baron's counters
should mostly look like administration**, because that is what his protection
actually is.
