# The Lady's Washing
*Gardwen's run · CH.9 · the temple steps, d0/dawn · Act III*

**What happened.** Gardwen went down in the line as **cloth-bearer** — the
position in the rite that nobody has looked at in nine hundred years — and held
spirit sight the whole way through.

Ten ordinary men came down. The eleventh boat came last, rowed by six.

Forty seconds, hands near her brother, inside a rope line no armed man may cross,
and his father eleven feet away.

**Why it worked.** The best infiltration in the campaign and it is done **by
invitation, in daylight, in front of everyone.** The disguise is a job so menial
that it is invisible. And the time limit is not arbitrary — it is the length of a
ritual, so the pressure is liturgical rather than a countdown.

Also: what she learns is worth the risk. The Lady's water **dims the working**,
and full immersion would put it out. The anchor is a **chain at Hanzo's breast**,
and while it is on him the water is worthless.

**The dice.** Piety 13/87 to hold spirit sight through the rite. Magda's Endurance
6/52 at Hard — a critical — is what got the boat back on the flood in time for
her to make the count at all.

**Carrying it over.** Highly portable and one of the best set pieces available.
**The sacred ground is a jurisdiction where armed men have no standing**, and a
rite is a place where the PC can stand next to the untouchable thing legitimately.
Keep the cloth-bearer. Keep forty seconds.
