# What the mud kept
*Conall's run · CH.5 · Lost Isle, the reed landing · Act III*

**What happened.** A girl was missing. The ground told the story better than any
witness could have:

- A girl's prints, **standing eleven feet up the landing** — not walking.
  Standing. Waiting.
- A big **barefoot** man coming out of the reeds.
- The two of them walking down to the water **side by side**, with no drag-mark
  anywhere.
- A keel-drag pointing south-west.

She went willingly, with somebody she was expecting.

**Why it worked.** Nothing is stated and everything is legible. The horror is
carried entirely by the absence of a struggle, and the player assembles it
themselves from four physical facts.

**The dice.** Perception 45/73 and Survival 17/50 — two modest successes in a
skill nobody thinks of as an investigation skill.

**Carrying it over.** Portable to any outdoor scene, and the rule worth keeping
is the one about **the absence**: the missing drag-mark does more work than any
of the prints. Tell them what is *not* there.
