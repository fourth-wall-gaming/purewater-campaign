# Ask about the woman, not the ship
*Gardwen's run · CH.6 · Corbie's · Act II*

**What happened.** Gardwen opened by asking Mab Kellow about the ship and got
**nothing** — a flat, hard failure. She changed her approach, asked about the
missing woman instead, and got a **critical**: the Bight has been dead ground
six weeks, the water is wrong-warm from below, eels are coming up dead in the
pots, and the Order sent somebody six weeks ago who went white and promised it
would be seen to.

**Why it worked.** Same NPC, same scene, two minutes apart, and the only variable
was what the player led with. The failure was not a wall — it was information
about **how to ask**.

**The dice.** Influence 82/57, failed. Then Influence 6/57, critical.

**Carrying it over.** Very portable, and it is the argument for letting a social
failure be re-approached from a different angle in the same scene. Make the
*subject* the lever rather than the roll. A working person will talk about their
own trouble long before they will talk about yours.
