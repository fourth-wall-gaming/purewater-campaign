# The unhelming
*Gardwen's run · CH.6–7 · the presentation, Temple Isle, d-1/day · Act II*

**What happened.** The Baron's champion came forward last and took his helm off,
and it was a thin boy of seventeen with dragon-scale growing out of his throat —
and he was Gardwen's brother.

She was at the rail eleven feet away and could do nothing at all.

Nerissa, Isbet Marrow and Magda staged a loud conversation **about drainage** to
get her off the rail before she did something. It fooled the stand. It did not
fool Blau.

Afterwards Gardwen said it out loud — *"That was my brother. That was him"* — and
Nerissa stood up, the first time she had moved all scene, and did not manage her:

> *"You are not mistaken. I have had two years of people being kind to me about
> what I can feel in that water, and I will not do that to you."*

**Why it worked.** The midpoint of the campaign, and nothing happens in it. The
whole scene is a person being unable to act, in public, surrounded by people
improvising cover for her. And the allies' response is not comfort — it is
**belief**, from someone who has spent two years being disbelieved herself.

**The dice.** None for the reveal. The cover story fooled the crowd and failed
against the one man who matters, which is exactly the right split.

**Carrying it over.** The unhelming is fixed. The drainage conversation is the
part worth stealing — **allies cover for a PC badly and in public**, and the
enemy notices. And Nerissa's line only works because it costs her something to
say; keep the two years.
