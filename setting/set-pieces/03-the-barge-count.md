# The count at the DragonBarge
*Magda's run · CH.5 · High Isle seaward pier, d-2 · Act II*

**What happened.** Magda and Gardwen lay up and watched the Baron's ship for a
watch, and worked out what was aboard without going near it:

- Eight Dragon Knights. No banner. No Baron and no household — he is lodged
  ashore, round the point.
- She is fed twice a day by her own boat, **in armloads** — food for a handful,
  not for a warship.
- One shuttered port amidships, **barred from the outside.**
- Nothing is ever collected from her.

**Why it worked.** Nobody told them there was a prisoner. They deduced a prisoner
from the catering. It is the best kind of investigation scene — no roll to "find
the clue", just patient observation of ordinary logistics, and then the cold
moment when the arithmetic lands.

**The dice.** Almost none, deliberately. The scene is the reasoning.

**Carrying it over.** Completely portable and should probably be a fixture. Any
time the party can watch a place for an hour, give them four mundane facts and
let the fifth be the one that turns the stomach. Never narrate the conclusion.
