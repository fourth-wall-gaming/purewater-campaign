# Crowbill fetches you off a ledge
*Gardwen's run · CH.11 · a cookshop roof on Nettle Lane, d1/dawn · Act III*

**What happened.** Crowbill collected Gardwen off a ledge at first light — as he
has done to people in this city for thirty years — and put her on a cookshop
roof to talk.

He already knew everything about the previous night's run at the barge: the
stair, the ebb, four aboard, back on the flood. He did not ask what they found.

And asked straight out why he wanted Ila, he answered straight: because she can
put Blau at the bottom of the back stair with a cloth ready, and eleven people
cannot.

**Why it worked.** An ally who is better informed than the party and does not
perform it. He does not bargain with what he knows and he does not ask for the
one thing he would most like to hear — which is far more unsettling than a
threat, and establishes exactly how much of this city he sees.

And the answer about Ila is the model for how to handle a PC asking a hard direct
question: **give them the real reason, immediately**, and let the real reason be
worse than the one they suspected.

**The dice.** None.

**Carrying it over.** Portable. Keep the collection off a ledge at dawn as his
signature. Keep him not asking. When a player asks a straight question, answering
it straight is almost always the stronger move.
