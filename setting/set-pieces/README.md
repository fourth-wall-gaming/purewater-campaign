# Set pieces — moments from play, kept

Scenes that actually happened at this table across three runs of Purewater, and
were good enough to be worth having again.

**They are not canon.** The runs diverged hard — in one of them Santo is shot in
the head on the first night and fed to pigs; in another he is taken alive in a
bath-house; in a third he is still walking around on day three. Every entry says
which run it came from. Treat them as **stock**, not as history.

Each one is written to be re-runnable rather than re-read:

- **What happened** — the event, compressed.
- **Why it worked** — the craft note. This is the part that ports.
- **The dice** — what was actually rolled, because in most of these the dice are
  the reason the scene is memorable and not the GM.
- **Carrying it over** — what is portable, and what was a one-off.

Linked from [the-story.md](../the-story.md) at the scenes they belong to. Nothing
in here is scheduled; they are there to be reached for.
