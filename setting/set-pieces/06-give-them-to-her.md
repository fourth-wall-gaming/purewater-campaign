# Give them to her, and say somebody came back
*Magda's run · CH.11 · Lost Isle · Act V*

**What happened.** Magda's first attempt at a funeral rite for two drowned men
**failed**, badly. Ravella corrected the form: do not ask the god to *take* them.
**Give** them to her, and say that somebody came back for them.

The second litany worked.

**Why it worked.** The failure was not a wasted roll; it was the scene. A PC who
is certain she knows her own god's business is told, by somebody older, that she
has been asking wrong for years — and the correction is three words long and
changes everything about what the rite means.

**The dice.** 87/52 failed. 52/66 succeeded. The gap between them is the whole
piece of characterisation.

**Carrying it over.** Portable to any rite in the game, and it is the key that
opens Thuban: what he has been owed for a thousand years is not release. It is
**somebody coming back for him.** Say it in those words when the time comes.
