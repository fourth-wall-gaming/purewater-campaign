# The oath at the ford
*Magda's run · CH.1 · the ford, d-3/dawn · Act I*

**What happened.** Magda found the woman from her god's dream standing in the
tourney queue, got a hand into the wolf's ruff, told her the dream, and knelt in
the mud to swear herself to her. Gardwen — who had been alone for ten years —
would not take the offered staff. She hauled the old woman up out of the mud
instead, gave her name, and offered her forearm.

**Why it worked.** Two strangers bound together in the first five minutes, by a
gesture rather than by a conversation about goals. And the refusal is the whole
thing: the oath was accepted by being *declined in that form*. Somebody who has
been alone for a decade does not want a servant.

**The dice.** Survival 12/63 to get a hand into a wolf's ruff without losing it.
One roll, early, physical, and it bought a relationship.

**Carrying it over.** Entirely portable and works with any two PCs who arrive
separately. Do not script the acceptance — offer the kneel, and let the other
player decide what to do with a person on their knees in front of them.
