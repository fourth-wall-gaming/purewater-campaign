# The assault at the Mouth
*Magda's run · CH.10 · the harbour mouth, night · Act IV or V*

**What happened.** They took the DragonBarge on open water, on the ebb, a mile
out where the harbour lets go to sea, with the city's lights behind and nothing
ahead.

- **Ravella cooled forty feet of burning hull.** From the punt, in the dark — the
  white line of steam along the waterline simply *stopped*, mid-length, like a
  candle pinched out. That is what told everyone aboard that something had
  arrived.
- **Twelve of Crowbill's canal men went up the lines.**
- Magda stayed in the boat and slung: a critical **under a raised arm** dropped
  the man who was leaning out to cut the grapnels — which would have put three
  men into cold water on the ebb — and then a stone into **Blau's unarmoured gut
  from forty yards**.
- Then she went up the rope on Brawn (berserk made it one grade easier) and a
  Luck Point, and came over the rail screaming her god's fury.

**Why it worked.** Everything in it had already been established and was being
*spent*: the steam off the waterline, the ebb, Ravella's water, Crowbill's twelve,
the sling. And the funniest, best detail is the honest one — she is in a mail
hauberk with **Swim 27**, so the water is not an escape route, it is the bottom
of the channel. The danger was true rather than asserted.

**The dice.** The sling critical under the raised arm is the whole scene. Brawn
plus a Luck Point to climb a rope in mail is a player deciding to spend real
resources on getting into trouble, and should always be allowed to work.

**Carrying it over.** Very portable as a shape: a boarding action where the PC's
best weapon is at range, where going up the rope is a decision with a price, and
where an ally's magic does something huge and *non-violent* to open it. Keep the
hull cooling. Keep Swim 27.
