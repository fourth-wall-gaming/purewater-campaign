By mid-morning the whole Pearl knows Marisette's people took somebody in the
night for what was done at the Sylph's Embrace. **Sinnit makes sure it knows
who.**

He tells it on every stair and in every wine-shop between the Pearl Quay and
the Shoals: *Randall*. Randall, who was raised in the Promise of Heaven and
turned on the women who raised him. Randall, whom Hesper herself came for.
Randall, whose face was on the stair. Sinnit was in the Lullwater last night
with a man wearing that face, and *he* has a bruise to show for it.

**It does not matter that Marisette clears him.** The clearing happens in a
cellar under the Hill quarter, in front of four people. The accusation happens
in the open, forty times, in a voice that carries. Make the fact *The man who
carved Emmeralda was Randall the Pearl thief* true **in rumour** -- false, and
believed.

**What it costs Randall, on the one ground where he is rich:**

- The Promise of Heaven's door opens to him a little more slowly, and
  Asphodel's face has a question in it she is ashamed to ask.
- A fence he has used for six years is "out" when he calls.
- Somebody spits on the stair behind him.
- Ila, at the Sylph's Embrace, looks at him and runs.

None of it is permanent. All of it is this week, which is the week he needs it.

**How it ends.** Marisette can end it with one sentence on the Quay, and will
if somebody asks her to -- at a price. Emmeralda, if she is alive and herself,
ends it by being seen with him. Or Randall can go and find Sinnit, which is what
Sinnit wants.

Advance *Make Randall say it* by 1.
