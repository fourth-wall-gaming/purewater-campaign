The column is coming over the gate bridge and the crowd has been pushed back
against the parapet to let it through. Conall is in the front of that crowd,
close enough to see the Baron's face -- warm, unhurried, letting the city look
at him.

A guard at the Baron's stirrup turns his head and sees Conall, and **goes
white.** He breaks step. He says **"My lord --"** in a voice that is not
respect but fear, the fear of a servant who has just seen the heir standing in
the street where the heir is not supposed to be, and who will be the one
blamed for it.

Then the column moves and the guard has to move with it, looking back twice.

**What the guard thinks he saw:** Santo, slipped out of the household again, in
borrowed clothes, among the crowd. He does not report it, because reporting
the young master's absences to the Baron is how a guard stops being a guard.
**This does not wake *A di Teufel who is not his*.** Nobody in that column has
seen a second face; they have seen the first one in the wrong place.

**What Conall gets:** a stranger who was terrified of him and called him a lord,
with the Baron sitting three feet away. Nothing to act on, and something he
cannot stop turning over. Let him leave with it. Temerach, if he tells her,
goes very still and says nothing -- which is its own answer.

**If Conall pushes after the column,** an outrider's arm across the chest and a
polite refusal, and the guard does not look back a third time.

This is the first thread of the twins, and the reason Conall is already asking
the right question by `d-2/night`.
