**The third ending, and the one the players have to earn.** Only fires if the
party kept Nus alive through the burning *and* the locket is still out of the
Baron's hands at dusk. If Blau brought it back, *The private audience* fires
instead.

The Baron has spent the day burning the Lullwater and has not found it. At
dusk he has no leash, and for the first time in ten years nobody renewed the
seat last night with the anchor in hand.

**The party cannot command Thuban.** They are not demonologists; holding the
locket keeps it from Hanzo and does nothing else for them. They cannot break it
either -- it is a robust working, and destroying it would take a hefty Dispel
Magic that nobody here can cast. **What they can do is keep it away from him,
and that is enough.**

Because Thuban is no longer obeying anybody.

## The breakout

Somewhere on the DragonBarge, or on the competitors' ground where the Baron has
dragged his champion to keep him close, the thing in Cailan stands up. A knight
of the Sixth Wing with a thousand years of courtesy and ten years of grievance,
in a seventeen-year-old's body, with the leash off.

**He fights his way out through the Dragon Knights.** Play it as the dragon
wars come back for an evening: unarmed at first, then with a Knight's own
sword, through the deck, over the side, up the pier. He is looking for one
person -- the woman who came aboard on `d0/night` and spoke to him in her
brother's name -- and when he finds the party he falls in beside them as though
he has always been there.

**Temerach** covers the retreat from the far side of the water, with a bow,
shot after shot; she has seen what came out of that ship and she knows now what
is under the helm. Whether she meant to or not, she is leaving with them.

They get out of Purewater. It should cost them -- a boat, a route, a friend
holding a stair -- but they get out.

## And he is still not her brother

The locket only ever tied Thuban to *Hanzo*. What holds him in the world is a
box of bones in a tower on the border estate, four days' hard riding, behind
the Baron's own men. Until the bones are found and the rite is said, Thuban
stays where he is, and Gabriel stays under him.

**The last beat of the adventure is Thuban telling her where the bones are** --
with perfect thousand-year-old courtesy, in her brother's voice, because under
pressure his manners get better rather than worse -- and asking, very politely,
whether she would be so kind as to help him die.

This is the hook for the next adventure. Do not resolve it. Do not let anyone
say the words tonight. Magda's dream said *lay the dragon warrior to rest*, and
now she knows where.
