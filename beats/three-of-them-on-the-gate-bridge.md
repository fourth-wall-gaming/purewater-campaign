THE FIRST THING PUREWATER TEACHES YOU, and it costs nothing and takes four
sentences.

Three men in dragon-blazoned harness are standing in the middle of the Gate
bridge, not doing anything. Eating, mostly. The bridge is the only way in from
Caravan Square and the whole morning's traffic -- carters, a fish-cart, two
temple girls, an old man with a goat -- is going round them in single file
against the parapet, and nobody is saying anything about it.

A watchman in blue-grey is standing at the far end. He is also not doing
anything.

**Play it as scenery and let it be noticed.** Do not point at it. Do not tell
anyone what it means. The information is entirely in what the locals do with
their feet.

**If a PC intervenes** -- and somebody will -- the Knights are bored, not
spoiling for it, and they will give way to a well-dressed stranger with a
confident manner sooner than to a carter, which is its own lesson. The watchman
will not back them. Afterwards he will want to know their name, quietly, and
will not write it down where anyone can see.

**This fires the same day the column arrives.** These three are the *advance*
men. By dusk there will be twenty-five.

Advance *Hold the streets* by 1 if nobody interferes.
