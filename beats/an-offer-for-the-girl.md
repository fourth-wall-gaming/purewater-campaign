THE BEAT BEFORE THE WOUND, and if the party sees it they get the whole shape of
what follows for free.

Santo has spent one evening on the Pearl and watched Emmeralda dance. Now he is
back with a purse and a proposition: he will buy her contract, tonight, and she
will be moved to his father's barge before the Tourney.

Constantine refuses him in her own front hall. The Pearl does not sell women off
the islands to visiting nobles -- Marisette's rule, older than Constantine's
house, and the reason the Pearl still has anyone working in it. She is polite for
the first refusal and much less polite for the third.

He raises the offer twice and then says the name di Teufel slowly, as though she
has failed to hear it correctly.

**What a PC standing there gets:** a rich young man being told no in public and
not knowing how to be. Nothing criminal, nothing actionable, and a face that
several people in this city are going to see again.

**Emmeralda is in the room for part of it.** She has been bought at and around
before. Her read on him -- if anyone asks her afterwards -- is that he is not
dangerous the way the dockers are dangerous; he is dangerous the way a boy with a
lit lamp in a stable is dangerous.

Advance *Have her* by 1. He has tried the legitimate way and it is closed.
