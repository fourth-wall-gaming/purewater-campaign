At night, for the evidence, himself, because he cannot report the loss to his
father twice.

Pathetic and lethal at once. **He does not run when he panics.** He takes hold
of whoever is nearest -- a servant, a girl, a thirteen-year-old -- because it
has always worked for him and nobody has ever made it stop working.
