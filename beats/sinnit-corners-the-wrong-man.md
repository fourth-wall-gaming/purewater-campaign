**Conall's first evening in Purewater, and the first time anybody in the city
calls him by his brother's name.**

He has come over to the Lullwater by Shilling Stairs on an errand of
Temerach's -- a sealed note for the storyteller at the Moist Oyster, which she
did not explain, because she never does. He has dressed down for it, which is
the kind of thing that does not attract attention anywhere but here. Within an
hour he is lost in the narrow canals north of the Still, and within the same
hour he is cornered.

**Three of them, in a passage between two tenements with water at both ends.**
The one in front fills it: big, canal-rat, permanently aggrieved. He says the
name with enormous satisfaction.

> "Randall. *There* you are."

**Sinnit thinks he has finally caught Randall off the Pearl, alone, in his own
ground.** He does not notice the clothes, the accent, the King's Assay ring. He
sees the face he has hated for seven years. And he announces exactly what is
going to happen -- because that is the only mercy in him -- and it is not a
beating, not at first. It is a reckoning:

> "Pearl Quay. Seven years back. The purse. The bow. Say it. Say you're sorry,
> in front of my lads, and we're square."

**Conall has no idea what he is talking about.** That is the whole scene. Every
honest denial sounds like Randall being clever, which is exactly what Randall
did last time, and Sinnit gets louder with each one.

**The ways out, and all of them are fine:**

- **Fight.** Sinnit hits hard and fights dirty (Unarmed 70), and his two lads
  are worse than he is. Conall is a capable fighter; three on one in a wet
  passage is still three on one.
- **Magic.** A borrowed face, an illusion, find-magic -- whatever Conall has. It
  works, and it leaves Sinnit very frightened and very sure Randall has learned
  something new.
- **Say it.** Conall can apologise for something he never did. It does not
  count -- the wrong man said it -- but Sinnit does not know that, and he goes
  away shaken. *That* he will find out about tomorrow.
- **The Lullwater.** A door opens, an old woman in a window shouts, a punt comes
  under the passage. The quarter looks after strangers who are being picked on,
  sometimes.

**What Conall leaves with:** a thief called **Randall** who, as far as the
Lullwater is concerned, wears his face. It is the first thread of the twins on
his side, and it arrives the same day Randall is called *my lord* in Caravan
Square.

**What Sinnit leaves with:** a story -- and tomorrow, when he sees the real
Randall walking the Pearl free after the catacombs, with a different coat and no
bruises, the beginning of an understanding that is going to be worth money.
