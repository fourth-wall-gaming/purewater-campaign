**The moment Conall learns what was done at the Sylph's Embrace, he investigates
it.** It is not curiosity. Seating a spirit in an unwilling person is a hanging
matter under the Assay's charter, and he is the only warranted assayer in
Purewater. In the natural course he learns it in the catacombs, from Marisette's
questions, and walks out of that cellar and straight to the Pearl. **If he hears
sooner** -- on the night, from the street, from a note of Temerach's -- bring this
forward. Do not make him wait for a time on a page.

## The door

Constantine does not let strangers into that room. **The warrant opens it.** A
thumb-ring of assay silver, a steel punch, and a man who says *the Crown's
Assay* in a voice that expects to be obeyed. Constantine has never seen one, and
she has heard of them; she lets him in and stands in the door to watch.

## What is in the room

Whatever Santo left and nobody has taken (see *Santo's working*):

- **The chalk seat** under the rug, still live. Detect Magic finds it; Read
  Magic, or the Assay's vocabulary, reads what it was *for*: a seating, not a
  summoning. Somebody tried to put something **into** her. Whether the seat is
  spent or broken says whether it took.
- **The spent scroll**, burnt out but legible. Nine lines in an **unregistered
  hand** -- which is an offence by itself, before anybody asks whose. Conall can
  say, accurately, that whoever wrote this has never been on the Register, and
  that they are very, very good.
- **The barbed knife** behind the armoire, if the working failed: an Asmodeus
  mark on the pommel, and the Assay knows that mark.
- **The coat** over the chair, with the di Teufel crest under the collar -- if
  Marisette has not already taken it.
- **Emmeralda.** Cut open and tainted, if it failed; and if it took and she is
  still in the house, bright and wrong and asking for the window to be opened.
  Conall's find-magic sees what is sitting in her.

## What it is worth

**Establish, record, refer.** This is what the Assay is for, and Conall is good
at it. A written finding -- the seat, the scroll, the hand, the mark, struck with
his own punch -- is evidence a council cannot wave away and the basis of a
capital charge. It is the first lawful lever anybody has had on the di Teufels
all week, and it reaches a jurisdiction the standing order does not touch.

**And the price.** A finding that names a great lord, laid by a junior assayer
who cannot make it stand, is a finding he answers for himself. Temerach will
want to know what he wrote before he seals it. The doctrine says write it and
send it to Caldreth, where it arrives after everything has happened. His brother
-- he has had a brother for about four hours -- says do something now.

## The race

**Santo wants that room back.** His men come by water between the noon rest and
the evening trade (*Santo comes back for her*). In the natural course Conall
gets there first, straight from the catacombs, and **whatever he takes is safe
from Santo**. If the party dawdles, the boat arrives while they are still on the
stairs, and the scene becomes a fight in a doorway over a coat.

Whatever Conall secures here is what the party carries into the rest of the week.
