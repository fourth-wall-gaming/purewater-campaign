Randall has apologised, cancel this beat; it is the clearest thing the apology

Sinnit has worked it out, slowly, the way he works everything out: a man in the
Lullwater with Randall's face who did not know Randall's story; Randall himself
walking the Pearl free the next day; the young lord in the street on the Pearl with a
bandaged hand, shouting fire, wearing **the same face as both of them**.

That is three. A man who knows there are three of something has something to
sell.

He goes east to the Vantt camp at night, in a borrowed boat, and asks for the
albino. He is kept waiting an hour in the wet, and then Blau comes out and
listens without a word while Sinnit tells it -- badly, twice, with the grievance
in it. *A thief called Randall, off the Pearl. And another one, a gentleman, with
a ring. Both of them with his lordship's face. I thought you'd want to know. I
thought it'd be worth something.*

Blau pays him, more than he expected, and asks him one question: where the
thief sleeps.

**This wakes *A di Teufel who is not his*,** at once and permanently. Blau goes
to the barge that night. By `d0/dusk` there is *a price on the face*, and from
then on the twins cannot move openly -- in the same two days that one of them is
meant to be on the Baron's ship in the dark.

**What Sinnit wanted out of it** was Randall hurt, and he will get that. What he
did not think about is what the Baron does with sons he did not know he had.
When he finds out, a long way down the week, it is the first thing that ever
frightens him more than being laughed at.
