She has seen his face for four seconds after ten years, and she is not going to
wait for the sand to see it again.

**The ship.** Twenty aboard, a private pier on High Isle's seaward side, hull
steaming where it meets the water. A stranger on that deck is noticed inside a
minute, and since the presentation Blau has doubled the watch and stopped
changing it on the glass. Getting aboard is a whole operation: Crowbill's canal
routes, Ruhi's nose, the dark under the pier, a party making noise at the far
end of the wharf. Make it a real infiltration and let it cost something.

**What she finds below the waterline aft** is a lamp that is never put out, a
keeper asleep across a door -- and *Orrin Sculle decides*, which fires the
moment she is seen. Play it in full. Everything she learns tonight she learns
from him or from the boy:

- **What is in her brother.** A dragon-knight called Thuban Eta, a thousand
  years dead, held in a living body by a torment and a binding. Her brother is
  still in there. Orrin can prove it: Gabriel holds a cup left-handed, and the
  thing wearing him is right-handed.
- **What holds the binding.** Two anchors. The **bones** in a tower on the
  Baron's border estate, which hold Thuban in the world. And the **locket** on
  the Baron's chain, which ties Thuban to *Hanzo* -- the leash, renewed every
  night. Without the locket the Baron cannot command him and cannot move him.
- **What he is for.** If Orrin's Love wins, he tells her the thing he has
  worked out and never said: the Baron does not want a champion. He wants the
  room after the bout.
- **The ship's routines** -- the third step that complains, the watch that
  changes at the turn of the glass, the Baron's cabin aft beside the working
  cabin. *This is the map Randall and Nus will use tomorrow night.*

**If Thuban surfaces** -- and in the small hours he sometimes does -- he speaks
to her with perfect, thousand-year-old courtesy in her brother's voice. He does
not want to be there. He cannot leave while the bones hold him, and he cannot
disobey while the locket does. That is the most important conversation in the
campaign and it should be quiet.

**She cannot take him tonight.** Orrin will not help, and he is right: taking
the body frees nobody while the anchors hold. She leaves knowing exactly what
has to be taken, and from whom.

**If Emmeralda is aboard** (Santo got her out), she is forward in the good
cabins, bright and wrong, and will call out to anybody she sees.

**If she is caught,** she is not killed -- Blau wants to know who she is. A
captured Gardwen on the barge on `d0/night` is a rescue for the party and a
very bad night for her.
