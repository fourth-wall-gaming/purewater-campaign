HER SIGNATURE, and it is in the catalog so that it is played deliberately rather
than improvised into every scene.

Somebody of standing addresses the person next to her. Or says the numbers again
more slowly. Or hands her the heavy end without looking up. It is not cruelty
and it is barely even rudeness -- it is a man who has looked at eight feet of
half-orc and filed her correctly, as he sees it, in under a second.

**What is available to her:** `Hate (being taken for dumb muscle) 75`, which
augments any skill it drives by a fifth of its value -- **+15** to Commerce, to
Insight, to Intimidation, to whatever she reaches for. It is the best number on
her sheet and it only exists when somebody has just done this to her.

**The two ways it goes, and both are correct:**

- **She is right, out loud, in front of witnesses.** Prices, weights, the clause
  in his own contract, the thing he is lying about. This wins the scene and
  costs her the man permanently, because nobody forgives being corrected by
  furniture.
- **She lets it stand and uses it.** People say a great deal in front of things
  they have decided are not listening, and `Insight 70` is on all the time.

**Never play it as a joke and never play it as a wound.** It is the ordinary
weather of her life and she has forty years of practice at it. What makes it a
beat is that this week she has a reason to spend it.

**GM note.** Available, not compulsory (`TABLE.md` section 6, *a want is not a
compulsion*). Fire it when a scene offers the opening cleanly. Two or three
times a session is a character; every scene is a symptom.
