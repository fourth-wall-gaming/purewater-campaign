Fifty, distinguished, guild indigo, instruments kept beautifully, and he has
not slept properly in six weeks.

He has the whole defence prepared and wants to deliver it; a question he has not
anticipated stops him dead. What he wants is to be told that what he signed was
reasonable.

**There is no middle with him.** Vigorous defence, and then everything, in one
breath, the moment an independent reading appears. He has not destroyed the
original notes and has moved them twice.

What this buys is **a name** -- who is actually paying the Baron -- not a
conviction. The man who is paying is not in this city and does not die on the
sand.
