**Only if the locket was taken.**

The low canals below the Fishmarket: plank walkways, rope bridges, boat-houses
on stilts, four thousand people living on water and a punt to every family. The
one part of Purewater the Knights have never gone into, because horses are
useless in it and the locals know every roof.

They go in at dawn, on foot and in boats, with hooks.

**They take the bridges first.** That is the cruelty of it -- cut six rope
bridges and the district stops being a neighbourhood and becomes forty islands,
each with a few frightened people on it who cannot help each other.

They are not searching. They are making it **unbearable to keep** the thing that
was stolen. Boats stove in, houses fired, people dragged onto the planking in
front of their own families.

**Nus is found**, and hung at the Fishmarket stair where the district walks past
him, and left there. Blau arranged it and does not appear to have enjoyed it.

**The choice at the centre:** the only thing that stops this is giving the
locket back, and they have been told so plainly by somebody with no reason to
lie. Every hour they hold it has a name attached.

Play it so they can hear it from where they are. **Do not cut away.**

## Honour what the players did

**This is a consequence, not a scheduled tragedy.** If they took steps to hide
Nus, get him out of the city, or draw the hunt onto themselves, then honour it:
revise this beat onto whoever is actually exposed, or let the body at the
Fishmarket stair be somebody the party failed rather than somebody they used.
The purge still happens. Who pays for it is theirs to have changed.
