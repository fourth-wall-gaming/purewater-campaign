**THE PIVOTAL SCENE OF THE OPENING, and where the party is made.**

The old burial galleries under the Hill quarter: dry, cold, chalk-white,
three centuries of the respectable dead stacked in them. The houses use them
because nobody official goes down there and sound does not carry up.

Two men tied to chairs in the dark with the same face, who have never met.
Marisette in front of them with a lamp. Hesper behind them, where they cannot
see him.

## The interview

She questions them **together**, which is cruel and clever: each one hears the
other's answers, and each one is looking at his own face while he does it. She
wants to know where they were at the turn of the night and who the girl was to
them. She does not say what happened to the girl.

Somewhere in it somebody says the word **brother** out loud. Let the twins get
there themselves if they can. Randall arrives already carrying a guard who
called him *my lord*; Conall arrives carrying a canal-rat in the Lullwater who
called him *Randall* and wanted an apology for something he never did -- and here
is Randall.

## The witnesses

Marisette sends for the two women from the house -- or they come on their own,
because Gardwen saw the man on the stair and wants to know whom they have
taken. **Magda and Gardwen exonerate the twins on what they saw:**

- **The hand.** The man on the stair was cradling a burnt right hand. Neither
  of these two has a mark on him.
- **The coat.** He was dressed better than either -- and the coat he wore is
  still over a chair in that room, with a crest under the collar.
- **The hour.** If either twin can be placed elsewhere at the turn of the
  night, a witness who was there makes it stick.

Marisette believes a person who was there over a face, every time.

**If the witnesses do not carry it** -- bad rolls, or a PC who will not
cooperate -- other influences tip the scales, each with a price attached:
a folded note reaches Marisette by Murgeroch's hand, and whatever is in it --
Temerach does not come herself -- makes Marisette untie Conall without another
question and look at him differently afterwards; Crowbill sends word that Randall is a known man of the
Lullwater and he would like him back; or Emmeralda, if she is alive and
herself, is carried down to look at them and shakes her head. Marisette is
right in the end either way. Slow means hours in the dark first.

## What it makes

**This is the first time the four PCs are in one room.** Two women who saw it,
two men who were blamed for it, and a madame who now owes the twins an
apology. Play the moment the ropes come off; it is the party's founding image.

**Marisette's turn.** Satisfied, she looks at the face in front of her for a
long moment, and if the coat has been brought from the house she turns the
collar over and reads the crest. **She names the di Teufels.** She does not
know which di Teufel, and she knows exactly what it means that two strangers
in her city wear their face. That is the handoff from mystery to adventure.

Advance *Find who carved Emmeralda* by 2.
