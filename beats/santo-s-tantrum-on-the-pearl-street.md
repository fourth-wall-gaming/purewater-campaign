**Fires if Santo does not have her** -- the working failed, or the boat did.
If he has her safe aboard the barge he has no reason to make a scene; skip
this and let Constantine take her loss to Marisette instead.

He comes back to the Pearl street at dusk with his right hand bandaged and
nobody with him, because he has run out of people to send. He stands in front
of the Sylph's Embrace and demands her. Then he demands his coat. Then he says
**"I'll burn your house with your girls inside it,"** in front of the evening
crowd.

**And then he does something about it.** One of the cantrips his father let him
learn: a gout of fire into the doorway, the hangings, the painted shutters.
It is not big. It does not need to be. On a street of timber and lamp-oil it
is the worst thing anybody has done on the Pearl in twenty years, and people
are running with buckets before he has finished.

**Blau arrives inside a minute** -- he has been looking for the boy since noon
-- takes him by the collar and the burnt wrist, and walks him off the Pearl
without a word, through thirty witnesses who now know exactly what that face is
called. The name goes up and down the street behind them: *di Teufel. The son.*

**This is the beat that makes Santo known.** Until now he was a face; now the
face has a name, a burnt hand, and a public threat on it, and anybody who was
in the catacombs this morning can put the three together.

**Constantine** puts out her doorway, counts her girls, and goes straight to
Marisette. *The houses close to the di Teufels* follows tonight.

Advance *Clean up after the young master* by 2. Blau now has to decide how much
of this reaches the Baron.
