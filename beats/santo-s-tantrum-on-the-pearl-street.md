"I'll burn your house with your girls inside it." Said in the street,
in front of witnesses, by a nobleman who has lost his temper and his judgement.

He has been refused twice now -- once with money and once with a boat -- and this
is what he has left. The threat is about the house, but the thing he keeps coming
back to, loudly, in the open, is *the girl*, which is why this beat is worth so
much to anyone standing in the street with ears.

This is the beat that makes Santo *known* to the whole city as the kind of man who
might have done it, and it is free evidence for any PC standing there. It also
brings Blau, who ends it by physically removing the heir -- and who now has to
decide how much of this reaches the Baron.

Advance *Clean up after the young master* by 2 when this fires.
