Fifty-two, warm, well fed, genuinely good company, and hosting. Drink,
chairs, somebody's coat, your name remembered aloud. He moves a conversation the
way a man closes a door -- so smoothly you only notice you are in the corridor.

He is the channel the Baron's money runs through, and he has not said out loud,
even at home, how frightened he is.

**The tell is two self-deprecating jokes in a row.** That is the seam, and it is
the only one he has.

The Baron dines here tonight. Anyone watching the house sees who arrives and in
what order, which is worth as much as anything said inside it.
