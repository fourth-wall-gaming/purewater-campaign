The hinge of the whole adventure. In the crush at the Lady's blessing,
Nus takes the binding locket off Baron Hanzo di Teufel.

**REVISED at d0 of run 4.** The original text said "tournament registration".
The presentation of the entrants is `d-1/day` and was played there; there is no
second registration, so that hour does not exist.

**The hour is the Lady's blessing, `d1/dawn`, on the Temple Isle water-steps.**
By the Order's form every combatant and the head of their house goes down to the
Lady's water before the sand. Nerissa's people run it, in the Order's order of
precedence, on the Order's ground. The Baron cannot decline it and cannot
arrange it. Forty-odd people pressed onto a narrow wet stair for the better part
of an hour, stripped above the waist for the water, with the whole city watching
from the bridges.

That is the one hour in the week Hanzo di Teufel stands in a crowd he does not
control.

**Only fires if someone asks him to,** or if the party has left the idea lying
around where he could pick it up. Nus is bold, not omniscient -- if nobody points
him at the Baron, he robs someone safer and this beat should be revised or
cancelled.

Once the locket is out of Hanzo's hands, the daily reinforcement gets harder:
advance *Wake a Dragon King* nothing, but stall *Field the champion* -- and start
the purge clock.
