**THE HINGE OF THE WHOLE ADVENTURE.**

The champion has won. The Baron has come back to the barge with victory in his
grasp -- tomorrow at the closing he walks into the room -- and for the first
time in four generations a di Teufel goes to bed easy. He reinforces the seat
(*The nightly reinforcement*), takes the chain off, lays the locket on the
table beside his bed, and sleeps.

**Nus and Randall go aboard after the reinforcement,** in the dead hour. Nus
has wanted one big score since Crowbill named the locket on `d0/dawn`; Randall
is the only thief in the city who has been mistaken for a di Teufel and can
stand on that deck for three seconds without anybody shouting. Between them:

- **The way in** -- Gardwen's map from `d0/night`, if the party has it: the
  third step, the turn of the glass, the cabin aft. Without it, the doubled
  watch makes this very hard.
- **The cabin** -- Blau asleep across the door, which means the way in is not
  the door. The stern windows, from a boat, over steaming water.
- **The table** -- a locket on a chain with a clasp. Nus's hands. **It is not
  welded on and it is not on his neck.** That is the whole point of doing it
  tonight.

**Roll it for real.** Stealth, Sleight, the watch, Blau. If it fails, they are
seen and run with nothing, or are caught; either way **the locket stays with
the Baron and the original plan goes ahead** -- the private audience at the
closing, and Thuban into the Prince.

**If it succeeds,** they are over the side with it before anybody knows, and
the Baron wakes at first light to an empty table. *The Lullwater burns* follows
at dawn.

**Only fires if somebody points them at it** -- Crowbill on `d0/dawn` does, so
in the natural course of things it fires. If the party have deliberately
stopped Nus, revise or cancel it; that is a legitimate campaign and it leads to
the first ending.

**What the locket is and is not.** It is the leash: without it in his hand,
the Baron cannot command Thuban and cannot draw him out of the boy. It is a
robust magical object, and **destroying it takes a powerful Dispel Magic** --
the party cannot simply break it. It does not free Thuban from the world;
the bones in the border tower do that, and they are four days away.

Stall *Field the champion* at once. Advance *One big score* to full.
