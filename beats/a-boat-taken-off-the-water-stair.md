Four of them come down the water-stair and step into a working skiff that is
tied up with a fare already in it. The fare gets out. The boatman -- a Shoals man
named **Perrin Cale**, forty, who has rowed this stair for twenty years -- does
not, because it is his boat and his livelihood and he has decided, wrongly, that
he can stay with it.

They let him row. They are going to the Forge Quarter and then they are going
wherever else they feel like going, and he will be brought back when they are
finished, or he will not.

**The two before him were not brought back.** Numbers 24 and 25 in the referral
book. Nobody has found a body, which is somehow worse: the Dredgers have decided
they were rowed out through the Mouth and left, and the Knights have not troubled
to deny it.

**Onscreen** this is the easiest intervention in the campaign and the most
instructive. Four armoured men in a small boat on deep water, in a city where
every local can swim and none of them can. A party that thinks about *water*
rather than steel wins this without a fight and makes three hundred dockworkers
extremely interested in them.

**Offscreen**, Perrin Cale comes back at midnight with a broken hand, or does not
come back at all -- roll it -- and either way Marda Blackwater hears about it
before breakfast.

Advance *Hold the streets* by 1 and *Make them pay for one of ours* by 1.
