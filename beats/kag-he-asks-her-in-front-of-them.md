THE BEAT THE CHARACTER IS FOR, and it is four seconds long.

A room with Crowbill's people in it. The two who are what everybody pictures
when they picture Crowbill's. A question on the table that has money and blood
in it both.

And he turns to **her** and asks what she thinks they should do -- plainly, the
way you ask somebody whose answer you intend to use, in front of men who have
worked for him longer than she has.

**Play the room, not the compliment.** He does not praise her. He asks a
question and then waits, and the waiting is the whole thing, because nobody in
that room has ever heard him wait for her before. One of the two men makes a
sound. Somebody looks at the floor.

**Both her passions fire at once and they are pulling the same way, which is
the danger.** `Hate (being taken for dumb muscle) 75` has just been answered, in
public, by the one person whose opinion she would have taken. `Ambition (to run
these streets the way he does) 55` has just been shown the door standing open.

**There is no roll.** This is not a thing to succeed or fail at. What it does is
make the eventual collision expensive: after this, every time loyalty and
ambition go to an opposed roll, she will have this afternoon in her hand.

**GM note.** Do not repeat it. Once. If it happens twice it becomes a
relationship instead of a hinge, and the hinge is worth more.
