The inciting event of the campaign, and **the dice decide which one it is.**

He has a room on the second floor, the girl's evening, and the scroll out of
his father's case in his coat.

**What he thinks he is doing.** Making her *willing*. He has watched his father
seat a spirit in a living body and be obeyed by it for ten years; he means to put
something small and pleasant in this girl -- a lust spirit, a little hungry
thing -- so that she wants to come to the barge, and he has one use of a stolen
scroll with which to do it and no training whatsoever.

## Roll it -- twice

**First, the working.** The scroll does most of the work and Santo does the
rest badly. Roll against a poor chance -- he is untrained, frightened and
nineteen. **The scroll burns out in his hand either way**, and leaves a fresh
burn across the palm and fingers of his right hand that will not be healed by
morning. *That burn is the clue that clears the twins.*

- **It takes.** The channel opens, the seat holds, and the thing settles into
  her. She stops being afraid of him. She wants what he wants, and she is
  bright and pliant and wrong in a way anybody who knew her would see in a
  minute. **Then roll the second thing: getting her out.** Stealth down the
  back stair and through the water-door to a skiff -- easier than it should be,
  because she is helping. Success, and **he keeps her**: she is aboard the
  DragonBarge by midnight and nobody saw her go but the people in the next
  paragraph. Failure, and somebody in the house stops them on the stair; he
  lets go and runs, and the girl is left on the landing laughing at nothing --
  he will try again by boat (*Santo comes back for her*).
- **It fails.** The channel opens and the seating does not. Whatever came for
  the invitation will not go where he tells it, and he finishes the working the
  only way panic knows, with the barbed Asmodeus knife: a long ritual cut,
  opening her the length of her body. She screams. He runs, and in running
  leaves the knife behind the armoire.

## The witnesses

**Magda and Gardwen are in the house** -- this is why Magda brought her here.
Whichever way it goes, they are close enough:

- **If it failed,** they hear the screams and are first through the door.
  Gardwen's healing or anybody's First Aid saves her life; without it she dies
  before dawn. They see the man on the stair going the other way -- young,
  rich, bare-headed, **cradling his right hand.**
- **If it took,** they see a couple on the back stair in the small hours, and a
  girl who was frightened of him at dusk now hanging on his arm. Magda, who
  has seen possession before in Hel's service, knows what she is looking at.
  If they intervene, the getaway roll is opposed.

What they saw is what exonerates Randall and Conall in the catacombs at dawn:
**the burnt hand, the coat, and the hour.**

## What is in the room afterwards

He left in a panic either way and took nothing:

- the **barbed Asmodeus knife** (only if it failed), behind the armoire;
- the **spent scroll**, burnt out but legible, **in the Baron's own hand**;
- a **chalk seat** under the rug, live until somebody sweeps it;
- his **coat**, over a chair, with a Purewater tailor's chit in the pocket and
  the di Teufel crest under the collar.

Whatever is not taken tonight is still there in the morning -- and so is the man
who wants it back.

**Offscreen** (no PC in the house): Magda and Gardwen are still there as NPCs,
and it goes the same way. Either way Marisette's clock starts, and the hunt for
a man with that face begins.

Advance *Have her* by 2 if he gets her out of the house.
