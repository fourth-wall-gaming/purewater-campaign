She closes the houses. Four hundred people out of work on one woman's word,
because a boycott is the only weapon her trade has ever had and she has been
saving it twenty years.

It is a real threat for about a day and a half, and after that it is a **wasting
asset** -- which everyone involved knows, and which is what makes her dangerous
to be allied with. She will spend it once. What she spends it on is the question
the party eventually answers for her.
