A quiet boat and a sealed packet from Lady Ysolde Marr at Caldreth: word from
court about the Prince's household, the names of the men who will be waiting for
Emeric when he comes home, and a line of credit, countersigned with her ember
mark, that explains where the tournament money came from.

Intercepting this is the party's only chance to learn the shape of what the Baron
has built -- a court being made ready at Caldreth, answering to the man on the
ship -- and to understand that the room is the first step, not the last.
