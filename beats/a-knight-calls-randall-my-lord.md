The gate bridge: a stone span over the outflow, a customs house at the far
end, the lake opening green and enormous behind it.

A Dragon Knight takes Randall by the arm, calls him **my lord**, and offers a
hand down off the cart he is in the middle of robbing.

Deceit to play it, Stealth to be gone. Playing it well is worth more than the
escape -- a name, a piece of the household's business, and on a critical, where
his lordship is expected this evening. Failing it puts him in front of somebody
who has seen Santo close up.

Running clean costs nothing and teaches nothing. **That is a legitimate outcome
and should be allowed to be dull.**
