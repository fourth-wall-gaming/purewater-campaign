**The wall, in the person of a decent man.**

Seventy-one, upright, chain always worn. He does not interrupt and cannot be
interrupted. He reads the whole thing -- actually reads it, in front of you, at
his own pace, while you stand there -- and then repeats your position back to
you more accurately than you put it.

And then tells you exactly why it is not his.

The watch is under a written standing order. He is not corrupt, he is not
frightened, and he is not persuadable, because he is **right**. A baron's son
with a knife is weather, and you do not sue the weather.

If he reads it twice, he has changed his mind, and the room should know it.
