**Only if the locket was taken.** Braids into *The Lullwater burns*: this is how
Blau finds Nus.

The quarter is fighting and burning, and the whole Lullwater knows somebody
went over the Baron's stern last night and is hiding in it. **Sinnit knows
who** -- Nus has been Randall's second pair of hands for years, and Sinnit has
watched them both for seven. He knows Nus's boat, his sister's loft over Eel
Bay, the drying-shed on the Hook where he sleeps when he does not want to be
found.

## If nobody has turned him

He finds Blau in the smoke at Shilling Stairs and tells him -- because it hurts
Randall, and because he is frightened, and because he thinks being useful to
the men with fire will keep the fire off him. Blau goes straight there. **That
is how Nus dies**, and the party can learn it afterwards from anybody who saw
Sinnit at the stairs.

**This is the beat to intercept.** A party that has been watching Sinnit, or
that guesses who would sell Nus, has one chance to get between him and Blau --
or to move Nus before Blau arrives. Do that, and keep the locket out of Blau's
reach, and the road is *Thuban breaks out*.

## If Randall apologised

Then Sinnit is the one in the door of the drying-shed when Blau comes, big and
loud and announcing exactly what he is going to do -- *"Nobody does that to our
people"* -- and he does it. He will not win. He buys Nus the minutes it takes to
get into a boat, and that may be all the party needs.

Whether he lives through it is the dice.

Advance *Make Randall say it* by 1 if he sells Nus. Convert it if he stands.
