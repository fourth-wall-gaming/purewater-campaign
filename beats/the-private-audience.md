THIS IS THE POINT OF THE ENTIRE HUNDRED YEARS.

**Only fires if the Baron has the locket at dusk** -- never stolen, or brought
back by Blau out of the Lullwater. If the party still holds it, this beat is
cancelled and *Thuban breaks out* fires instead.

Prince Emeric is two-and-twenty, well-meaning, badly advised, and has already
been used once this week: three of his councillors had him sign the instruction
that silenced the temple's fouling alarm, for three unrelated reasons, none of
which was this one.

Cailan is the jar. Emeric is the destination. In that room Hanzo casts DRAW
FORTH on the boy and SEAT THE BOUND on the Prince -- both on his sheet, both
requiring an anchor, and he wears one round his neck.

## Two ways into the room

**The old rite (the locket was never stolen).** The Tourney is older than the
Governor's office, and by the old form the Single Combat champion is presented
to the Crown's representative **privately and unarmed, at the closing**, with
the doors shut -- a rite, not a favour, which is exactly why nobody guards it.
The champion won yesterday; tonight, when the Run comes back to the temple
steps and the Prince takes his place, Hanzo walks his champion through that
door. Quietly. Nobody in the city knows anything has happened.

**Summoned to answer (the Lullwater burned).** By noon the Prince can see the
smoke from the Governor's box. By mid-afternoon the reports are coming in --
a foreign lord burning a quarter of the city on the second day of a sacred
festival. Emeric does the brave, decent, stupid thing: he **summons the Baron
to answer to him, in private**, and the Baron comes, with his champion at his
side, smoke on his clothes and the locket back on his neck. The councillors
stand outside the door. It is the same room, reached the other way, and Hanzo
could not have arranged it better.

## What comes out

The Prince comes out. He is gracious, and composed, and **right-handed in a way
nobody thinks to notice.** Thuban Eta is wearing the Crown's nephew. Cailan is
carried out behind him, emptied and barely breathing.

**After the old rite:** the Baron goes home with a prince in his pocket and
begins to move on the throne. Nobody in Purewater knows. The adventure ends
with the party knowing exactly what has happened and no one in the kingdom who
would believe them.

**After the summons:** the Prince steps out onto the steps above the burning
city and **sides with the Baron** in front of everyone -- the thieves' quarter
rose, the Baron restored order. And across the arena an elf has a bow in her
hand: *The impossible shot*.

Three ways to break this, all live until dusk: stop the champion winning; keep
the Baron out of that room; or take his anchor and keep it.
