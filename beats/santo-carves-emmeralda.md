The inciting wound of the campaign, and the first real test of the
staging rule.

Santo has taken a room at Constantine's house and paid for Emmeralda's evening.
Some time before midnight he attempts the binding: a long ritual cut, the barbed
Asmodeus knife, and a demon that does not go where he tells it. The girl screams.
He runs, and in running he leaves the knife behind the armoire.

**Onscreen** -- PCs in the house hear the shrieks and can reach her while she is
still bleeding. First Aid or healing magic saves her life; without it she dies
before dawn. They may catch a glimpse of a fleeing man they will later realise
looks like someone they know. Whoever searches the room properly can find the
knife.

**Offscreen** -- it happens exactly the same way, minus rescue. Emmeralda dies
or survives on Constantine's own resources (survives, badly, if Gardwen is
anywhere in the city and can be fetched; dies if the party is far away). The
party meets the aftermath: a house in uproar, a girl marked with demon-taint,
a knife still hidden behind the armoire for whoever thinks to look.

Either way Marisette's clock starts, and the hunt for a man with that face
begins.
