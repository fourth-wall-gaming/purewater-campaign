The south-west corner of High Isle, where the landward channel and the road
from Caravan Square bring the city's trade ashore. On the master map this is
the quarter holding Market Square and the guildhalls.

Draw: the great market plaza as an open paved square with the regular rows of
stall-footprints marked out on it; the guildhalls around it as large formal
double-lined blocks with courtyards — the Merchants, the Shipwrights, the
Dyers and the Watercrafters' Guild; counting-houses and warehouses crowding
the water with loading doors opening directly onto the canal and cranes or
hoist-beams at the upper floors; the palazzi of the merchant princes as big
courtyard houses with private water-stairs.

The working detail of a trade quarter: wide barge-canals that can take a
loaded hull, stone wharves with bollards, goods-yards, and a dense grid of
narrow alleys between the warehouse blocks. Dyers' drying-racks and tenter
frames in the open yards near their hall.
