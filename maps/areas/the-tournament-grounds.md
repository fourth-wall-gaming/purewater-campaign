The ground where the campaign ends. A measured site plan, not a building plan.
The SACRED LAKE lies along the TOP (north) of the sheet; the ground runs back
from its shore to the south.

THE LISTS: a rectangle of raked sand inside a roped boundary, sand stippled,
rope dotted between posts. Tiered timber stands on THREE sides, drawn flat as
nested ranks of parallel bench lines. The Governor's box at the WESTERN end,
drawn in plan as a rectangular enclosure with its seating, hung with cloth.

THE LONG BUTTS: adjoining, two hundred paces of flat grass between the water
and the stands, the shooting line at the near end by the Governor's box, round
targets at the far end, and the distance marked along it in paces.

Behind the stands to the south, the open slope where the rest of the city
watches, as rising ground in contour hatching.

Everything is visible from everywhere -- that is the whole point of this
ground. Keep the middle clear; nothing interrupts a sightline. At the water's
edge to the NORTH, the temple steps running down into THE SHALLOWS where the
Order stands waist-deep, in plain view of the stands, with the open lake
beyond and Temple Isle small at the sheet's northern edge.

Behind the Governor's box, the small room where the winning champion is
presented alone and unarmed: one enclosed chamber, one door.

Dual scale bars, in paces and in feet.
