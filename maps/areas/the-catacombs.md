A subterranean plan of the tunnels, caverns and centuries-old water-pools
under the city, older than anything above. This is the one sheet where the
underground is drawn.

Draw: a network of vaulted passages, drawn as double wall-lines with hatched
arcs for the vaulting, running irregularly and on more than one level —
mark the level changes with stairs and break lines. Older, rougher-cut
sections where the tunnels predate the masonry. Collapsed stretches drawn as
rubble hatch and dead ends.

Standing water throughout: still pools in the low chambers, drawn with the
master map's water shading; channels running between them; and places where
the water has risen to the springing of the vault. A few large caverns with
pillars.

Stair-mouths coming down from above, drawn at the sheet's edges as entry
symbols without naming what they come down from. Smugglers' routes implied by
the mooring-rings and landing ledges cut beside the navigable channels.

Keep the sheet unlabelled apart from its title. No place names, no surface
references, no indication of which buildings stand above. It is a plan of a
dark place and should give nothing away.
