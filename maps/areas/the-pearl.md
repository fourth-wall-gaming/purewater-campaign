The brightest of the Northern Isles: a small island north of High Isle given
over to pleasure and the high end of the city's nightlife. The campaign opens
here.

Draw the island whole at close range. Its houses string along the waterfront
PEARL QUAY, a single curving stone quay with landing steps every few doors and
gondolas moored along it. The named houses face the water as large double-lined
buildings, each with its own water-stair, courtyard or garden court behind, and
a lantern-bracket at the door drawn as a small plan symbol: the Sylph's
Embrace, the grander Siren's Call, the Mother of Pearl, the Lily Garden, the
Promise of Heaven.

Mark the Mother of Pearl as the oldest house on the Quay: a plain water-stair,
no lamps strung out over the water, and no sign — draw it without the lantern
symbols the others carry.

Behind the quay, a second rank of smaller buildings — kitchens, laundries,
lodgings for the staff — and the narrow service lanes between them. The back of
the island quieter, with gardens and a few private moorings on the far shore.
