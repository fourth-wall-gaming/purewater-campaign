The brightest of the Northern Isles, given over to pleasure and the high end
of the city's nightlife. The campaign opens here.

PEARL QUAY curves along the island's southern water: a stone quay with landing
steps every few doors, mooring posts, and gondolas drawn as flat ovals at
them. Lamps strung between the houses as fine catenary lines.

The named houses front the water, each DISTINCT:

- The Sylph's Embrace -- a deep, compact, thick-walled house with a covered
  landing and a small back court.
- The Siren's Call -- the grandest frontage, sited at a corner where a lesser
  channel meets the quay, so it has TWO landings on different water.
- The Mother of Pearl -- the LARGEST footprint on the Quay and the plainest:
  three ranges round a walled garden court, a plain water-stair, no lamps
  strung out over the water, and no sign. It should be unmistakably the
  biggest and most private building on the sheet.
- The Lily Garden -- the smallest house, but with the most garden: formal beds
  laid out behind it running back to the island's spine.
- The Promise of Heaven -- a long low range round an inner courtyard.

Behind the quay, a second rank of smaller buildings -- kitchens, laundries,
staff lodgings -- and narrow crooked service lanes between them. The island's
north shore quieter, with orchards, a few private moorings, and a ragged
shoreline of shallows and reeds.
