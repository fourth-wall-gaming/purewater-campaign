An elegant, high-end pleasure house on Pearl Quay, its walls thick enough to
muffle the night's business -- draw external and party walls noticeably
heavier than internal partitions.

GROUND FLOOR: the water-stair and covered landing from Pearl Quay; an entrance
hall; a receiving salon with hearth; a small office near the entrance where
the house's business is done; and a service range to the rear -- kitchen,
scullery, laundry -- opening onto a small back court with a well.

FIRST FLOOR: a central passage with six private chambers opening off it, each
with a bed, a washstand and a window to the quay or the court. One chamber
larger and better appointed than the rest. The proprietor's room at the head
of the stair. An armoire drawn against the wall of one chamber as a fixed
piece of furniture.

GARRET: where the house's own people live -- individual rooms for the
courtesans, a shared sitting room, a bathing room, and a back stair down to
the service range so they need never use the front of the house.

Three aligned plans on one sheet, every room named. Label rooms by function
only; do not mark any event or crime scene.
