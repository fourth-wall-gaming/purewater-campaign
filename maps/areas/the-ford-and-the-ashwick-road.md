The inland approach, where the north road out of Ashwick meets the water and
everyone entering Purewater from inland has to queue.

The ASHWICK ROAD running as hedgerow and cart-ruts, wide enough for two carts
and no wider -- show the verges and how little room there is to pass. Hedge
lines as the master draws field boundaries, with irregular fields behind them
and a few farmsteads set back.

At the centre, THE FORD: a pale gravel shoal crossing the channel, the water
narrowing and running shallow over it, the crossing marked by withies or posts
in the shallows, and the road running down a shelving bank into the water and
up the far side.

The queue ground on the near bank as open trampled grass with a verge wide
enough for carts to stand off the road, and a single low alehouse drawn flat
in plan.

The road runs south off the sheet toward the city under its label. Do not draw
the city as a skyline.
