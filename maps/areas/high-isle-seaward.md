The southern, seaward face of High Isle, where the island's quays front the
channel running down to the open sea. On the master map this is the quarter
labelled Docks and Wharves, with the Harbour Master's Tower at its point.

Draw: a long run of timber and stone wharves standing out into the water on
piles, with finger-jetties between them and ships moored alongside drawn as
flat plan outlines; warehouses and net-lofts packed immediately behind the
quay line with their doors to the water; capstans, bollards, and stacked
timber and barrels in the yards.

The Harbour Master's Tower as a distinct round or square tower standing clear
on the point where it can see down the channel, with its own enclosure and
landing. Seaward of the quays, the water carries the open-sea shading of the
master map rather than the stiller stipple of the inner canals.
