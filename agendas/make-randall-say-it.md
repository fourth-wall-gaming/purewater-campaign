**What he wants.** Seven years ago, on the Pearl Quay in front of the Promise of
Heaven, Randall lifted Sinnit's purse and handed it back to him with a bow,
while the house's girls laughed. Everybody who saw it has forgotten. Sinnit has
not forgotten one second of it, and he wants it **said** -- *I'm sorry, Sinnit*
-- in front of witnesses, before he can put it down.

**Why he is dangerous this week.** Not because he is clever; he is not. Because
this is the week Randall's face is worth money. Sinnit sees things on the
street, he has a grievance that wants feeding, and for the first time in his
life the things he sees are worth selling to people who can hurt Randall far
worse than he ever could.

**How it advances.** One segment per step, and every step is somewhere the party
can stop him.

| clock | Sinnit |
|---|---|
| **1** | Corners Randall in the Lullwater and gets the wrong man -- Conall (*Sinnit corners the wrong man*, `d-3/dusk`) |
| **2** | Sees the real Randall walking the Pearl free on `d-2`, unbruised, and understands there are **two** of that face |
| **3** | Tells the street Randall carved the girl (*Sinnit tells the Pearl it was Randall*, `d-2/day`) |
| **4** | **Sells the two faces to Blau** (*Sinnit sells the face*, `d-1/night`) -- which wakes *A di Teufel who is not his* |
| **5** | Sells Nus's hiding place in the burning Lullwater (*Sinnit sells Nus*, `d2/dawn`) |
| **6** | Walks the Pearl Quay as the man who outlasted Randall |

**What does not stop him.** Threats -- he announces what he is going to do before
he does it, and he does it anyway. Beating him in public -- that is a second
humiliation, and it advances the clock by 1. Money buys a day, never more.

**What stops him: the apology.** Randall, out loud, in front of people who
matter to Sinnit, meaning it or making it look like he does. It costs Randall
his pride on his own ground, in a week when he needs every bit of standing he
has, and it is the cheapest thing in the campaign. When it comes, Sinnit
stands there for a long moment, and then the agenda converts: he is a
reluctant, loud, entirely unreliable **ally**, and the next time somebody with
a dragon on his coat comes for one of the Lullwater's own, Sinnit is in the
way -- *"Nobody does that to our people."*

**This is his whole character.** A small man with one grievance, standing next
to a war, and the war is what makes him matter.
