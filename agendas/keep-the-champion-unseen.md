**What he wants.** Nobody -- not a servant, not a priest, not a
curious knight -- close enough to Cailan to notice that the champion does not
answer to his own name, or that he sometimes answers to another.

**How he pursues it.** The champion never leaves the estate. Meals go in, no
conversation comes out. Guards rotate so none of them accumulate observations.
Anyone who asks after the champion is remembered.

**What changes his mind.** He will trade secrecy for control if forced -- if the
party gets close, he would rather move Cailan somewhere worse than let the
questions continue.

**This agenda is why the city gossips.** Crowbill's people, the Pearl's
courtesans and the dockhands all know the Baron has a champion nobody has laid
eyes on. That absence is the most visible thing about him.
