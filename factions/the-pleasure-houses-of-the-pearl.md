The entertainment trade of Purewater's pleasure district is no loose collection of houses but a network under the near-absolute authority of the madame [[Marisette]], who rules the business of pleasure from [[The Mother of Pearl]]. Individual proprietors answer to her: [[Constantine]] runs [[The Sylph's Embrace]]; the half-merfolk broker [[Ravella]] runs the grander, information-soaked [[The Siren's Call]]. The lesser houses -- the Lily Garden, the Promise of Heaven (where [[Randall]] was raised) -- line the Quay below them.

**The rule that matters.** The Pearl does not sell women off the islands. A night is for sale; a person is not. It is Marisette's oldest rule, it predates Constantine's house, and it is the reason there is anyone left working on the Pearl at all. Every proprietor enforces it and no amount of money has ever moved it.

**Stated aim:** run a profitable, orderly trade. **Real aim:** to remain the only power in Purewater that answers to nobody -- not the council, not the Order, not the Baron -- by being indispensable to all of them and knowing what all of them do at night.

## Where they stand, Tourney week

Tourney week is the richest fortnight of the year and this one has gone wrong before it started. Twenty-five Dragon Knights are drinking on the Pearl at night with no watch worth the name, and the houses are absorbing it because complaining is bad for trade. Two girls have been hurt already in ways that got settled quietly with money.

**Then a di Teufel offers to buy a woman outright**, is refused in Constantine's own hall, and comes back that night with a knife. See *An offer for the girl* and *Santo carves Emmeralda*.

**When that happens** -- and it is a beat, not a certainty; the party may be standing in it -- Marisette's response is total and immediate: **all services rescinded for the entire di Teufel retinue, enforced city-wide**, and she will pay [[Crowbill]] a princely sum to guarantee it. That is a courtesan network declaring economic war on a baron in a city where his men outnumber the watch. It is also the single loudest thing that happens before the Tourney, and it puts the Pearl in the Baron's way for the rest of the campaign.

## The door

**[[Constantine]]** is the one a party meets first: proprietor of the Sylph's Embrace, shrewd, protective, and not intimidated in her own house by anybody. After the attack she is hiring protection and wants justice, and she does not much care which of those a stranger is offering.

**[[Ravella]]** is the one worth cultivating: the Siren's Call is a pleasure house and an information market in one, and she brokers both. Light innate water magic, Insight 85, and a quiet second business protecting the city's hidden waterfolk.

**[[Marisette]]** you do not meet until you are worth meeting.

## What they can do for you, and the price

| | |
|---|---|
| **What soldiers say at night** | the best intelligence on the Dragon Knights in the city -- see [[Mariarta]], who is sent up to entertain them |
| **A name, a habit, a debt** | any notable in Purewater, from Ravella, for a favour or a fee |
| **Shelter on the Pearl** | quiet lodging nobody official will search |
| **An introduction** | to merchants, councillors, and the Baron's own officers |
| **The city-wide boycott** | the network's one real weapon, and Marisette spends it once |

**The price** is never money alone. Marisette trades in obligations and keeps perfect accounts. **[[Hesper]]** -- her mage-enforcer, and secretly far more than that -- is what collects.

## Friction

Cordial with [[Crowbill]] (a paying relationship, mutual and old). Arranged terms with the [[The Order of the Lake Lady]], who disapprove and need them. Contemptuous of the [[The Harbor Masters]]' public piety. **Hostile to the di Teufels the moment Emmeralda is cut**, and until then merely wary of a baron whose son is spending freely and badly.

## Hooks

- A party lodging on the Pearl is *in the house* when the screaming starts -- the campaign's best opening.
- Constantine hires the party as protection: honest work, and it puts them exactly where Santo returns.
- [[Ila]], thirteen and devoted to Emmeralda, will not be kept out of it.
- Marisette offers to buy something the party has -- the knife, a name, silence -- and her price is a favour unspecified.
- A Knight goes too far with one of Ravella's people and the Pearl has to decide whether it settles this quietly again.

**Agendas on the clock:** *Find who carved Emmeralda* ([[Marisette]], 0/6), which is the Pearl hunting a man with a face two PCs share.
