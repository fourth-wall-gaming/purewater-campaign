Ostensibly a noble martial order, the Dragon Knights are in truth Baron Hanzo di Teufel's instrument of conquest: hardened raiders and murderers who have terrorized the borderlands for thirty years. They wear armor emblazoned with dragons and many carry fire-touched weapons. Roughly twenty to thirty strong in Purewater once the column is in -- a dozen came ahead, eight days early, to open the estate and provision the barge. Well-disciplined and thoroughly violent. The city calls them **the dragonshits**, and does so quietly.

**They are in the streets, daily, and that is the point.** They are quartered in a fortified estate east of the city, but they are not kept there. They walk the Merchant's Quarter and the Forge Quarter in threes and fours, they drink on the Pearl, they take the middle of a bridge and let the locals go round, and they eat and drink on the promise that *the Baron will settle it* -- which he does, late, and short. Boats are commandeered off the water-stairs. A dockhand who was slow got a broken jaw six days before the Tourney and has not worked since. Nobody has been killed yet.

**And the watch has been told to leave them alone.** See *[[The dragonshits in the streets]]*. That single instruction is what turns twenty-five thugs into an occupation: every Purewater citizen has now worked out, separately and correctly, that there is no one to complain to.

**Command.** The mercenary captain [[Tat Atarer]] is the leader of record -- a professional disciplinarian who fights for pay, not ideology ("the Baron pays for our swords, not our opinions"). He does not like what the standing order is doing to his company's discipline and says so, once, to the wrong person. Day-to-day operations and the Baron's dirty work fall to his albino lieutenant [[Blau]], known to the ranks as Commander Vask: burn-scarred, raspy-voiced, utterly loyal in public while secretly studying the Baron's techniques to one day break away.

**Ranks** (per the order's own conceit): Ember Recruits, Flame Soldiers, Smoke Lieutenants, Charred Captains, Ash Lords (elites who have undergone partial fire-binding), and the Dragon Circle (the Baron's inner guard, who serve aboard [[The DragonBarge]] and are not seen in the streets at all).

**Stated aim:** keep order in the Baron's territories and honor the order through tournament display. **Real aim:** use the Lake Lady's Tourney as cover to plant a foothold in Purewater, disrupt its water magic, and extend Baron Hanzo's reach. Their worst enemies are those who survived their past raids -- [[Gardwen]] foremost among them.

**How a GM should run them.** Not as an encounter table. As *weather* -- present in most street scenes, mostly not fighting, always making the city smaller. A party that never draws on them still spends the whole campaign watching people step off pavements. And every incident is a lit match near [[The Dredgers]], who work the water-stairs the Knights keep commandeering.
