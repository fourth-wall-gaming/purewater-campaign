The Movement is [[Baron Hanzo di Teufel]]'s, and nobody stands above him. Its believers want the Dragon Kings literally back, and think he is their herald; he lets them think so, and holds them in contempt. His aim is political: to depose the King and rule Thornmere as a man. The Dragon Kings were never dragons but men wearing demons (GM: see the GM guide, *What the Dragons Were*). The Baron's possession ritual, binding the ancient dragon-knight spirit [[Thuban Eta]] into a living vessel, is the instrument: a leashed knight seated in the Prince, and the court and the crown after him.

**Under him:** Lady Ysolde Marr, the Steward of the Ember House at Caldreth, makes the friends at court; [[Cassian Bree]] keeps the Ember Society in Purewater and reports to her; Caldus Vane and the oath companies are the provincial organisers and sworn men, all believers; Severin Kade works the fire, making Ash Lords quickly; Alderic Vantt is the money, in debt and frightened; Blau, Tat Atarer and the Dragon Knights are the force. Compartments hold: Vane does not know who is at the top.

How wide the movement runs is unknown -- it may have members across the realm, in other cities suffering their own corruptions. To the water-faiths and the [[The Mermaid's Court]] it is an existential threat: the return of an ancient order of fire and dominion against the older, pre-human powers of water. The Baron's victory at the Lake Lady's Tourney is meant to be the movement's first foothold in Purewater. **(GM:** see the GM-secrets lore for how the movement connects to the Baron's tower, Thuban's bones, and the wider campaign.)

**How it recruits, and the only face it has in Purewater.** Not with robes. Through
[[The Ember Society]] -- a respectable thirty-year-old antiquarian dining club it
hollowed out eight years ago, whose printed addresses contain no dragons and no
magic, only a beautifully argued melancholy about an age that was greater than
this one. Sixty subscribers; four who have ever been asked for anything; and none
of the four told why. Its secretary, [[Cassian Bree]], is the only confirmed
Movement member in the city -- a courteous clerk who keeps immaculate minutes,
decides who goes further in, and reports to Lady Marr.

**After the Tourney.** If the Baron gets the locket and the room, the Movement
becomes his court and the Ember House its seat. If he loses either, it is his
hidden network, rebuilding. Either way it outlives the week.
