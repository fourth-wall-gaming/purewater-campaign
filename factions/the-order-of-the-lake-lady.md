Both a religious cult and a civic power, the Order maintains the temples and sacred pools of Purewater (chiefly on [[Temple Isle]]) and performs the rituals that keep the increasingly polluted waters pure. Members wear blue-green robes worked with silver waves.

**Ranks:** Water Initiates, Reflectionists, Tide Wardens, Wave Speakers (those with proven water-affinity), Deep Visionaries (who commune with the Lake Lady), and The High Tide -- the ruling council of five Deep Visionaries.

**Leadership.** [[Lilura Deepcurrent]] has led the Order for three decades: elderly, piercing-eyed, rumored to carry merfolk ancestry. Beneath her, [[High Priestess Nerissa]] runs the Temple of the Lake Lady day to day -- she can sense water corruption, bless sacred waters, and speak with water spirits, but **temple law forbids her from intervening directly unless the sacred sites are threatened.** She therefore needs agents. That constraint is the engine of the party's whole relationship with the Order.

**Stated aim:** preserve the sacred waters and the Lake Lady's blessings. **Real aim:** hold their political power as the waters fail; some higher clergy fear the goddess's power is waning and quietly study the corruption to understand it.

## Where they stand, Tourney week

**The Tourney is theirs.** It is a sacred festival, older than the Governor's office, and the Order runs it. That means the Baron's entire scheme is being staged inside their liturgy, on their island, under their authority — which is why the campaign's climax is desecration and not merely politics.

**And they have been gagged.** Six weeks ago the fouling alarm registered the corruption bleeding off the Baron's hull. The assessment went to the Watercrafters, [[Ivo Calder]] signed it off as a false reading, and the Governor's council carried an instruction standing the alarm down for the duration of the festival. Nerissa knows the reading was right. She has an instruction over the Prince's signature telling her it was not, and temple law telling her she may not act unless the sacred sites are threatened.

She is a powerful priestess, boxed in on two sides, watching the water go wrong and holding a festival anyway. **She is desperate for agents and will not say so in those words.**

## The door

**[[High Priestess Nerissa]]**, and she is actively looking for the party rather than the other way round. She will receive strangers who show any sign of caring about the water, and the temple is public.

**[[Sana]]**, her niece — nineteen, six weeks at the temple, notices everything and says it at the wrong moment — is the cheaper way in and the one who will tell you what the priestesses will not.

[[Lilura Deepcurrent]] is reachable only once the party matters, and reaching her is itself a victory: the Order's head can overrule an instruction the way Nerissa cannot.

## What they can do for you, and the price

| | |
|---|---|
| **Healing** | the best divine healing in the city, freely given to the hurt |
| **Sanctuary** | temple ground, which even the Baron will not violate openly |
| **Water-reading** | Nerissa can sense corruption and say where it comes from -- point her at a hull and she will tell you |
| **Speak with water spirits** | what the Lullwater saw is a question they can ask |
| **Standing** | the Order's public word carries weight the council cannot easily contradict |
| **The Tourney itself** | they can delay a bout, close a ground, or refuse a rite |

**The price is discretion and the sacred.** Bring violence onto temple ground or use them as a weapon in a factional fight and the door shuts.

## Friction

Allied with [[The Watercrafters Guild]] -- which is now *awkward*, since a Watercrafter's signature is what silenced them. On arranged terms with [[Ravella]]. Cautiously allied with the progressive wing of [[The Dredgers]]. Their chief enemy is the Baron's fire demonology, and since his arrival the corruption -- and their alarm -- has steadily risen.

## Hooks
